# Playtest verdicts

This file records what the user said after playing a prototype, and what we
change because of it. It is the highest authority in the project: it outranks
every design document. When a document and this file disagree, this file wins
and the document is corrected.

## Prototype v2 — played 2026-11-16 — verdict: rejected

| # | What the user said | What changes in v3 | Stage | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Movement is heavy and slow | Full speed the moment you press, instant stop, faster run | 1 | in progress |
| 2 | The dash is useless | Dash replaced by a quick backdash | 1 | in progress |
| 3 | The jump is heavy and useless | Hold for a high jump, tap for a short hop, steer in the air | 1 | in progress |
| 4 | I do not like the knockdown at all | Removed; a hit gives a short flinch and a moment of safety | 1 | in progress |
| 5 | No sounds, music or visual effects | Footsteps, jump, land, swing, hit, enemy death, music, hit sparks, dust | 1 | in progress |
| 6 | No animation for movement | Stand-in pixel heroine with idle, run, jump and attack animation | 1 | in progress |
| 7 | The prototype feels dead | Sound, effects, animation, small screen shake, damage numbers | 1 | in progress |
| 8 | The boss looked backward to attack; its animation was just flipping pictures | Boss always faces the player and uses full animation | 3 | planned |
| 9 | Enemies looked picked without thought (a crab?); where are the demons and cyborgs | Demons and machines from our art packs, each with one clear behaviour | 1 (two enemies), 2 (full cast) | in progress |
| 10 | Gameplay depends on counter and timing; I wanted the feel of Castlevania: Order of Ecclesia; counter and slash should be minor extras | Attack always hits when it connects; counter removed for now, returns in stage 3 as an optional bonus | 1, 3 | in progress |
| 11 | No attack animation like Order of Ecclesia | Real sword-swing animation, on the ground and in the air | 1 | in progress |
| 12 | No backdash like Symphony of the Night | Backdash added | 1 | in progress |
| 13 | No platforms to jump around on | Solid platforms and drop-through ledges | 1 | in progress |
| 14 | No doors | A working door in stage 1; connected rooms in stage 2 | 1, 2 | in progress |
| 15 | Nothing felt good | The whole prototype is rebuilt as `prototypes/feel-v3/`, judged by the user after each stage | all | planned |

## Stages
