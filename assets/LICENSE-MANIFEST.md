record that a clean checkout can audit.

`inbox/downloads/organized/audio/` (9), plus the two series entries carried over from

## Promotion rule

Per pack, at promotion time:

- [ ] License text or store-page URL recorded in the `Evidence` column
- [ ] Commercial-use and modification rights confirmed
- [ ] Attribution requirements captured (see `assets/CREDITS.md`)
- [ ] Redistribution clause checked: `Magic Pack 9` and the Metroidvania Kit both
      forbid redistributing their raw files on their own, and `assets/` is a public
      tree, so their raw files are not committed there — they stay in the build
      output (or a private store) until the rights holder resolves the term
- [ ] Only then copy the pack into `assets/`

Two items hold for every row and are recorded once here instead of ticked 35 times:
a human verification with its date is on record (2026-11-16), and a license file or
a provenance stub is staged at `assets/<category>/<pack>/LICENSE`.

Verification is complete for all listed packs, on the basis of the user
authorization above; the license-text capture gate stays open for the seven
user-recalled rows until their download page and license text are captured. Per-pack promotion
(bulk copy into `assets/`) happens on use, at the asset-spec pass — capture the
evidence for a pending row at that moment and replace the stub note before copying.

## Open items

- **Metroidvania Kit (luem)** — the license sheet states CC0 and also forbids
  redistributing the raw files on their own. Only the rights holder can resolve that
  contradiction, and this sweep has no channel to them, so it is recorded rather than
  resolved. Until it is: the pack is used inside the game only, and its raw files stay
  out of the public `assets/` tree (see the promotion gate above).
- **Seven user-recalled rows** — author and source are attributed per the
  user's 2026-11-16 recall (Ansimuz via itch.io) and they are usable with no
  exception under the same authorization; the store page and license text are
  captured at promotion time, and a row's attribution is corrected there if the
  recall proves wrong.

If a license changes or a pack is replaced, add a new row rather than overwriting the
previous verification.

## Staging layout

Only the license is staged, never the pack contents:

```text
assets/art/<pack>/LICENSE        # 24 art packs
assets/audio/<pack>/LICENSE      # 9 audio packs
assets/umbrella/<series>/LICENSE # 2 series entries (pointers to the pack records)
```

The `art`/`audio` segment is required because three pack names exist in both
collections (`cyberpunk-street-files`, `Magic-Cliffs-Gamekit`, `Warped shooting fx
files`), and it matches the `assets/art/**` and `assets/audio/**` layout the asset
tooling already expects. On promotion the pack directory lands at
`assets/<category>/<pack>/` with its `LICENSE` alongside.

## How this was verified

- The store records in `inbox/downloads/organized/docs/` were mined per pack
  (`public-license.pdf`, `license.txt`, `LICENSE.txt` + `README.txt`). PDFs were
  text-extracted, and the extracted text is what sits in each staged `LICENSE`.
- No pack directory in `art/` or `audio/` shipped its own license or readme file, so
  the `docs/` record is the primary evidence for every evidenced row.
- Packs with no `docs/` record and no embedded license were initially listed
  as `pending — author unknown`; they are now attributed per the user's
  2026-11-16 recall (Ansimuz via itch.io) — direct testimony, not inference
  from file names or art style.
- Three licenses are not plain CC0 and are recorded as they read: `Magic Pack 9`
  (purchased-pack terms, no standalone redistribution, plus a CC0 sheet in the same
  download), the six Ansimuz text licenses that grant commercial use, modification and
  redistribution without requiring credit, and the luem Metroidvania Kit, whose CC0
  sheet also carries a `no standalone redistribution` clause. Those two terms
  contradict each other; the pack is used inside the game and its raw files are never
  redistributed, which satisfies both readings. The contradiction is recorded rather
  than resolved, because only the rights holder can resolve it.
