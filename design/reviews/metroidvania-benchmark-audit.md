## 2. Head-on answers

### Where we FIT (at or above the bar, citation-backed)

1. **Low-friction backtracking (B6)** — compact slice, cache two rooms from the boss, non-punitive death with an in-place revive choice (`TR-HD-023/024/025`). The genre's most common complaint is structurally absent at MVP.
2. **Bosses as skill check-ins (B7 combat half)** — the two-channel telegraph law (`TR-ENM-013`), the 250 ms read floor (`V-ENM-01`), one-interaction-rule-per-boss doctrine, the `epoch_pin: 0` pristine-lie arena. The "Read, Then Strike" bar is not just met — it is over-specified and AC-gated.
3. **The anti-mash bar** — `F-CMB-04`'s 5–12-tick age gate + `TR-CMB-013`'s Fresh Press cost + `TR-INP-022`'s weak-domination handoff.
4. **Death as story (Remade Every Death)** — `DeathRecord` cause row, SYSTEM OFFLINE, the serial counter, the single-charge restore choice.
5. **The dual-truth architecture (One World, Two Truths)** — byte-identical geometry as a validated invariant (`TR-RWS-012`), sealed-not-absent partitions (`TR-RWS-009`), one atomic scripted flip + a derived reveal (`AC-RWS-08/09`).
6. **The itch mechanics (B3's core)** — the shaft tease ("honored later" — shape-is-memory), the 3/4 counter carrot (`TR-RWS-015`), the sealed wall that is *visible but unreachable*.
7. **The progression tension's benchmark fix (BT3)** — light stats + skill-based gates is our exact shipped architecture.
8. **The collectibles tension's benchmark fix (BT5)** — fewer, meaningful pickups is our exact doctrine.
9. **The discovery data at MVP** — `cells_discovered` is already a save source family (`TR-RWS-025`): the VS map needs zero extra plumbing.
10. **Verb-combination design (B2 at game scale)** — dash-jump momentum conversion, wall-jump re-arm chains, unhinted tech (`TR-MOV-019/014`, `TR-INP-058`).

### Where we DON'T FIT (deliberate divergences — recorded with reasons, not defects)

- **Linear MVP slice (B1)** — the fold-back at MVP is truth-space (the world turns wrong), not a spatial shortcut loop. Deliberate: the hub + loops arrive with the multi-zone structure.
- **Dash locked at MVP (B2)** — the unlock arc's payoff needs a world worth re-reading; the MVP Definition pins it (**unchanged by the user's decisions** — the D-3 mandate covers the gesture ability, not Dash).
- **No sequence breaking at MVP (BS5)** — deliberate (pivot; TRUTH_BORDER reserved).
- **Softened combat stakes (BS3)** — Read, Then Strike bans reflex walls by design.
- *Formerly on this list, now user-adopted into MVP (2026-11-14): the map (D-4) and the gear layer (D-1) — see § 5a.*

### Where we're BEHIND (honest deltas with owners — full plan in § 4; ★ = elevated to MVP scope by user decisions)

1. **No spatial loop in the slice** (B1) — playtest-gated amendment candidate (IP-1).
2. **Secret rewards are thin at MVP** (B5) — one memory behind the reveal; ★ the gear/relic reward layer enters MVP (D-1, IP-16) with its seed behind the post-flip partition.

5. **The audio/narrative atmosphere layer is unauthored** (GA10/18) — SYS-04's epoch ambient shift; SYS-28's micro-copy budget + out-of-order log states (IP-7/IP-8).
6. **The map tool** (GA6) — ★ MVP-promoted (D-4, IP-14); SYS-25 authoring pulled forward.

## 9. Verdict

The corpus is **architecturally ahead of the benchmark's bar on combat readability, death, and the dual-truth hook** — the three things the game actually bets on — and **deliberately behind on the slice-scale exploration furniture** (loop, map, gear, unlocked Dash). The external audit's five actions are now resolved three ways: **already instrumented** (door pacing, the itch), **adopted by user decision** (the map D-4, MVP gear D-1, the gesture ability D-3, the epistemic layer D-6, the low-stat baseline D-7, accessible stat menus D-2), and **rejected with recorded reasons** (Dash at MVP, tagged-wall relaxation, MVP linearity, lean single-pass GDDs, the reveal-arc trim). The rejected set is unchanged by the user's decisions — every one of them either preserves a pillar (SotN pacing, shape-is-memory, Five Goodbyes order) or protects the review doctrine.

What this audit leaves the studio with: **one expanded MVP Definition to write (IP-19 — the next session's first order of business)**, **two authoring paths pulled forward** (SYS-25, SYS-12, plus SYS-16/17 behind the SYS-07 baseline rework), **one sharpened schedule risk** (IP-12 — nine GDDs in the MVP window), and **one open judgment call** (IP-1's spatial loop — playtest-first, amendment-ready). The epistemic-tells obligation (IP-2) is the audit's best import: it converts the truth-flip from a scripted beat into the metroidbrainia hook the user has now mandated for MVP.
