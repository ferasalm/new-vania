## Setting

- Sci-fi fantasy. **No aliens. No space.** (Removed per designer decision.)
- Future: a demon-controlled world. Demons use **AI to manipulate and possess humans**.
- Past: an age where **magic existed**. The demon lord sends the protagonist to time-travel there to **retrieve magic essence** and bring magical powers to the future.
- **Parallel universes / spacetime glitches.** Environments that **warp, flip, or distort** — familiar places made eerie.
- Shocking contrast shifts: the **same place** suddenly glitches into a parallel, distorted version — for example, **a cozy library that glitches into a mass grave**.

## Protagonist

- An **AI android** with an **anti-hero vibe**.
- She **reflects a real human** — she was created from / trained on that woman.
- Currently **serves the demon lord**.
- A **preserved brain** — the real woman's — was used to train her; it exists in the base timeline (the future).
- In one of the past timelines, the protagonist **finds the human she's based on**.

## Core Story (as brainstormed)

- The demon lord sends the android to time-travel to the past to retrieve magic essence.
- She finds the human she's based on in one of the past timelines.
- The human is **unstable, like a mad scientist**, because she **hasn't processed grieving her father**, who was killed **accidentally because of her** during a physics experiment.
- She works diligently to finish creating the **time machine** so she can go to the past and **prevent her father from dying**.
- But the paradox she is trying to achieve (save her dead father — her motivation to create the time machine in the first place) **keeps preventing her**: it causes a **spacetime glitch** that warps her to a **parallel universe**.
- **The demon lord was the one responsible for killing her father.** He needs the time machine to go back in time and retrieve true magical powers — and he must keep her father dead, or kill her in any universe where she is not destined to create the optimized time machine or process her father's death (like the universe where she refuses to fight you).
- As the android trained on her brain, you **retrieve memories and make sense of them**.
- Final realization: **nobody can save her father unless you defeat the demon lord** — and then she wouldn't invent the time machine, and **you, the android, would cease to exist**.

## Self-as-Boss — Signature Structure

- The protagonist faces **herself as a boss multiple times** in parallel universes.
- In some timelines/universes, she is **hostile**.
- In **one other universe, she refuses to fight** — when you expect her to — **lowers her weapon and asks to come with you**. She **joins for a while** before the demon lord **kills her mid-conversation**.

## Environmental Storytelling Ideas

- In some alternate universe (inverted / upside-down / distorted environment), you find **messages from her father**: *"choose the one where you live"* / *"choose the one where you don't lose yourself."*
- **Experiment logs** that look humanly written by her: *"I am getting close to saving you, even if I have to die, I will fix my mistake."*
- Sudden shifts into a parallel, alternative, distorted universe of the same environment, causing **severe and shocking contrast** (cozy library → mass grave).

## Themes

- **Guilt** — the father's death, her fault (and not her fault).
- **Identity** — android vs. the human she's copied from.
- **Moral dilemma** — defeating the demon lord erases the android's own existence.
- **Grief (unprocessed)** — the engine of her instability and the plot.
