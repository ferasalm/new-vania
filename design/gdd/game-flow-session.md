## Overview

Game Flow & Session Structure is the game's **coordination authority**: the one machine that owns which context the game is in, the one flow that owns where a session begins and ends, and the one gate that decides when a loaded world becomes playable. Every other system raises **requests** to it; it alone **transitions**. It owns the context state machine (nine contexts — the seven SYS-01 ratified plus Loading and the TRANSITION fallback), the session loop's structure (the MVP's linear stub; the VS/full loop's named gates), the save/load flow's session half (the boot path, the loading context, the load-complete gate, the refusal flow), the death/revive flow's session half (the offline screen's flow shape and the three paths' routing), the Reading context's flow law, and the quit/teardown flow.

**What it does not own.** The gate *enforcement* (SYS-01's — input holds the verbs, clears the buffers, and defers to this machine's state); the save mechanism, the envelope and the refusal codes (SYS-03's — the flow consumes `load_result`/`restore_complete`/`save_completed` and owns only what the player may do next); the flip and its choreography (SYS-09/10's — the flow supplies the GAMEPLAY context gate and nothing else); the room data and the transitions' mechanics (SYS-02's — the swap is not this machine's concern beyond the law that it raises no context change); the death's resolution and the offline screen's fiction (SYS-07's contract, SYS-15's presentation); the Reading's text (SYS-28's); the pause menu's rendering (SYS-24's — the flow owns the rows' semantics, not their pixels).

**The one-line contract.** *The player is always somewhere in the world, and the world never announces the machinery.* Exactly one context is active at a time; every transition is atomic (no verb resolves on a transition frame, no half-states exist); every gate request is queued, prioritized, and resolved exactly once; a load completes only when every named input has arrived — and until then the world simply holds its breath. The flow adds no verb, renders no pixel, prints no word of its own.

**MVP posture (the approved decisions, D-ORCH-16).** The session structure is the **linear stub**: one zone, one run, the hub door `LOCKED` (SYS-02's Class B refusal is the boundary — no flow state admits the hub), no completion gate (the Denial repeats; the run ends only on quit). The VS/full loop's six named gates are authored as a table with owners and copy owners; their copy rows are **registered-but-absent** (SYS-28's pattern) — a present row without the flow is a boot lint failure. The **Loading context** is the eighth context (a full gate, never player-requested). The **load-complete gate** is a conjunction of named inputs: SYS-03's `restore_complete`, SYS-10's reconcile-settled, and the carry-complete acknowledgments of every registered namespace owner (SYS-15's and SYS-16's at MVP) — its payload carries `restored_epoch` (O-AUD-1). The **Stinger grant beat** fires at the first cache activation's context exit (D-3's story moment). The pause is not a freeze; the freeze is not a gate; no new input verb exists.

→ *Implementation patterns (the `ContextCore` node shape, the queue's drain order, the platform lifecycle wiring) are ADR raw material for the planned **Session & Flow ADR**; this GDD stays at the behavior level.*

## Player Fantasy

### The named fantasy — Always in the World

*The game never throws you out.* From the first frame to the last, you are somewhere in her world: the pause is the world holding its breath while you decide, not a menu that replaced it; the death is the fiction's own screen, not a system's; the reconstruct turns the world over like a page and you are standing somewhere else before you noticed a seam; the logs open onto the house's paperwork without the world ceasing to exist behind them. Every mode the machine has, the fiction already named: reading is reading, the terminal is the terminal, death is System Offline, the pause is a held breath. The machinery underneath is exact — one context at a time, every gate honest, every load complete before a step is taken — and the player's reward for that exactness is that they never see it.

### The contract — four terms, each testable

1. **No seam is ever announced.** No mode popup, no "LOADING", no context label, no transition splash reaches the player; every transition's visible face is a surface another system already owns (a print row, the System Offline rows, the held world frame). *Test: the machinery-leak lint (the language law's machinery-word ban; AC-GFS-22) + the playtest code (AC-GFS-27).*
2. **Every gate is honest.** A gate stops the world exactly at its gate-entry phase — never mid-arc, never a half-frame; pending orders die at the door (the buffers clear), held states persist; the transition frame resolves nothing. *Test: the atomicity fixtures (AC-GFS-11; V-GFS-02).*
3. **The loop is one world.** The session never restarts to change a mode: boot, death, and quit are the only edges that begin or end a world, and each does exactly what its fiction says — a reboot re-prints her where the record says she was; a quit keeps the record and ends the shift; the world you return to is the world the record holds. *Test: the boot/reconstruct/quit fixtures (AC-GFS-12/16/18).*
4. **Deliberation is always available.** The pause is reachable from any playable tick, never times out, never punishes thinking, and resumes exactly where the breath was held. *Test: the pause fixtures (AC-GFS-08/26; TR-INP-047's no-auto-timeout consumed).*

### Anchor moments

1. **The Quiet Pause.** Mid-fight, she presses the corner. The world stops — not a freeze-frame artifact but a held breath; the rows wait; nothing ticks. She reads the map's grey cells, resumes, and the Spitter's windup is exactly where she left it. The pause never cost her a frame of the fight she was reading.
2. **The Turn of the Page.** She dies to the pit, deep and far from the Sanctum. SYSTEM OFFLINE; the fault row names the world. She chooses RECONSTRUCT. The world turns over — one held frame — and she is standing at the terminal, the designation one higher, the corridor she died in already behind her. She never saw a loading screen; the world simply was elsewhere when she looked again.
3. **The Gift.** The terminal's print settles. She takes LEAVE — and in the room's quiet, one new line prints where no machine printed before: the lord's grant, a gesture with no button. Nothing explains it. She tries the shape, and the body answers. (The grant beat — D-3's story moment; the cue is SYS-16's, the words are the print voice's, the trigger is this machine's.)
4. **The Door That Says No.** She walks the Vestibule's east wall to the hub door. `LOCKED`, dimmed, patient. She presses it; the house answers with the reason and nothing else. The run continues west. The errands are not this build's story — the door says so, every time, the same way.

### Language law

**Use:** world, breath, held, page, turn, door, record, reboot, the run, the shift, the house, context (GDD prose), gate (GDD prose).
**Player-facing copy — banned:** *loading* (the word never reaches the player — the held frame is not a loading screen), *load screen*, *mode*, *respawn point*, *checkpoint*, *continue?*, *game over*, *menu game*. **GDD prose — permitted:** context, gate, phase, slot (SYS-03's nouns), namespace (SYS-03's). **Telemetry/field names — unconstrained.** Falsifiability rule: every feel-sentence is verifiable by playtest rubric or build check; if verifying it would need a stopwatch on a transition, cut it.

### Success signature

Playtesters say: *"I was back at the terminal before I even noticed a pause"*, *"the game just… stopped when I paused — like it was waiting for me"*, *"I never saw it load — it was just there"*, *"when I died it told me what got me and I was walking again"*. **Anti-signatures** (the failure modes this GDD exists to prevent): *"there was a loading screen"* (the machinery leaked); *"the game ate my input when the log opened"* (a gate stole an order instead of clearing it honestly); *"I paused and the fight kept going"* (the pause is not a freeze — if the world moved, the gate lied); *"I got stuck after dying"* (a flow path stranded the player); *"so pausing is a menu and loading is a screen?"* (the fiction failed to name its own machinery).

*Pillar ties: "SotN, Not Hollow Knight" (the pause's deliberation, the cache sanctuary's flow, the loop's bookends at VS) · "Remade Every Death" (the death arc's flow is the pillar's spine — SYS-05 owns the arc, SYS-07 the record, SYS-15 the telling) · "One World, Two Truths" (the flip fires only in GAMEPLAY; the load path's re-fire is the same logical flip — the flow's gates make both true) · "Read, Then Strike" (the Reading is a full gate — the read is protected, never rushed).*

## Detailed Design

### Core Rules

**Rule IDs (`TR-GFS-*`) are stable requirement proposals — each carries a falsifiable test.** PROVISIONAL marks indicate contracts whose owning GDD or surface is not yet authored (SYS-24, SYS-25 queued; SYS-28's copy at the VS pass; **SYS-12's ability consumes TR-GFS-011's trigger — the registration arrives with its authoring**; the marked instances: **TR-GFS-023's MAP and LOADOUT rows — marked inline; TR-GFS-016's surface PROVISIONAL (SYS-24 queued) — the surface is SYS-24's render; TR-GFS-010's copy rows PROVISIONAL (SYS-28's VS pass) — the copy lands at the VS gate**) — the requirements are final, the surfaces are theirs. **SYS-05 reads no `Input` (the gates are SYS-01's enforcement), renders no pixel, writes no save byte, and resolves no hit.**

#### 1 · The context state machine

- **TR-GFS-001 — Own the machine; the core is pure.** `ContextCore` is a pure `RefCounted` over injected ports: `GateRequestPort` (in: every requester's gate request — the raise-and-queue seam), `RecordPort` (in: SYS-03's `load_result` / `restore_complete(slot, tick)` / `save_completed` records; SYS-15's activation edge; SYS-16's carry-complete acknowledgment), **`StoreReadPort`** (read: the restored store's `truth_epoch` at the swap — SYS-03's restored store, read once after `restore_complete`; **the `restored_epoch` payload's input path** — an unreadable store read is the stall path's named input, the dev assert names it), `ClockPort` (read: the tick — the machine transitions at gate-entry phases, consumed from the ADR's pipeline), `FlowPort` (out: the context transitions, `load_complete(slot, tick, restored_epoch)`, the spawn-anchor decision, the grant beat's trigger edge — **SYS-16 stays the `GrantEvent`'s sole publisher**, the flow emits only the trigger), `TelemetryPort` (write), `TuningPort` (read). **No `Input`, no `SceneTree`, no `Time`, no `RenderingServer` inside the core** (ADR-0001 §15's seam pattern, applied after `InputCore`/`WorldCore`/`RealityCore`/`CameraCore`). - **TR-GFS-005 — The gate priority and the no-double-gate law (F-GFS-01).** At each gate-entry phase the machine applies **at most one** queued request — the highest-priority **eligible** request. **Priority:** P0 forced (the blur, per TR-GFS-008's ruling) > P1 Death/respawn > P2 TRANSITION/Cutscene > P3 ack-born (Reading, Cache-terminal — arrival order within the class) > P4 reflex (Paused). **Eligibility:** P1 is always eligible; P2/P3/P4 are eligible iff the active context is Gameplay; P0's eligibility is the blur's per-context ruling (TR-GFS-008), not the queue's law. **The composition law:** a P1 win while a full-gate context is open (Reading, Cache-terminal, Paused, Menu-focus, Cutscene) composes **close-then-open into one atomic transition** — the open context closes to Gameplay and Death/respawn opens, with **no Gameplay logic tick between** (the gate-entry phase is atomic; the buffers cleared at the close stay cleared). **The queue laws:** (a) a P1 or P2 application **voids the entire pending queue** (nothing stale survives a death or a scripted beat); (b) a P3 loser **stays queued** (custody) and applies at the first phase where the active context is Gameplay again — the SYS-02 terminal-behind-Reading edge is this law's recorded instance; (c) a P4 loser **voids** (the pause is re-pressable at will — a deferred pause popping after a Reading would be a stale order); (d) the **forward-binding** (consumed, B4): a gate request raised earlier in a tick blocks every later ack commit in that tick — the same-tick pause+READ race resolves by the ack never committing, so no Reading request exists to queue. *Test: the priority fixture (two queued requests in one phase → one applies; AC-GFS-04); the composition fixture (AC-GFS-05); the custody fixture (the terminal request applies after the Reading's close); the void fixture (the pause loses to the terminal's open and does not pop later); the forward-binding race fixture.*

## Formulas

Three laws govern SYS-05's formulas: **the ordering law** (the machine's decisions are phase-ordered — a request's fate is a pure function of the queue, the priority, and the active context), **the conjunction law** (the load-complete gate is a conjunction of named inputs — no input may be guessed, defaulted, or skipped), and **the derivation law** (the spawn anchor derives from the composed record — never stored, never guessed). SYS-05 converts **no ms→ticks of its own**: the gate-entry phases are the ADR's pipeline's; the hold values are their owners' (`OFFLINE_HOLD_MS` is SYS-15's; `TRANSITION_BUDGET_MS` is SYS-02's); the machine's one ms constant is a dev-only diagnostic.

#### F-GFS-01 · gate_priority — the queued-request resolution (TR-GFS-005)

The `gate_priority` formula is defined as:

`gate_priority(t) = first r ∈ Q(t) in priority order where eligible(r, active(t))`; one application per gate-entry phase.

**Variables:**

| Variable | Symbol | Type | Range | Description |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| The pending queue | `Q(t)` | list[GateRequest] | ≤ 8 entries (**an arbitrary dev bound above the seven registered queued sources** — death, TRANSITION, Cutscene, READ, CACHE, pause, blur; the assert names it) | The non-ack requests raised since the last application, arrival order |
| A request | `r` | GateRequest | `{source, class, payload}` | One row of TR-GFS-004's table |
| The class priority | `P(r)` | enum | {P0 forced (the blur), P1 death, P2 transition/cutscene, P3 ack-born, P4 reflex} | The fixed order; arrival order breaks ties within P3 |
| The active context | `active(t)` | enum | the nine contexts | The machine's current state |
| Eligibility | `eligible` | bool | {true, false} | P0: the blur's per-context ruling (TR-GFS-008) · P1: always · P2/P3/P4: `active(t) = GAMEPLAY` |
| The application | `gate_priority(t)` | GateRequest \| none | — | The request whose transition runs this phase (or none — the phase passes) |

**Output Range:** exactly one applied request per phase, or none. **The fate laws:** a P1/P2 application voids `Q(t)` entirely (the composition law covers the close of an open full gate); a P0 application never voids the queue (the blur's replacement is its own effect — the signed orders keep custody); a P3 loser stays queued (custody — it applies at the first later phase where `active = GAMEPLAY`); a P4 loser voids. **Examples:** (1) the terminal's request queued at tick T; a death edge in the same tick → at T+1's phase the death applies (the composition if the terminal had opened), the queue voids — no stale terminal request survives the respawn. (2) The Reading open; the terminal's queued request (the RM-04 edge) → it stays queued; the Reading's close exits to Gameplay; the next phase applies the terminal's request — SYS-02's recorded outcome. (3) The pause press and a `READ` ack in one tick → the pause applies at the tick's GATE-ENTRY phase (the ADR pipeline's phase 3 — the mapping: captures → gate-entry → offers → acks → expiry); the ack commit is **blocked by the forward-binding** (the request raised earlier in the tick) — no Reading request ever queues; the Interact intent expires at the gate entry. (4) The pause queued behind the terminal's ack-born request → the terminal applies (P3 > P4); the pause voids. (5) The app blurs during a Reading → P0 applies (the forced pause replaces the gate — Paused; the queued terminal request keeps custody and applies after the resume's Gameplay return). (6) The app blurs during Death/respawn → absorbed (the screen waits — TR-GFS-008(e)); during Loading → absorbed with the pending-restore flag (the `load_complete` exit lands in Paused).

## Edge Cases

### The gates and the queue

- **If two ack-born requests queue in one phase** (the terminal's and a log's) → the earlier arrival applies; the later retains custody and applies at the first Gameplay phase after the first closes (SYS-02's recorded edge). [F-GFS-01; one transition per phase.]
- **If a death edge fires while a Reading is open** → the composition: the Reading closes (the close is not the completion edge in this path — the player never read to the end; no flag sets), Death/respawn opens — one atomic transition, no Gameplay tick between. [TR-GFS-005; the L2 flag sets only on the player's close — a death-interrupted read is an interrupted read, and the threshold is not crossed by dying.]
- **If the death edge fires while Paused or Menu-focus is open** → no ordinary pipeline can fire it (no world ticks run in a full gate); **the scripted-ENVIRONMENT vector is the recorded exception** (TR-GFS-007(d)'s full-gate class) — the composition covers it: Paused/Menu-focus close, Death/respawn opens. [TR-GFS-007; defence in depth.]
- **If the pause press and a `READ` ack land in one tick** → the pause applies at phase 3; the ack commit is blocked by the forward-binding; the Interact intent expires at the gate entry. No Reading request exists. [F-GFS-01; B4 consumed.]
- **If a second gate request arrives while one is mid-application** → queued for the next phase (one transition per phase); never interleaved, never merged. [TR-GFS-005.]
- **If the queue overflows the registered bound (8)** → the dev assert + telemetry; the oldest reflex-class request drops first, then the oldest ack-born (custody is honored as long as possible). Unreachable in practice — the sources are finite and drains are per-tick. [TR-GFS-005.]

### The load and the boot

- **If the boot probe returns `REFUSE_ABSENT`** → the new-game path: no Loading entry; the fresh world at the boot anchor; the designation at 0. [TR-GFS-014; the first boot is not an error.]
- **If the boot probe returns a refusal code** (corrupt, schema-ahead, unreadable) → the refusal flow: the two actions; the slot read-only for the session; the offline screen (if the reconstruct raised it) has yielded already. **The refused-boot loop's playtest note (the QA plan's refusal script carries it): after a boot refusal, every death's reconstruct re-refuses (`auto` read-only) and routes to NEW GAME; the RE-CACHE's `manual_1` cannot serve the reconstruct at MVP — the facilitator's explanation is the fiction's own: the machine cannot prove what it remembers, and the run re-prints from nothing.** [TR-GFS-016.]
- **If `auto` is refused but `manual_1` is loadable** → the refusal flow still runs (boot reads `auto` only); the manual-load affordance is VS scope (OQ-GFS-1's neighbor) — the recorded gap, never a silent fallback to `manual_1`. [TR-GFS-014.]
- **If a save request arrives during Loading** → refused at the request (SYS-03's gate and the machine's assertion both fire — one telemetry record, no snapshot). [TR-GFS-013.]
- **If the close-save's moment falls inside the flip window** → the close-save is skipped (SYS-03's landed law); the last station autosave is the fallback. [TR-GFS-020; TR-SAV-014 consumed.]
- **If the app's close/suspension event arrives while the offline screen is open** (a death unresolved) → the close-save is skipped (the anti-scum extension — the death's rollback must stay honest); the last station autosave is the fallback. [TR-GFS-020; the recorded SYS-03 rider.]
- **If a named load-complete input never arrives** → Loading persists; the dev stall record + the assert name the missing input; production waits (no timeout, no player surface). [TR-GFS-015.]
- **If the app's close/suspension event arrives inside the restore window** (a teardown during Loading) → the close-save is refused at the request (SYS-03's loading-context rejection — TR-SAV-013's gate); the load completes first, the exit lands per the pending-restore flag if a blur held one, then the teardown runs its own close-save decision. [TR-GFS-013/020; the order is the gate's.]
- **If the deferral-gap save restores** (epoch 0, flag set) → the request publishes before the gate; the first post-load GAMEPLAY tick re-fires the flip — the same logical flip completing; the flow's role is the context gate only. [TR-GFS-015; SYS-10's chain consumed.]
- **If a post-flip save restores** (epoch 1) → no request; the payload's `restored_epoch = 1` (the identity assert); SYS-04's bed map applies it at the load-complete tick. [TR-GFS-015; O-AUD-1.]

### The death arc

- **If the player dies before the first cache activation** → the offline screen's RECONSTRUCT resolves the pre-cache fallback: **the flow's new-game initialization resets the world truth to the defaults** (SYS-15's TR-CSD-014 consumed — the fallback is the initialization, not a continuation); the Loading context never enters; the namespace's live state carries back in the fallback's own carry step (before readability — the count accumulates, never resets); the increment on the first logic tick after the carry step (the stamp reads 001); the body at the boot anchor. [TR-GFS-018; SYS-15's fallback consumed.]
- **If the reconstruct's load is refused** → the refusal flow owns the session; the offline screen does not return; the death is not undone (the world is whatever the last good save holds). [TR-GFS-016.]
- **If the reconstruct's load is refused and the player then picks QUIT in the refusal flow** → the teardown with the close-save **skipped** (the death is unresolved — chosen-but-failed: the increment never applied, the rollback never ran; the anti-scum law's refused-reconstruct arm); the last station autosave is the fallback. [TR-GFS-020.]
- **If the player chooses QUIT on the offline screen** → the teardown with the close-save skipped (the anti-scum law); the death resolves as never-chosen (no increment); the duck releases at the exit. [TR-GFS-020.]
- **If the death edge fires during the Cache-terminal context** → the composition closes the terminal first (the in-flight save's snapshot predates the death — it completes and lands as the ack-time state), then Death/respawn opens; one atomic transition. [TR-GFS-005; SYS-15's law 4.]
- **If the module revive fires on a void death** → the placement is SYS-15's resolver (`last_grounded_safe_pos`); the flow's role is unchanged (the revive's same-application exit → Gameplay). [TR-GFS-019; SYS-15's TR-CSD-018 consumed.]

### The transitions and the pause

- **If the player crosses a border while a context request is queued** → the swap is context-blind (it raises nothing); the queued request applies at its phase regardless of the room — the Reading/terminal surfaces are world-anchored (SYS-24's), the flow never cancels a signed order for changing rooms. [TR-GFS-022; the custody law.]
- **If a first-entry load overflows the budget** (never at MVP) → the TRANSITION context enters; the load completes inside; the exit is atomic. A hard failure → the body returns to the origin's exit anchor (the crossing undone); Gameplay resumes. [TR-GFS-022.]
- **If the pause opens mid-attack-commitment** → the arc dies at the gate entry (TR-MOV-029 consumed); the input's part was done at the pull; the commitment's remainder dies with the arc — no half-signed order survives. [TR-GFS-023; SYS-01's consumed law.]
- **If the resume lands while a freeze source is somehow active** (a defect: a freeze raised in a full gate) → the dev assert (the discrimination law's violation is a defect path, never a state); the context resumes Gameplay with the freeze's source audited by telemetry. [TR-GFS-007.]
- **If the physics rate changes mid-session** → no flow constant is rate-derived (the machine has no tick-math of its own; the phases are the ADR's pipeline's) — nothing re-baselines. [The formula ledger's rate note.]

### Degenerate strategies — the register lives at TR-GFS-024

- **If the player mashes pause during a Reading** → the pause request voids on the preemption (the Reading holds); after the close the pause is one press away — nothing to farm, nothing stale pops. [F-GFS-01.]
- **If the player tries to reopen the hub door repeatedly** → SYS-02's refusal (one telemetry record per press); the flow adds nothing — the door's answer is the boundary's whole voice. [TR-GFS-009.]
