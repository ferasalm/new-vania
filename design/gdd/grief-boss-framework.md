## Overview

The Grief-Boss Framework is the shape of the game's goodbyes: **one rig, five grief states** — the recurring boss is the woman Pandora is copied from, fought once per errand, each fight a different stage of her grief wearing the same body. The framework owns the rig's data model (the five-state frame), the **Denial state at MVP** (the authored fight), the boss's own values (her pool, her phase floors, her scripted death — SYS-07's hooks, SYS-14's numbers), the **arena contract** (the held stage, the sealed basin, the pristine lie), the **knowledge route** (the weak point the environment teaches, so the grind is never the answer), and the **death/defeat contract** (her scripted goodbye and the player's offline handshake inside it). It consumes SYS-13's enemy machinery whole — the boss registers as an archetype; her strikes resolve through Combat's exchange like any enemy's — and adds exactly three things the machinery does not have: the grief-state layer, the arena contract, and the scripted death.

**What it does not own.** SYS-13 owns the enemy machinery (the archetype table, the strike contract, the telegraph doctrine, the decision scheduler, the state machine, the freeze integration) — the boss is registered data on it, never new code-shaped behavior outside the grief layer. SYS-08 owns the exchange (the clash matrix, the counter, the lose branch, the deflect) — the boss's strikes participate, never amend. SYS-07 owns the pool, the pipeline, the death edge and the profile hooks — SYS-14 authors the values, never the mechanism. SYS-32 owns the camera (the hold law, the staging band's derivation, the validator) — SYS-14 publishes the level and keeps the band. SYS-02 owns the room data (the bounds, the anchor, the framing zone, the borders) — the boss behavior is never room data. SYS-09 owns the render of the pristine lie. SYS-15 owns the offline screen; SYS-24 renders the boss bar and the cues; SYS-29/SYS-04 design against the published edges. SYS-14 never reads input, never resolves an exchange, never subtracts HP, never raises a freeze, and never reads `truth_epoch`.

## Player Fantasy

### The named fantasy — The Lie Won't Look At You

Her world is perfect and she is in it. The room behind you changed and she never noticed; the steel is bright, the symmetry unbroken, and she stands with her hands clasped, ring raised, and she does not see you. Every strike she throws is shown — the wide flat sweep you cannot meet, the tall slow lift you can, the short double-hand thrust that punishes the wrong answer. Your best press — the early chain, the counter — lands and sparks and numbers, and **she does not fall**: the bar holds at a third, and that is not a bug, it is *her*. The lie holds because she holds it. Three times you step out of her wide swing and let it pass through empty air — and the halo cracks, a little more each time, the one honest line in a room that has none. The third unanswered swing and the lie breaks: the stillness takes her, the fight goes quiet and real for two seconds, and when it resumes the bar finally moves — **now she can fall**. The fight was never about her health; it was about whether you would keep answering, or leave her alone with what she believes. And when she falls at last, the ring drops, and she looks at you — the goodbye's own moment, held to the end.

### The contract — five terms, each testable

1. **The telegraph is the promise (inherited).** Every boss strike displays color-flash + shape lean for at least a breath before its active window; the sweep's lean is the denied family — wide, flat, unmeetable — and the read says *step out*. Nothing lands unshown; the doctrine is SYS-13's and the boss inherits it whole.
2. **The lie is legible, never a glitch.** While the lie holds she cannot fall: damage lands, numbers print, and the bar stalls at a third. The stall and the hairlines tell you the truth before any text could: damage alone will not finish this. The bar's stall must never read as breakage — the pristine lie has no tolerance for "buggy" (the anti-code fails outright).
3. **The crack is earned by the read, not the DPS.** Three unanswered sweeps — her sign, left alone in the air she struck — crack the lie. The counter and the chains are your damage; the *step-out* is your key. The route is learnable on first sight: each unanswered swing visibly cracks the halo, and the scuff on the floor has been telling you where she stands since you arrived.
4. **The stage holds.** The arena is a sealed basin and a fixed frame: the camera takes the stage once and never scrolls; every participant and every threat stays inside the staging band; the way back is a wall she authored, not a door the game closed. The duel reads as staged choreography, never survived chaos.
5. **The goodbye is a beat, not a bar.** When the lie is broken and the pool is spent, she does not despawn: the ring falls, the crack spreads, she looks — the recognition beat — and then she goes. The death is held on her body, witnessed, without spectacle. The counter that remade you never remade her; this is the one death in the zone that is only ever hers.

### Anchor moment — The First Sight

The door admits you and the floor is gone — the basin takes you, the way back a sheer wall above. The camera settles on the stage, and there she is: hands clasped, ring raised, standing at the one dulled patch on an immaculate floor. She looks through you. Her world never changed. The wide sweep comes — you already know what the lean means — and the fight begins with the room holding its breath around you both.

### Anchor moment — The Unanswered Swing

You have met her tall lift and been paid for it — sparks, the lock, her stillness, your chain. But the bar will not cross a third. So you read the wide one instead: you flick back, the sweep passes through the space you left, and the halo above her cracks — one clean hairline, no glow, no sound of reward, just the room's first lie showing. The second one widens it. You are learning a route no log printed and no merchant sells: leave her alone with her own swing.

### Anchor moment — The Lie Breaks

The third sweep finds nothing and the halo's break runs its full circle as the ring passes — the lie is gone, and the stillness takes her: two seconds that are yours, and you spend them. When she moves again the cadence is tighter and the bar moves with it, past the third it guarded all fight. At the end she does not fall like the machines do: the ring falls first, the crack spreads, and she looks — sees you, the first time — and then she is gone. The scuff stays. The room stays perfect. You are the one who changed.

### Language law

**Use:** read, telegraph, shown, signed, carried, finished, the lie, the crack, the stage, the basin, the goodbye, honest, answerable, breath (for timing windows).
**Ban:** crisp, snappy, instant, frame-perfect, twitch, reflex, reaction speed, "just dodge", artificial, cheap — and, for this system specifically: the bar's stall is never described to the player as "stuck", "buggy", "broken" or "glitched" in any shipped copy (the pristine lie has no tolerance for reading as breakage — the F3 anti-code rule).

The boss's timing windows are "a breath" in player-facing copy; no millisecond talk reaches the player. Telegraph doctrine is color-flash + shape lean; opacity pulses are banned language and banned craft. Falsifiability rule: every feel-sentence must be verifiable by playtest or build check; if verifying it would need a stopwatch on reflexes, cut it.

### Success signature

Playtesters say: "her bar stopped — I had to make her miss"; "the wide one, you just step out — always step out"; "when I dodged it the halo cracked, that's the tell"; "I saw the crack get bigger"; "at the end she looked at me"; nobody reads the stall as a bug, and nobody describes her death as a health bar emptying. **Anti-signatures** (the failure modes this GDD exists to prevent): "her bar glitched/stuck/broke" (the floor misread as breakage — the named deviation with zero tolerance); "I just kept hitting her and nothing happened" (the grind route never taught the route); "she went down like every other enemy" (the goodbye missing — the recognition beat must land); "the camera moved during the fight" (the stage broke); "something hit me I didn't see" (a strike escaped the shared language or the band).

*Pillar ties: "Five Goodbyes" (the Denial fight is the first goodbye — the lie's) · "Read, Then Strike" (the whiff is the read's reward; the counter is the clash's) · "SotN, Not Hollow Knight" (the stage holds; the duel is choreography; no chase, no panic) · "One World, Two Truths" (the pristine lie stays framed — the most confident room in the zone) · "Remade Every Death" (her death is a goodbye, held on her body — the one death that is only ever hers).*

## Edge Cases

**Format: If [condition] → [exact outcome]. [rationale where non-obvious.]**

### Freeze and gates

- **If a freeze begins mid-telegraph, mid-active or mid-recovery** → the body's timer holds (SYS-13's law); the flash holds mid-flash; on unfreeze the arc completes with its remaining duration; the whiff window's boundary math holds in ticks (the derivation is freeze-invariant). [TR-ENM-021 consumed; TR-GBF-014.]
- **If a freeze begins mid-crack-beat** → the beat's timer holds; the floor release already landed (it is a state write, not a timer); the punish window's reward is freeze-invariant. [TR-GBF-013.]
- **If a freeze begins mid-death-script** → the script's beat timers hold; the recognition's remainder resumes with its remaining duration; the camera's death-hold is unaffected (the stage point never moves). [TR-GBF-021.]
- **If a gate opens mid-fight** → the body machine dies (any state); the grief layer's timers hold with the context; no damage on the transition frame; on resume the body is IDLE with nothing queued and the layer resumes its state (the phase, the counter, the beat's remainder). The telegraph that was shown may not land — the context broke, not the read. [TR-ENM-016's gate law + the layer's persistence.]
- **If the context is non-GAMEPLAY (Reading, pause, the offline screen)** → `logic_running` false; every boss timer holds; the raw publications continue; the layer's state is exactly preserved for the resume. [ADR §3; TR-GBF-022.]

### The exchange

- **If the player's meeting press meets the ring sweep** → the no-clash branch (the flag + the geometry): the sweep's own damage (2) and knockdown resolve; never the lose branch, never the double. [TR-CMB-009; TR-GBF-007.]
