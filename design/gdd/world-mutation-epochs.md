## Overview

World Mutation & Epochs is the **mutation half of the truth contract**: SYS-02 stores the world's word, SYS-09 renders it, and SYS-10 owns the machinery by which the word **changes** — the mutation state, the epoch's write, and the ledger of what each change means. It consumes SYS-02's dual-truth data model as its substrate (`truth_epoch` + the registered truth flags; the mutation registry's shape — `Mutation {set_id, room_id, from_epoch, to_epoch, effects}` — **exactly ONE mechanical set at MVP**: the Cistern reveal set `REVEAL_CISTERN`, V-RWS-07's validated count) and owns: the **story-threshold trigger surface** (the `sanctum_log_read` flag-set edge on the registered truth table — SYS-02 publishes the edge, SYS-10 acts on it), the **flip request** to SYS-09's sequencer, the **epoch write** (ONE atomic store at τ0 on SYS-09's `flip_epoch_boundary` signal — never before `freeze_open`; ADR-0001 §3's freeze law and §5's tick-boundary pipeline consumed; **compute-then-commit** — the transaction computes the derivations before the store commits, AC-DR-05's order), the **derived-open application policy** (a mutation's effects are checked against the current epoch, never stored — `gate_open(m, epoch) = epoch ≥ m.to_epoch` is SYS-02's law consumed verbatim; SYS-10 validates the registry that makes the derivation possible), the **pending-flip condition's persistence through save/load** (the flag/epoch reconciliation for restored saves — OQ-DR-2's answer lives here, published contract-first for SYS-03), and the tells' **semantic linkage + the telegraph-text surface** (user decision D-6's co-ownership: every `STATE_DELTA` ref names a SYS-10-applied delta that actually exists; the threshold log may dramatize the truth in the house's own voice but never inventories the tells — TR-DR-010's SYS-10 half). The player-felt consequence is the whole point, and it is not a power: the world's truth changes **everywhere at once, never on command** (no shift verb exists — the epoch mutates only through the story threshold and the backtrack reveal), and **never changes back** — the flip is a one-way story event, and every room you return to is *wrong now* because the state behind it moved once, atomically, and stayed. It never touches tile data (SYS-09's no-geometry law — SYS-10's abstention), never writes tell state (tells are stateless), and never applies an epoch outside the one τ0 boundary.

## Player Fantasy

### The named fantasy — The World After

*"The first world is gone. Not hidden — gone. What stands now is simply the world."* The flip was not a feature and not a mode; it was an **event in the world's history** — one change, at one boundary, that outlives your deaths, your saves, and every session after it. Nothing re-assures you afterwards ("still true!" is a sentence this game never speaks); the world did not wait for you and did not un-change — it went on being what it is. And the world's truth **answers the telling, not the walker**: it changed when the story was read, not when you reached something; no amount of reaching could call it early, and nothing calls it twice. The second truth was never added — it was already *underneath* — but the disclosure happened once, and after it the game is a world with a past that only you carry.

### Anchor moments

- **The Return.** You die after the Cistern reveal; the reconstruct wakes you — and the passage stands open, the wing stands there, nothing re-checked, no courtesy reset. The death took your position, your health, your momentum; it did not take the truth. Then you quit and come back tomorrow, and the world did not wait for you and did not un-change. The promise is felt as an *absence*: there is nothing to re-verify, because there is nothing that could have reverted.
- **The Settled Tell.** On a later pass through RM-03, the capped conduit you once stared at is a candle bracket now — and you walk past it. No ping, no credit, no ceremony. The tell has had an afterlife: it stopped being a hint and became a fact. (The noticing was SYS-09's moment; the quiet after is this system's.)

### The honesty clause

The fiction's lie is that the second truth was always there — the tells pretend innocence, the arena pretends nothing happened; that lie is the world's to tell, and the system never joins it: one atomic store at the flip's boundary, everything else derived from it, the save records exactly what the world shows, and no reconciliation path can produce a third state. **One world, two truths, one change, no way back.**

### Language law

**Use:** settled, stands, stays, once, no longer, before / after, the world's after, outlives, gone (the first world is gone, not hidden), the one change, underneath, the telling, truth. **Ban:** SYS-09's working words (remember, witness, half-noticed, recognize, still, breath, fault — and *"always there"*; SYS-10 speaks of what stands, not what is remembered); SYS-02's promise-keeping register (room, keeps, word, promise, corridor — *threshold* permitted only as the named mechanism, never a feel-word); control words (shift, toggle, switch, *flip* as a player verb, unlock, trigger, press, command — the mutation has no operator); reversibility words (revert, reset, undo, swap back, again — *"change back"* legal only inside strict negation); machinery leaks in fantasy prose (flag, store, registry, derive, apply, persist, save/load — legal in the section's technical body, never in feel-sentences); spectacle/tech-failure words (surprise, twist, glitch, bug, corruption, dramatic). **Exemptions (the law's own carve): quoted player/playtester utterances (rubric data); cross-references naming another system's register; the fiction's own voice in examples; negated usages of the control/reversibility families; mechanism names (proper nouns included) in the technical body.** Falsifiability rule: every feel-sentence is verifiable by playtest rubric or build check; if verifying it would need a stopwatch on spectacle, cut it.

### Success signature

Playtesters say: *"wait — it's still like that?"* (said as if the answer is obvious); *"I died and it didn't undo it"*; *"the world just went on"*; *"when I read the log, that's when everything changed"* (attributing the change to the telling, never to where they got to). **Anti-signature — The Toggle Question:** any probing of reversibility or permanence — *"can I change it back?"*, *"does it stay like this?"*, *"what if I read it again?"* — or re-verification behavior after a death (re-visiting a flipped landmark to check it took), or a re-read of the threshold log testing for a second flip. One occurrence flags the section; a pattern fails it. The trilogy's anti-signatures tile one event from three sides: SYS-09 owns failures of *cause* ("so the world changes when I press X?"), SYS-02 owns failures of *extent* ("the flip changed one room but not the corridor"), SYS-10 owns failures of **permanence** — the moment reversal is imaginable, the mutation stops being the world aging and becomes two presets and a switch, and every memory the player holds stops mattering, because the before it remembers could come back. **The design test:** after the first playtest with the flip in, ask unprompted — *"is the world still like that?"* A player who answers as if the question is odd ("yes? why wouldn't it be?") means the fantasy landed; a player who pauses to think means The Toggle Question is alive.

*Pillar ties: "One World, Two Truths" (the two truths are the visible half — SYS-02 stores them, SYS-09 renders them; the one world is the invisible half and SYS-10 is its mechanical home: one geometry, one epoch, one change — the load-bearing word is one; a second truth that could be traded back is not a truth but a costume — permanence is what converts the flip from a mechanic into an event in the world's history) · "Five Goodbyes" (the first truth's passing is the count's first, structural goodbye — a whole world's; her remembering has weight only because the before is really gone, which is what makes SYS-09's One Who Remembers possible) · "SotN, Not Hollow Knight" (the one-way re-skin of the familiar map is the inverted castle's actual experience — traversing the doubled world from memory; the mutation's permanence is what makes backtracking memory-work rather than re-learning) · "Read, Then Strike" (the world answers the telling, not the walker — the truth's timetable is the story's) · "Remade Every Death" (the reconstruct wakes you into the world's after — the truth survives your death because the store behind it does).*

## Detailed Design

### Core Rules

**Rule IDs (`TR-WME-*`) are stable requirement proposals — each carries a falsifiable test.** **No `Input` reads; no tile-data writes; no tell-state writes; no save writes (SYS-03's); no render, camera, or audio content.**

- **TR-WME-001 — Own the mutation state; consume the model; the core is pure.** SYS-10 consumes SYS-02's dual-truth data model **as read-only data** (`truth_epoch`, the registered truth flags, the room-level `epoch_pin` — ratified: the mutation application is per-zone derivation with the owning room's pin already resolved; SYS-10 never overwrites a pin) and owns: the mutation registry's game-side validation, the threshold table, the flip request, the epoch write transaction, the load-time reconciliation, the tells' semantic linkage, and the telegraph-text requirements. **`MutationCore`** is a pure `RefCounted` over injected ports: `TruthPort` (read: epoch, flags — SYS-02's publication), `FlipRequestPort` (write: the request to SYS-09's sequencer — the intake ack returns synchronously), `BoundaryPort` (read: SYS-09's `flip_epoch_boundary` signal), `WorldOpsPort` (read: operation-in-flight state for the request eligibility test), `ClockPort` (read: the wall tick + the elapsed-ms stamp — **SYS-29's `GameClock` publication consumed directly through the injected port** (the clock is an injected service — the clock's semantics are SYS-29's contract; no SYS-09 pass-through); the quiet-tick and quiet-time checks' source), `TelemetryPort` (write), `TuningPort` (read). A node adapter subscribes SYS-02's flag-set edge signal and SYS-09's `flip_epoch_boundary` signal and calls core intake methods — flip edges never arrive through a port (SYS-09's published seam pattern, mirrored). No `Input`, no `SceneTree`, no `Time`, no `RenderingServer` inside the core (ADR-0001 §15's seam pattern, applied after `WorldCore`/`RealityCore`). **The core's state space is deliberately tiny** (the States table): three runtime states, no epoch-keyed state at all — the epoch lives in SYS-02's model; SYS-10's only memory of the flip is the absence of further legal stores. *Test: the purity audit (no banned symbols in the core; zero writes outside the single τ0 store across the replay); the port-coverage assertion; a mocked-trace replay is bit-identical.*

- **TR-WME-002 — The mutation registry (the data, validated, closed).** The registry is authored data in SYS-02's consumed shape: `Mutation {set_id, room_id, from_epoch, to_epoch, effects: [Effect…]}`. **Exactly one mechanical set at MVP**: `REVEAL_CISTERN` (room RM-03, from_epoch 0, to_epoch 1) with effects `GATE_OPEN(rm03_partition)` · `ZONE_MATERIALIZE(cold_corridor)` · `INTERACT_EXPOSE(M4)`. **The closed MVP effect vocabulary — three types, no fourth:** `GATE_OPEN(gate_id)` (the gate's collision derives `epoch ≥ to_epoch` — SYS-02's F-RWS-04 verbatim; the gate must have **no independent opener** — the epoch is its only trigger); `ZONE_MATERIALIZE(zone_id)` (the zone's own material rule applies ⟺ `epoch ≥ to_epoch`; before that the zone renders the enclosing room's current-epoch material — sealed, never absent; contributes **zero collision change**); `INTERACT_EXPOSE(target_id)` (**reach, never activate**: the target's collectibility derives from the set's own closure; zero stored state, zero runtime writes; taken-ness lives in `memories_collected` and is orthogonal — the effect asserts reachability only, and is the transaction pre-pass's AC-DR-05 throw point; SYS-09's G1 names the same effect "M4's presence" — presence is the render side's noun (the anchor renders), reach is the mechanics side's (the path exists); one effect, two faces). **Boot validation (V-WME-01/02, joining SYS-02's V-RWS-05/06/07):** every effect's target id resolves in room data; `from_epoch ≤ to_epoch`; the palette map resolves every materialized zone at `to_epoch`; exactly one set; **the closure check** — every `INTERACT_EXPOSE` target lies inside a zone materialized by the same set or behind a gate opened by the same set (M4's reach is *only* through this set — the 3/4→4/4 carrot economy, boot-protected against a later level edit). **SYS-10's private threshold table** (never an extension of SYS-02's record shape — that would be a published-contract revision for a private concern): `{flag_name → set_id}`, one row at MVP (`sanctum_log_read → REVEAL_CISTERN`); validated: the flag ∈ SYS-02's registered table (V-RWS-05), the set ∈ the registry, exactly one row at MVP. **The extensibility law:** a new effect type or a second set is a **contract revision, never a silent shift** (G7) — recorded, not implemented, at MVP. *Test: the registry lint at boot (V-WME-01/02); a second set, an unresolvable target, a set whose to_epoch exceeds `max_epoch`, and a closure-violating expose each fail the boot naming the record.*

- **TR-WME-003 — The trigger chain (the threshold's request; the ordering laws).** The chain: SYS-02's Reading-close law sets `sanctum_log_read` at the operation's completion boundary → SYS-02's adapter emits the **flag-set edge signal** (post-reducer-write; the pre-shaped threshold surface, consumed as-is) → SYS-10's request handler records the flip request **synchronously inside the emitter's tick processing** (side-effect-disciplined: no SYS-02 writes, no SYS-02 calls from within SYS-02's drain) → SYS-09's sequencer intake acks → E1 fires at tick R (SYS-09's TR-DR-004, consumed). **Two ordering laws (determinism, load-bearing):** (1) **request-before-pending-check** — SYS-10's request handler runs before SYS-09's pending-check in the tick's event queue, so E1 fires in the Reading's close tick itself when no operation is in flight (otherwise E1 shifts a tick and the determinism fixture breaks); (2) the request's **eligibility test is request-presence ∧ the GAMEPLAY context gate**, not a Reading-close event (the fresh path's "the Reading context has closed" is request-presence's instance; the load path's request publishes during reconcile and is observed at the first post-load GAMEPLAY tick — SYS-05's context gate, ADR-0001 §3's law consumed; published as a contract input to SYS-09's authoring, its wording preserved verbatim there). **Single-shot enforced by assertion, not trust:** the handler asserts the trigger flag's prior value = unset — a set-edge on an already-set trigger flag is a dev assert + one telemetry record, no request, no state change (a *different* registered flag setting is a consumed no-op — flags serve other systems; at MVP one flag exists). **The standing request (the idempotent safety net):** while `pending ∧ ¬settled ∧ ¬awaiting-τ0 ∧ tail-cleared ∧ no operation in flight` (the unacked path re-emits directly; the acked-then-aborted path re-enters after the abort clears AWAITING_τ0 and the presentation tail clears), the adapter re-emits the request each quiet tick (bounded constant work; rate-limited telemetry on re-emits, never on the first) — this makes SYS-09's > 1 s pending-flip watchdog unreachable in a healthy pipeline; the watchdog stays a tripwire for genuinely lost handshakes (production keeps truth A; SYS-10's repair is *the standing request, never a self-commit* — a unilateral store would land the write before `freeze_open`, a direct E3-order break). *Test: the close-tick E1 fixture (the Reading closes at tick T, no operation in flight → E1 fires at T); the already-set-flag assert fixture; the unacked-re-emission fixture (a stubbed intake denies → re-emits on subsequent quiet ticks → one rate-limited telemetry record); the request-presence fixture (a pending load-path request with no Reading this session → E1 legal).*

- **TR-WME-004 — The epoch write transaction (compute-then-commit; the one store).** At τ0 — on SYS-09's `flip_epoch_boundary` signal, **never before `freeze_open`** (SYS-02's law verbatim; E3's contract) — SYS-10 executes the transaction in AC-DR-05's order: **(1) the derivation pre-pass** — a **read-only validation** resolving each of the set's effects against the post-commit epoch (targets exist; `to_epoch` satisfied; palette members resolve; the expose closure holds) with **zero writes** — because every effect is a pure function of the epoch (SYS-02's derived-open law), "computing the derivations" *is* validating them; **(2) the commit** — **one atomic store**: `truth_epoch := m.to_epoch` (the bound set's target — 1 at MVP; SYS-02's `TruthPort` write through the mutation transaction — the only write SYS-10 ever makes). The commit **returns an outcome** (`committed` / `aborted` + reason) — never a throw across the signal boundary (an unwinding error through SYS-09's emit stack is untestable; the outcome is the seam SYS-09's fault-injection mock consumes). **On abort:** the store never lands; the world is bit-identical to pre-flip; SYS-09 takes its failure exit — the **force-release family** (a second force-release trigger joining the raise denial and the extension overflow, owned by SYS-09's sequencer on SYS-10's `aborted` outcome — rider (ii): the flip source releases early; E3's success semantics, E3b, E4, E7 never fire — **E5 exists only in its degenerate form (the window closes at the force-release boundary with the decay tail; no success beats)**; `freeze_close` derives at the force-release boundary; dev assert + telemetry — AC-DR-05's loud failure) — and SYS-09 enters the **latched parked state** (*flip begun, epoch unlanded*). SYS-10 returns to IDLE with the world at truth A and the flag set; **no new request is minted from the failed handshake** — the standing predicate's re-emission is the repair, resuming on the first post-abort quiet tick once the tear's presentation tail has cleared (the wait consumes SYS-09's `GLITCH_TAIL_MS`; its tick conversion is SYS-29's `freeze_ticks` family, consumed — no SYS-10 ms→ticks conversion), and the resume is a **re-raise from the raise step — the same logical flip's second run**: it re-runs the E1–E7 sequence (E1 re-fires at the resumed raise tick R′; G2's "exactly once" reads per landed flip — **the epoch store lands at most once per load**; G7's fire-once reads per run — rider (i)). The resume's `request_freeze` is a **fresh raise of the same-named source after it has left the set** (the force-release emptied it) — not a same-source re-request; TR-JCE-002's anti-mash law is untouched. **The τ0 punctuality invariant:** the store precedes **every epoch read of wall tick R+1** — SYS-02's material resolution and R+1's render phase included (a post-τ0 truth-A frame outside the pin violates SYS-09's transition law 4; the invariant is SYS-10-published — the ADR-0001 §5 rider naming the boundary phase rides the planned World Mutation ADR, the OQ-M1 pattern). **The two τ0 actions:** the store **and** the `ambient_shift` order to SYS-04 (SYS-10 is E3b's publisher — pinned to `epoch_swap`'s tick, the receding-first composition consumed as data; emitted by the commit's success path only — on abort the bed never turns over while the world stays truth A). **No read race:** the queued in-window save (E4's law) writes at the first unfrozen tick, strictly after the τ0 store, and captures the post-flip epoch by construction. **The abort case (the queued-save's fate):** on abort, the queued in-window save executes at the first unfrozen tick as-is — capturing `{epoch 0, flag set}`, **exactly the deferral-gap state (b)** (the derived pending re-fires the flip at the next load — legal per the withdrawn-cap law); a save requested after the resume's successful commit captures the post-flip epoch. The queued-save fixture covers both paths. *Test: the fault-injection fixtures at both AC-DR-05 throw points (the store injection → bit-identical world; the M4 derivation throw → abort, no store); the outcome-return fixture (the mock consumes `aborted` + reason); the latched-resume fixture (abort → the standing predicate resumes → the re-raise → the choreography completes → the post-resume choreography's trace hash equals the fresh flip's — the force-release prefix is the declared offset, AC-WME-04's); the punctuality fixture (SYS-02's R+1 material resolution observes epoch 1); the E3b pin fixture (`ambient_shift` at τ0's tick exactly; zero arena-bed orders — AC-DR-27's assertion from SYS-10's side); the queued-save fixture (post-τ0 epoch captured).*

- **TR-WME-005 — The derived-open application policy (SYS-10's runtime derivation role is zero).** After the store lands, **nothing is applied by SYS-10**: SYS-02's readers derive the gate's collision, the wing's material, and M4's reachability from the epoch each tick (`gate_open(m, epoch) = epoch ≥ m.to_epoch` — F-RWS-04 verbatim); SYS-09 renders from the same epoch; the effects are checked against the current epoch, never stored. SYS-10's runtime presence is the transaction's pre-pass (at τ0 only) and the boot lint (boot phase only) — **no per-tick validation service exists** (a per-tick presence would forfeit the not-join ruling, TR-WME-011). The application policy SYS-10 owns is therefore *declarative*: the registry is the validated claim that the derivations resolve; the pre-pass is the claim's receipt at the one moment it matters. *Test: the write-ban audit (zero SYS-10 write calls outside the single store — the runtime spy per port, the AC-DR-18 pattern); the no-per-tick-service static audit; the derivation equivalence (the pre-pass's computed values equal the readers' derived values at R+1 — the replay compares).*

- **TR-WME-006 — The save reconciliation (OQ-DR-2's answer: derived, never stored).** **No pending-flip field exists in the save** — the condition is **derived at load** from SYS-02's two existing source families (F-WME-01: `pending(s) = s.flags[T] ∧ (s.epoch < E_to)`); SYS-03 serializes SYS-02's five families unchanged, SYS-10 adds nothing (no schema change, no `schema_version` bump, no migration; no third source can contradict the first two — the honesty clause's "no third state" holds by construction). **The reconcile chain:** `[SYS-03 restore] → [SYS-10 reconcile: validate sources → derive pending → publish the request] → [SYS-05 load-complete] → [first post-load tick — the first GAMEPLAY tick after SYS-05's load-complete (rider (iii)'s context gate): the sequencer observes → RAISED] → [τ0: the store]` — the request is published **strictly before** SYS-05's gate (the re-fire never fires inside the loading context), and the deferral-gap re-fire is the **same logical flip completing in a new session** — the first store of that load, not a second flip (the same-logical-flip doctrine — SYS-09's G2 + the deferral-gap edge case / AC-DR-08 — keeps G2, E4's law, and AC-DR-08 mutually consistent). **The per-state outcomes:** (a) epoch 0 ∧ flag unset → ordinary pre-threshold restore; no request, no flip, no telemetry. (b) epoch 0 ∧ flag set → **the deferral-gap save**: pending true; the request published before load-complete; the choreography re-fires at the first post-load tick; **replay hash identical to the fresh flip** (AC-DR-08); player experience — she sits at the cache; the world turns over under the tear. (c) epoch 1 ∧ flag set → ordinary post-flip restore; truth B renders **from the epoch** regardless of the flag (TR-RWS-014 verbatim); the gate re-derives OPEN; no re-fire — cross-session single-shot. (d) epoch 1 ∧ flag unset → **impossible by construction** (no writer emits epoch-1-without-flag; the slot-overwrite hypothesis produces (a)/(b), never (d)); posture: save-data integrity violation — dev assert + one telemetry record at load; production keeps truth B (the epoch is authoritative for everything material; **no flag repair, no re-fire, no clamp** — the save stays playable). (e) epoch ∉ [0, `max_epoch`] → reconciliation **aborts before the predicate** → SYS-03's corrupted-save path (the slot refuses to load, SYS-03's refusal UX; dev assert + telemetry) — **never clamped** (a silent clamp is the banned silent default; clamping a source is a repair write SYS-10 must not make), never boot-failed (runtime save data, not config). **The absent-key default:** a restored save lacking the trigger-flag key reads the key as **unset** — pending false, no flip, never assumed set (the rename case rides SYS-03's `schema_version` migration; the predicate never guesses). *Test: the five per-state fixtures ((b)'s replay-hash equality; (d)'s assert + truth-B-stands; (e)'s refusal hand-off to the SYS-03 mock); the absent-key fixture; the reconcile-before-load-complete ordering fixture (the request recorded before the gate opens); the no-re-fire fixture ((c) across sessions).*

## Formulas

Three laws govern SYS-10's formulas: **the reconciliation law** (the pending condition derives from the sources, never stores), **the naming law** (the delta namespace is a pure derivation over registry data), and **the consumption law** (SYS-10 converts no ms→ticks of its own — the flip's timing is SYS-09's F-DR-02 family; SYS-10's budget figures are dev-assert measurement bounds, never timing contracts, and never tick-converted).

#### F-WME-01 · pending_flip — the reconciliation predicate (TR-WME-006)

The `pending_flip` formula is defined as:

`pending_flip = s.flags[T] ∧ (s.epoch < E_to)`

**Variables:**

| Variable | Symbol | Type | Range | Description |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| the restored save | `s` | save record | — | the restored save's source families (SYS-02's five, TR-RWS-025) |
| the trigger flag | `s.flags[T]` | bool | {true, false} | the threshold row's trigger flag, read from the registered table (V-RWS-05); an **absent key reads false** (the conservative default — the predicate never guesses) |
| the trigger flag's name | `T` | flag name | {`sanctum_log_read`} at MVP | the threshold table's trigger flag (SYS-10-owned, TR-WME-002) |
| the restored epoch | `s.epoch` | int | [0, `max_epoch`] | the restored `truth_epoch`; **out of range aborts reconciliation before the predicate** (state (e)) |
| the threshold epoch | `E_to` | int | 1 at MVP | the bound set's `to_epoch` (`REVEAL_CISTERN`) |
| the result | `pending` | bool | {true, false} | the derived pending-flip condition — **unclamped, never stored, never serialized** |

**Output Range:** the load-time reconciliation's decision — true publishes the flip request before SYS-05's load-complete gate (the deferral-gap save, state (b)); false → (a) ordinary restore / (c) post-flip no-re-fire / (d) integrity violation (dev assert + telemetry, truth B stands); **(e) aborts before the predicate** (epoch out of range — SYS-03's refusal path), all per TR-WME-006's outcome table. **Examples:** deferral-gap save `{epoch 0, sanctum_log_read: true}` → `true ∧ (0 < 1)` = **true** → the request publishes before load-complete → the choreography re-fires at the first post-load tick (replay hash ≡ the fresh flip, AC-DR-08); post-flip save `{epoch 1, true}` → `true ∧ (1 < 1)` = **false** → no re-fire, cross-session single-shot; pre-threshold save `{epoch 0, unset}` → **false** → ordinary restore.

**Rate law note:** no ms operand exists in F-WME-01 — the predicate is epoch/flag arithmetic; there is no rate-dependent conversion in SYS-10's own math (the flip's ms/tick conversion is SYS-09's F-DR-02 family, consumed; the never-stingy ceil law binds there, not here). SYS-10's budget figures — the pre-pass ≤ 0.5 ms, the reconcile ≤ 1 ms (TR-WME-011) — are dev-assert measurement bounds, never timing contracts.
