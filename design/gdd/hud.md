---

SYS-24 is the glass the whole trust contract is read through: it renders what the landed machines publish and owns nothing they did not say. It is deliberately the smallest presentation system in the game — the minimal MVP set is the HP bar, the quick-slot chip, the dormant Dash socket, the interaction prompt's render, the memory counter, the print slot and the save indicator, the pause affordance and its surfaces (the readout, the loadout rows, Recalibration), the boss bar and the crack cues, the System Offline render with the chassis reboot, and the save refusal/load-result surfaces. Nothing else. Its **one per-tick duty** is the thumb-coverage caps: a bounded integer check consumed from SYS-32's 9-field publication — live every physics tick from activation, never cached — because world-still ≠ screen-still during a hold pan. Everything else is context-time presentation that prices event budgets, not ticks. The HUD introduces **no new gameplay state**: it reads `HealthRead`, `ProgressionRead`, the world store, the save machine's records and SYS-14's publications; it writes exactly one persistence surface (the `settings` namespace, through SYS-03's envelope) and one command surface (the pause's equip/unequip, through SYS-16's command set). It never authors touch geometry for the world — the movement zone, the cluster's audited table, the bubble's placement cascade are SYS-01's; the HUD registers its own regions into the single dispatcher and renders the visuals, and the registration z-order is load-bearing (EX-9). Without it the machines are honest but unread: the body's memory would have no glass, the death would have no print, the lock would have no promise.

→ *Implementation patterns (the `HudCore` reducer, the caps service, the CanvasLayer topology, the AccessKit wiring, the render-command adapter) are ADR raw material for the planned **HUD Architecture ADR**; this GDD stays at the behavior level. The dispatcher's mechanics, the D1 SubViewport wiring and the safe-area derivation are ADR-0001's and SYS-01's; this GDD consumes them.*

## 1 · Player experience — the machine's honest glass

You never watch a health bar. You glance at a chassis fault readout shaped like the machine that wears it: flat rectangles in the print layer's edge, pale bone on dark, segmented in the unit the chassis grows by. Every hit is a subtraction you could have counted on the segments; the low state is a slow breath, not an alarm. The lock where a dash would go says *not yet* by its shape alone — a dim socket and a settled glyph, never a toast, never an error. When you die, the glass does not scold: the world desaturates, the print says SYSTEM OFFLINE, and the machine's options wait. When you spend, the terminal's words land in one shared print row — no popup, no fanfare, no "ORDER RECEIVED". The HUD is the part of the game that has no opinion: it shows you what the machines already decided, in the machine's own voice, and gets out of the way of the fight.

**The glanceability bar (the anti-pillar's instrument):** the HUD's success criterion is negative — a tester watching a fight should report watching *the fight*. Any element that demands gaze (a flashing meter, a counter you must read to play) is a defect against this GDD's own AC, not a style question.

## 2 · Design principles

1. **The minimal-set law.** The MVP element table (§4) is closed. An element not in the table does not ship at MVP, and an element another GDD assigned elsewhere does not migrate here silently — the not-mine list (§4.2) is part of the contract.
2. **The read-only law.** The HUD consumes publications; it never writes a gameplay byte. Its two output surfaces are named exhaustively (the `settings` namespace through SYS-03; the equip/unequip commands through SYS-16). A static audit enforces both.
3. **The no-touch-geometry law.** The HUD registers its own regions into SYS-01's single dispatcher and renders visuals; the movement zone's rect, the cluster's audited table, the bubble's placement cascade, the hit-px floors and the safe-area derivation are SYS-01's — consumed values, never re-derived, never re-owned. No world-side geometry (the bubble's spawn cascade, the telegraph flashes) is authored here.
4. **The live-read law.** The caps derive only from the current tick's publication (TR-CAM-015). A cached `player_screen_px` or a retained publication reference is a defect, not an optimization.
5. **Meaning = text + shape, never color alone.** Every state-bearing HUD visual carries its meaning in text or shape in addition to color (SYS-01's law, extended to every element below). Red is reserved by the REVIEW's rule and appears nowhere in the HUD.
6. **The presentation-clock law.** Per-tick semantics (the caps) run on the physics tick; menu/screen feel (holds, fades, pulses) is wall-clock UI-feel (SYS-01's TR-INP-061 doctrine; SYS-15's presentation-clock law consumed). A hit-stop holds the world, not the menus; a menu's fade never gates a gameplay fact.

## 3 · Architecture — the seam, the per-tick pass, the not-join

- **TR-HUD-000 — The purity seam.** **`HudCore`** is a pure `RefCounted` reducer over injected ports: `CameraPublicationPort` (SYS-32's 9-field publication — the sole channel, TR-CAM-015), `HealthPort` (`HealthRead` — SYS-07), `ProgressionPort` (`ProgressionRead` — SYS-16), `WorldPort` (SYS-02's read view: `memories_collected`'s length, the placed-fragment total, `truth_epoch`), `BossPort` (SYS-14's rig registration + `crack_stage` + `grief_phase`), `SavePort` (SYS-03's read view: the save machine's state, `load_result`, the slot-list metadata), `PrintPort` (SYS-29's print-slot records), `SettingsPort` (the settings namespace + the consumed player-facing settings), `TuningPort`, `TelemetryPort`, `RegistrationPort` (the region registrations out to SYS-01's dispatcher). **No `Input`, no `SceneTree`, no `Time`, no `RenderingServer` inside the core** (ADR-0001 §15's seam pattern, applied to the presentation half); the node adapter owns the CanvasLayer, the dispatcher registration calls, the AccessKit property surface and the SubViewport body-effect injection (the chassis reboot). Determinism: the same inputs at the same ticks produce bit-identical decision traces (the caps decisions + the per-tick render-command diff). *Test: the core instantiates with fakes; the purity audit greps the four forbidden symbols; two replays are bit-identical (AC-01's trace clause, AC-25's replay clause).*
- **TR-HUD-000a — The per-tick pass is the caps service alone.** The HUD's gameplay-tick work is exactly one bounded pass: consume the publication's current slot, evaluate the caps (§5), emit the per-tick render commands for the affected visuals. No other HUD subsystem runs per tick: the bar, the counter, the chip's contents and the print slot update **on change** (a publication-diff check that costs one comparison per read struct per tick is part of the same pass's bounded work; a changed value emits one update command). *Test: the soak's pass-count assertion (one pass per tick, no hidden per-tick allocators) inside AC-25.*
- **TR-HUD-000b — The envelope join and the not-join.** The HUD joins the MVP gameplay-tick envelope with its own entry: **p99.9 ≤ 0.10 ms / max ≤ 0.30 ms per physics tick** (the caps pass is a handful of integer comparisons over borrowed references; the change-diff adds the read structs' compares). The **seven-system envelope re-records as eight: p99.9 ≤ 2.55 / max ≤ 6.85 ms** (2.45 + 0.10; 6.55 + 0.30) — the re-derivation is recorded here and the registry's envelope record re-records at the sync (never silent). The sum is the budget allocation; the **combined eight-system soak is the QA-plan sync's gate** (owner qa-lead, the AC-JCE-33 stopwatch methodology extended to the co-running eight; the HUD's W-number is assigned at that sync — the OQ-J2/OQ-CAM-8 pattern; no single-system AC asserts the combined number). The **context-time surfaces join no tick envelope** (the SYS-15 not-join precedent consumed): the pause, Recalibration, the offline screen, the refusal/load-result surfaces and the print slot price event budgets, not ticks — no SYS-24 symbol belongs in the gameplay-tick envelope's sum beyond the caps entry. *Test: AC-25 (the HUD's own 10,000-tick soak with the envelope-identity re-derivation); the traceability audit names the not-join.*
- **TR-HUD-000c — The allocation bar.** No hot-path allocation (the AC-54 family). Preallocated: the publication snapshot slot (one), one read struct per port (fixed), the caps result (one), the render-command scratch (fixed), the print-slot record (one). Heap growth ≤ 256 KB; churn ≤ 16 KB / 1,000 ticks; net object/resource delta 0 across the soak. *Test: inside AC-25.*

## 5 · The 9-field publication consumption (the spine)

- **TR-HUD-001 — The live-read law.** Every physics tick **from the camera's activation** the caps service consumes the publication's current slot into the HUD's own per-tick snapshot; no reference is retained past the tick (TR-CAM-015's memory shape consumed; the ports return retained objects — the snapshot is a bounded copy of the eight consumed fields). **The consumed fields:** `mode`, `view_rect_px`, `player_screen_px`, `on_screen`, `shake_offset_px`, `active_hold`, `bounds_generation`, `tick`. **`authority` is not consumed** — it is instrumentation and never a consumer input (TR-CAM-015's own law; the HUD has no read path for it). The caps' every decision derives from the current snapshot — **never cached, never world position, never a retained read** (world-still ≠ screen-still during a hold pan). **Pre-activation** (no publication at all — before AC-CAM-20's gate): the HUD renders its default layout with no caps-driven adaptation and its regions already registered inside the safe rect — the boot frame is presentation's, not the camera's; the first activation tick arms the caps. *Test: the liveness fixture (a scripted 12-px/tick hold pan over a world-fixed body: the rendered center changes every tick and the caps' decisions change with it; an injected stale read fails the trace); the retention audit (no publication reference outlives its tick); the authority-ignored audit; the activation fixture (zero caps work before the first publication).*
- **TR-HUD-002 — The protective posture (the missing-input failure path).** `on_screen = false` — whether she is legitimately off-frame or the camera's lost-body conservative default publishes it — applies the **protective posture**: the interactive layer holds its default footprint, never expands toward the frame interior, and no visual is fabricated to cover her ("treat her as off-frame and keep guarding; never a stale or fabricated true" — the camera's law consumed). A **`bounds_generation` change** mid-room (the adopt): the caps re-derive from the live fields in the same tick — the view origin may move, and no cached geometry survives the adopt (the camera's F-RWS-02 path consumed). A **stall** (the `tick` field stops advancing): the caps hold the protective posture for the stalled span, one telemetry record names it, and the HUD's touch regions stay live (the HUD never freezes its own capture because the camera froze). *Test: the stall fixture (both failure orders — the false-by-extent path and the false-by-stall path — yield the same posture); the adopt fixture (a mid-hold generation change re-derives within the tick); the never-fabricated assert.*
- **TR-HUD-003 — The hold-mode anchor rule.** `mode`/`active_hold` tell the HUD a hold is live; the two sub-phases are distinguished by the **live** data alone. During the **approach** (the frame's screen anchor moves, ≤ 12 px/tick at MVP): the caps evaluate every tick against the live `player_screen_px` (+ `shake_offset_px`, F-HUD-01) and any layout response steps with the pan, bounded by the pan's own per-tick delta. The **fix** is detected, not assumed: the derived anchor's per-tick movement ≤ `CAPS_SETTLE_EPS_PX` (0.5 art px — the camera's snap epsilon) for `CAPS_SETTLE_TICKS` (6) consecutive ticks while `active_hold ≠ null` → the anchor is fixed → the hold layout **solves once**; the guard continues to evaluate every tick, and a swap/adopt/stall re-arms the detection. **Failsafe:** if the settle condition has not fired within `CAPS_SETTLE_TIMEOUT_TICKS` (600 = 10 s) of the hold's start, the caps adopt the protective posture and telemetry names the defect (dev assert) — the HUD never churns a per-tick layout forever. Post-release (`active_hold → null`, fresh FOLLOW): the full form returns and the settle state resets. *Test: the two-sub-phase fixture (the approach tracks per tick; the fix solves once and holds; the exit restores); the settle-detection fixture (exact-arrival holds; a never-settling defect hits the failsafe); the re-arm fixture (an adopt during a solved hold re-solves once).*
- **TR-HUD-004 — The collapse rule (the exposure mitigation).** The hold's thumb-band exposure — the reveal's structural low frame; the arena's authored floor inset that still leaves her feet 24 art px from the frame bottom at the band's low extreme (TR-CAM-012) — is mitigated here, in the caps' geometry: while a hold is live **and fixed**, every **bottom-anchored interactive visual** (cluster buttons, chip, sockets, the home ghost's visual) renders the **collapsed form** — its opaque content reduces to the **affordance strip**: a glyph bar pinned to the visual's bottom edge, ≤ `THUMB_BAND_CEILING_ART_PX` (24 art px) tall, at `CAPS_COLLAPSE_ALPHA` (0.5); the **hit rect, the registration and the z-order are unchanged** — capture stays full-size, so the controls remain operable and no re-registration churns mid-hold. During play (no hold), the **same collapse applies to any visual the body rect intersects** (F-HUD-01's occlusion predicate) — the play-time trigger is the guard, the hold-time trigger is the law. Transitions are one-tick steady swaps — **no animation, no alternation** (the photosensitivity law's shape; `REDUCED_FLASH` has nothing to remove here). **The full form's license during play:** the follow law keeps the body center-frame (the camera's vertical bias), so the un-collapsed visuals occlude nothing in the ordinary case; the guard covers the transients (the deep-fall tip, the knockdown's low arc). The **pause button and the top-band displays never collapse** (they never covered the frame's bottom interior). *Test: the collapse fixture (the hold collapses every bottom-anchored visual to ≤ 24 art px of opaque content; the glyph reads; the hit rect is byte-identical before/after; the exit restores the full form); the play-time occlusion fixture; the no-animation assert (a one-tick swap; zero alternation frames); V-HUD-06.*

#### F-HUD-01 · occlusion_guard — the caps' core predicate (TR-HUD-002/004)

The guard's position input is the **rendered** center — the world's pixels move under shake, so the occlusion truth is post-shake:

```
rendered_center = player_screen_px + shake_offset_px        (int art px, view-relative)
body_rect       = rendered_center ± (guard_half_w, guard_half_h)
occluded(v)     = on_screen ∧ intersect(v_screen_rect, body_rect)
low_profile(v)  = (hold_fixed ∧ bottom_anchored(v)) ∨ occluded(v)
```

**Variables:**
| name | type | range | source |
|---|---|---|---|
| `player_screen_px` | int art px pair | view-relative, legitimately negative or > VIEW | the publication (TR-CAM-015) |
| `shake_offset_px` | int art px pair | ≤ `SHAKE_MAX_PX` 3 per axis (SYS-29's envelope) | the publication |
| `on_screen` | bool | — | the publication (the hurtbox-extent test) |
| `guard_half_w`, `guard_half_h` | int art px | derived at boot | `guard_half_* = ceil(standing_extent_half) + CAPS_GUARD_MARGIN_PX` from **SYS-06's published standing-extent constants** — derived, never owned (the SYS-32 re-assert pattern); a missing/invalid extent constant is a dev boot failure (the cross-system-input rule) |
| `CAPS_GUARD_MARGIN_PX` | int | 4 (range 2–8) | the HUD's knob |
| `v_screen_rect` | int art px rect | inside the view | the registration converted by SYS-01's `RenderMetrics` (dp → art px at the live `k`, the band origin) |
| `hold_fixed` | bool | — | TR-HUD-003's detection |

**Output Range:** `low_profile` ∈ {collapsed, full} per visual; the guard never mutates a hit rect, a registration or a z-order. **Worked examples:** (1) the reveal's low frame at the binding profile (640×360 dp @ ρ 2.0 → k 2, so 1 dp = 1 art px): her feet 24 px above the view bottom, the body center ≈ y 202 (44-px body), `body_rect` y ∈ [176, 228]; the Attack visual's top edge at 90 art px up from the bottom (y 180) intersects → collapsed, while the hold-fixed trigger already collapsed every bottom-anchored visual — the two triggers agree. (2) play, no hold: the deep-fall tip puts the rendered center at y 240 → `body_rect` y ∈ [214, 266]; the Attack visual (y ≥ 180) intersects → collapsed for the intersect span; the hysteresis (below) prevents boundary flicker as she passes; the counter, the bar and the pause button are top-band and never in the predicate's reach. (3) `on_screen = false` during the camera's stall: the predicate short-circuits false; the protective posture governs; **no** visual is collapsed by a fabricated position (there is none). (4) the shake case: a 3-px shake shifts the rendered center — a visual clear at the pre-shake center and intersecting at +3 px collapses; the predicate reads both fields of the same tick.

**The hysteresis (anti-flicker):** enter the collapsed form when `occluded(v)` turns true; exit only when the rect has been clear by ≥ `CAPS_HYST_PX` (4 art px) for ≥ `CAPS_HYST_TICKS` (6 = 100 ms). A body oscillating on a visual's edge produces no per-tick form flapping. *Test: the hysteresis fixture (a boundary walk produces ≤ 2 form changes; a raw per-tick predicate would produce dozens).*

#### F-HUD-02 · thumb_band_ceiling — the constant the world's inset references (TR-HUD-004)

## 11 · Visuals — the look, pinned at the bible's tables

*(The palette and the shape grammar are the art bible's; this GDD pins the HUD's application of them. The asset-spec pass follows `/asset-spec system:hud` after the art bible — the standing flag.)*

- **The shape grammar:** flat rectangles and ticks in the print layer's hard 1-px edge; no skeuomorphic chrome, no rounded glass (the bible's §7 consumed). The HUD echoes the world through type and edge, never ornament.
- **The placements (the top band's three zones + the bottom's controls):** the bar top cluster-opposite corner (16 dp margin); the memory counter beside the bar; the print slot top center (the boss bar's reserve above it when active); the pause button the stick-side top corner; the cluster/chip/sockets per the audited table + TR-HUD-006; the bubble per SYS-01's cascade.
