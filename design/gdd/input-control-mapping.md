## Overview

The Input & Control Mapping system is the game's action registry and control-feel contract. It defines every player verb as a named input action — **Move, Jump, Attack, Crouch, Dash (locked at start), Backdash (day-one gesture), Interact (contextual), Quick-use Item (reserved slot)** — and maps them across touch (floating stick + button cluster), keyboard, and gamepad, so the touch-first mobile experience and the day-one PC experience are the same game. On the infrastructure side it owns the cross-platform policies every other system consumes: press/buffer semantics, input priority when verbs conflict, gesture reservation for the Demonic Cyber Stinger (vertical-slice), remapping and persistence, and accessibility. On the experience side it is why the game feels SotN-committed rather than twitchy: every action answers immediately or is buffered deliberately, telegraph-reactive inputs (clash, deflect) stay frame-honest, and nothing important is buried behind a gesture the player must discover. Without it, the game loses its platform promise, its control-weight identity, and the shared action vocabulary that Movement, Combat, Stinger, HUD, and Juice all build on.

→ *Implementation patterns (InputMap layout, buffering service, remap persistence) are specified in the **Input Architecture ADR** — planned; created via `/architecture-decision` and cited by ID here once it exists.*

## Player Fantasy

### The named fantasy — The Body Obeys

You deliberate; she obeys. Every order kept, every commitment honored. You are the will inside a purpose-built chassis, and the body never mishears an order: presses inside the breath are never lost, never doubled, never second-guessed. Deliberation is safe *because* the body is trustworthy — this is the "slick and smart" competence fantasy at its root. The fiction resonance is free: in the story her body obeys the demon lord; at the input layer, it obeys **you** — the player is the self she is trying not to lose. The fiction labels, never prescribes: no simulated impedance, no "warming up" — the body is precise because the input layer is precise.

### The contract — The Honored Commitment

Pressing is signing. Startup and recovery are the cost of signing — *those values belong to Combat and Movement; this system owns only the trust half*: that a decision, once pressed, is executed honestly through its full arc. Three terms, testable on every rig:

1. **A press inside the intent window is never lost** — the window holds it; a press outside the window is not a promise this system makes.
2. **A press is never doubled** — the second-hit lockout is honest.
3. **A press resolves at the intended frame** — never swallowed, never fired early into a whiff.

Verb clarity is a term of the contract, not a feature: eight verbs — Move, Jump, Attack, Crouch, Dash, Backdash, Interact, Quick-use Item — each means exactly one thing, in every room, in every truth, on every rig. Interact's carve-out is honest by design: *one button — the world tells you what it means.* Backdash's gesture carve-out is honest the same way: *one shape — the stick tells you what it means*, with a direct a11y press as the button-rig source (TR-INP-056). One honest per-verb family, stated in the open — each verb's unavailable behavior is named, not implied: **Interact waits** (TR-INP-029's window derivation), **crouch publishes raw** (never held back — posture application during a commitment is Movement's handoff, TR-INP-066/F-04), **backdash drops** (EX-1's reflex-class rule) — a dodge ordered into an occupied hand is **declined, not misheard**; the defensive family — backdash · Dash · clash — finalizes commitment behaviors in Combat's GDD. **Three rigs, one body**: touch is the native rig, not an adaptation — parity of contract, not of milliseconds. Never apologize for mobile.

### Reserved slots are promise, not absence

Dash and Quick-use Item are empty sockets in a body built with room to grow — "built for verbs she doesn't have yet" turns locked-at-start into anticipation. When the Stinger arrives (vertical slice), the contract extends to gestures: the game asks for *shapes, never reflexes*, and the touch tolerance window is generosity, codified.

### Anchor moment — The Early Press

A heavy enemy winds up a wide overhead. The player reads it and presses Attack **within a breath** *early*. The intent window holds the press; the body answers on the exact frame of contact: clash, lock, sparks. *The press was early; the answer was on time.* Combat owns the clash's design; **input owns whether the early press was kept** — held within the intent window, resolved at the optimal frame, identical on floating-stick and keyboard. The promise is **window-scoped**: a press inside the intent window is kept; a press outside it is a read the system does not promise to honor. Every failure mode **inside the window** indicts input, not tuning: fires into a whiff (buffer too stingy), fires twice (too greedy), swallowed by lockout (dishonest). And the strategy the body rewards must remain the deliberate read — downstream press-age rulings inherit this term: mash-through-windup must be **weakly dominated** — some timing exists where the single deliberate early press wins and the mash stream fails or pays (D6, TR-INP-022's handoff).

### Language law for this GDD (all sections)

Use *kept, honored, exact, whole, trustworthy*; ban *crisp, snappy, instant, frame-perfect, razor-thin, zero lag* — twitch-coded promises the design rejects. "Precision" is the body's property, never the player's demand. The intent window is "a breath" in player-facing copy; millisecond values live only in Tuning Knobs. Remapping is diegetically *Recalibration* (flavor in the UI half, near-zero cost). No per-press popups — "ORDER RECEIVED" on every button would cheapen MEMORY CACHED's rarity. Falsifiability rule: every feel-sentence must be verifiable by a playtest or build check; if verifying it would need a stopwatch on reflexes, cut it.

*Pillar ties: "SotN, Not Hollow Knight" (deliberation as chosen texture, not sluggishness) · "Read, Then Strike" (an optional miracle must be dependable) · "Remade Every Death" (body half only — the memory/caching fiction belongs to the death systems, not this GDD).*

**Success signature**: playtesters describe the controls in trust-language ("it always does what I mean"), call out the early press unprompted, and nobody — touch or PC — describes startup frames as lag.

## Detailed Design

### Core Rules

**Rule IDs (`TR-INP-*`) are stable requirement proposals — each carries a falsifiable test and is ready for `tr-registry.yaml` ingestion.** PROVISIONAL marks indicate contracts with undesigned downstream GDDs; ownership boundaries are intended final.

#### 1 · The action registry — two tiers, eight body verbs (five impulse + two state + one recognized gesture)

- **TR-INP-001 — Two-tier registry.** All player input resolves through one named-action registry: **Tier 1 — body verbs** (the eight-verb vocabulary = **five impulse verbs + two state verbs + `backdash`**, plus the reserved Stinger socket) and **Tier 2 — system verbs** (Pause; Open Map at VS). Tier 2 never aliases a Tier 1 id. *Test: build audit lists every action; each in exactly one tier.*
- **TR-INP-002 — One meaning per verb.** Exactly one semantic meaning across every room, context, rig. *Test: no verb does two things; playtesters can't name a button that "sometimes does two things."*
- **TR-INP-003 — Status lifecycle.** Every action is exactly one of **Active**, **Locked-at-start** (registered, delivery gated by the owning system's grant), **Reserved-VS** (socket only, no consuming code in MVP). *Test: Dash pre-unlock produces no execution and no error state.*
- **TR-INP-004 — No derived moves in the registry (amended 2026-11-11: eight body verbs).** The registry holds **eight** body verbs: **five impulse verbs (Jump, Attack, Dash, Interact, Quick-use) + two state verbs (Move, Crouch) + `backdash`** (a RECOGNIZED verb — the gesture detector publishes it from the stick, or it arrives as a direct press via its accessibility sources; see TR-INP-056). "Crouch attack," "air attack," "clash" are not input actions — they are Combat states produced from (verb press + body state). No NINTH body verb without a ratification amendment. *Test: a third aerial variant requires zero registry/input changes; the count is eight and audited (AC-01/AC-04).*
- **TR-INP-005 — Single puller per verb.** One designated consumption owner per action; second registration rejected. *Test: unit test injects two consumers; second is rejected.*

- **TR-INP-057 — Behavior-level gating (traversal ladder).** The Jump action is live from day one; its **air-jump** and **wall-jump** behaviors unlock through progression — ladder: **Dash → Wall Jump → Double Jump** (user-mandated 2026-11-11). Gates are Movement's (SYS-06); input delivers every Jump press honestly regardless of unlock state. Contrast with Dash's action-level lock (TR-INP-003). *Test: pre-unlock jump pressed mid-air executes nothing and errors nowhere; post-unlock it executes — input behavior bit-identical in both cases.*
- **TR-INP-056 — Backdash gesture (MVP, day-one).** Double-tap **back** with a release between taps, both edges inside `BACKDASH_TAP_WINDOW_MS` (default 280 — generous, SotN-exact feel). Touch: flick-back → release → flick-back on the floating stick; keyboard/gamepad: double-tap the back binding — **or the a11y direct source** (KB `C` / pad `B`, rebindable; touch = toggle-only cluster socket). The detector publishes a `backdash` intent; **SYS-06 pulls and owns the hop**. **Reflex-class: not buffered** — fires when the body is free at pattern completion; if the body is committed, the pattern is **dropped at MVP** (a delayed dodge can walk the player into a hit; the Combat/Movement defensive-family ruling D-5 may amend to short-defer by recorded ruling — EX-1). Brief i-frames are the combat GDD's ruling (defensive family: backdash · Dash · clash). **Direction discipline (rewritten — D4, re-review #5; amends R-13's same-sign classification):** press1 must be a **true back entry** — its entry into the **active arming zone** (the WALK band by default; the promoted `BACKDASH_ARM_MAG` zone when the fallback is active) must satisfy `entry_sign = −facing` **at press1**; a forward entry (`entry_sign = facing`) never arms the machine, so a **forward double-tap never fires** (a KB tap-walk D-D while facing right is two forward entries — nothing). The chain's `entry_sign` is latched at that press1 and **every late same-sign re-arm inherits it** — re-arms never re-classify, so the sustained retreat (the press1-induced turn, then keep flicking the same way) arms normally. The intent payload carries **`(entry_sign)` only** — **hop direction = `entry_sign`** (away from the threat she faced at press1); **SYS-06 sets facing = −`entry_sign` during the hop** (she faces the threat while hopping away — SotN-exact). A facing flip **after press1 other than the press1-induced flip** — knockback, a wall turn — still **resets the machine to IDLE** (flip-attribution). An entry into the active arming zone with the sign **opposite the chain's `entry_sign`** between the presses is a direction change and **resets to IDLE**; an **in-band sign flip** (the stick crosses zero without dropping below the release threshold) is ruled **release + opposite entry** → reset; an **opposing-key edge** (the other direction's key between presses) is an opposite-sign entry → reset; a **rig-transfer edge** is classified by the same entry rules (the published `(raw_axis, move_order)` stream is rig-blind — same-sign continues, opposite-sign resets); creep-magnitude wobble between edges is ignored. **A11y direct source:** the KB `C` / pad `B` press publishes independently of the gesture machine — direction = opposite the **currently published** `facing` at the press; a direct press while the machine is mid-pattern **resets the machine to IDLE** (different source, different gesture). **Arming:** press edges are entries into the arming zone — by default the walk band (one definition of "back is pressed"; a shallow tap peaking below the threshold is *correctly* a walk, not a tap); the `BACKDASH_ARM_MAG` fallback decouples the gesture from the walk band only if device evidence (AC-64) shows natural flicks under-arm (pairwise-validated, V12/V16). **Release = dropping strictly below the arming *release* threshold (review fix — the pair, named):** with the default arming zone this is exactly the walk-band exit (< 0.40 — F-03's own hysteresis); if the fallback is promoted, the release threshold is `BACKDASH_ARM_RELEASE` = ARM − 0.05 (pair invariant V17: spread ≥ 0.05 — sensor noise at the boundary cannot arm/release/arm a pattern completion from a stationary thumb). *Test: tap-tap-back on empty ground = hop in the entry-sign direction (SYS-06 sets facing = −entry_sign during the hop); tap-tap-back during attack recovery = nothing; sustained retreat (turn, then keep flicking the same direction) = chained attempts, each arming with the chain's entry_sign; a forward double-tap (press1 = forward entry) never fires; held back = walk, never backdash; opposite-sign entry, an in-band sign flip, or an opposing key between the presses = reset; a `C` press mid-pattern resets the machine.*

#### 3 · Delivery semantics — events and states

- **TR-INP-013 — Two channels.** **Events** (Jump, Attack, Dash, Interact, Quick-use, backdash) are edge-triggered intents that may be buffered. **States** (move axis, crouch, jump-held, grounded, body-busy) are continuously readable body truth. Events may wait; states never do.
- **TR-INP-014 — States are raw.** No smoothing, no grace of its own; hardware-noise deadzone only — **and only in F-02: engine InputMap deadzones are forced to 0 at registration so exactly one layer owns noise rejection** (review fix; the signed-strength representation for injected/hardware action strength is an ADR decision, OQ#1). Walk/run curves and coyote are Movement's. *Grace belongs to Movement and Combat — never to the post office.* *Test: toggling coyote changes behavior while the reported `grounded` stream is bit-identical.*
- **TR-INP-015 — Edge-triggered only; OS repeat filtered.** Hold = one press. *Test: 3 s hold under OS key-repeat = exactly one press.*
- **TR-INP-016 — Multi-touch is table stakes.** ≥3 simultaneous contacts, zero cross-capture. *Test: move + jump + attack concurrently.*
- **TR-INP-017 — Holding is the buffer states already have.** States are never buffered; a held state persists into the next resolvable frame (airborne Down held → still held on landing).
- **TR-INP-076 — Event stamping law (new — review fix; owns the touch→physics latency budget; logic-tick split 2026-11-11).** Every captured event is stamped to the **first `logic_tick` boundary at or after arrival** — never back-dated (zero retroactive stamps under fuzz). **Two clocks:** `physics_tick` always advances; `logic_tick` advances only while gameplay time runs (frozen under hit-stop, stopped in non-Gameplay contexts). **Capture entry point:** raw enqueue in exactly **one input callback per event family** — the ADR pins `_input` (pre-GUI) for gameplay capture; registering both `_input` and `_unhandled_input` for the same family double-captures unconsumed events — no semantic processing on the render path (this is what makes AC-53's "zero semantic render-path work" true); all semantic work (F-02/03/04, binding, buffer) runs at the next `physics_tick`'s capture phase, whose relationship to engine input flushing is pinned by engine-verification item 16 (`agile_input_flushing`/`input_buffering` — per-frame vs per-tick flush changes the arrival distribution this law and AC-65 measure). **Arrival timestamp:** a monotonic clock reading taken at engine receipt (`Time.get_ticks_usec()` at the capture callback — Godot events carry no reliable cross-platform OS timestamp; OS-event-time exposure is engine-verification item 12, and if unavailable the device budget degrades to callback-arrival with a documented OS-side caveat). **Multi-tick frames** (2 ticks in one render frame at 30 fps): all queued captures process in the next `physics_tick`'s capture phase, arrival order preserved, never retroactively distributed. **Dispatcher runs on `physics_tick`, stamps on `logic_tick`.** While `logic_tick` is frozen, the dispatcher drains captures but does not offer or process gameplay intents; a press arriving during a freeze stamps at the first `logic_tick` after unfreeze. **Freeze-onset boundary (re-review #4 fix):** the tick in which the freeze request lands is the last stamped `logic_tick` — a press arriving on that boundary's capture phase stamps at it; presses arriving after it wait for unfreeze. Budget (device half conditional on engine-verification item 12 yielding OS event time — B8): with OS event time, p99.9(arrival→stamp) ≤ 2 `logic_tick`s and max ≤ 3 on the reference profiles, release build, **measured in unfrozen gameplay; freeze arrivals are excluded and asserted separately** (AC-65); without it the budget is ADR-owned with the documented callback-arrival caveat and no device gate. Remediation on breach is ADR-owned (stamp-policy amendment or dispatch-pipeline escalation, OQ#1). *Test: sub-tick-offset fuzz stamps to the next boundary; freeze arrivals stamp at unfreeze.*

#### 4 · The intent window — "a breath" (all ratified)

- **TR-INP-018 — One service, uniform default, per-verb single slots.** `INTENT_WINDOW_MS` = 140 default applied per verb's own slot — *not* one shared slot (same-frame Jump+Attack both survive). Per-verb overrides via tuning map. *Test: same-frame Jump+Attack during recovery → both pending independently.*
- **TR-INP-019 — Entry.** A press enters at its **stamp tick** (the first `logic_tick` boundary at or after arrival — TR-INP-076) with a **stamp-tick body snapshot** (crouching, grounded, move_x, body-busy) attached.
- **TR-INP-020 — Offer–pull–ack.** Input **offers** the pending intent to its single puller every frame; the puller decides when to pull (Combat pulls at the clash-contact frame) and **acks**; input clears the slot. Input never executes on its own — it structurally cannot fire early into a whiff. **Pull–ack atomicity (review fix):** pull and ack are **one atomic service call** — `try_pull(verb) → intent | none` — the consumed flag, the snapshot, and the custody transfer are a single transaction committed **before** the puller's accept is invoked. A puller that faults after commit has been *honored but unexecuted* — logged as a puller fault, never re-offered (one-consumption is absolute); the slot can never be observed half-consumed. Dev assert: accept completes within the tick. *Test (the Early Press): attack pressed early during a windup → offered each frame → pulled at the strike-contact frame → clash.*
- **TR-INP-021 — One consumption.** A pulled intent cannot fire twice; a new press opens a new window. Layered with Combat's active-frame lockout, "never doubled" holds twice over.
- **TR-INP-022 — Latest Order Honored (the anti-mash law, corrected theorem).** A second press of the same verb while pending **supersedes**: `press_tick` and snapshot are replaced, the window restarts, one slot per verb forever. The law's true guarantees: **(G1) count** — the slot resolves at most once per consumption; mashing cannot produce a second resolution while the first is pending (layered with Combat's active-frame lockout, "never doubled" holds twice over). **(G2) freshness** — the order honored at any pull is the most recent press at that tick. **(G3) keep-alive corollary, owned explicitly** — a same-verb stream whose inter-tap gap ≤ `window_ms` maintains an unbroken pending slot and can carry an order across an arc longer than one window; it cannot create extra resolutions (G1) and the honored order is always fresh (G2); residual chain-rate consequences are Combat's tuning (ratified boundary). **Retracted claim (Annex EX-6):** "identical stall durations across tap rates" is false — a stream whose inter-tap gap exceeds `window_ms` leaves pending gaps, and a pull frame inside a gap resolves nothing. Stall parity is not an input guarantee and never was. **Combat handoff (review ruling D-7, 2026-11-11; amended by R-11, re-review #4):** the honored intent's payload exposes `press_tick` and supersede recency (age at pull) — documentation of existing record data, zero new machinery; Combat's GDD owns any clash-gating rule built on press age. Input promises the field, never the ruling — **with one inherited design term (tightened per D6, re-review #5 — replaces R-11's "never strictly dominant," which G3 makes unfalsifiable):** Combat's press-age/clash gating must make mash **weakly dominated** — at least one arc timing exists where a single deliberate early press resolves the clash and a same-verb mash stream into the same windup either fails to resolve it or pays a documented cost (the cost must be named in Combat's GDD at authoring and be player-observable; absent a named cost, only the fail branch counts); **hold-repeat a11y presses are exempt** from the mash definition; the term is falsified if no such timing exists — **probe: a GUT sweep on AC-20's arc harness at Combat's GDD review** (the playtest Q2 returns to non-gating observation — B11). *Test: 12 t/s stream (83 ms gaps) across a 600 ms arc mock → pending at every tick of the arc, exactly one resolution at the mock's pull frame; 4 t/s stream (250 ms gaps) against the same arc → pending gaps exist and a pull frame in a gap resolves nothing — both behaviors asserted, not assumed.*
- **TR-INP-023 — Expiry is silent.** Dropped with no execution, no notification (per-press popups would cheapen MEMORY CACHED's rarity).
- **TR-INP-024 — The window ticks on the game clock (ratified).** Hit-stop extends the breath 1:1; paused contexts clear intents outright. Touch impulse verbs **bind on touchdown** (the region takes the touch — ratified); the verb's **commit moment follows its confirmation weight** (TR-INP-068, D-2 ruling 2026-11-11): tap-weight verbs (Attack, Jump, Dash, Interact) sign on touchdown; hold-weight verbs (Quick-use chip) sign on the measured release (F-09 — the chip's press moment is its commit tick). On tap verbs the release is additionally delivered as an honest separate event (unused at MVP; enables hold-glide later with zero input changes; a release with no live binding is dropped at the dispatcher — never enters a slot). *Test: buffered press consumed N ms of game-time later regardless of wall-clock; jump pressed mid-clash window resolves at touchdown.*
- **TR-INP-025 — Jump-buffering vs coyote, the division.** Press 60 ms before landing → held → Movement pulls → jump on landing (input's buffering). Press 60 ms after walking off → input already reported airborne honestly; firing is Movement's coyote call. Input is identical in both coyote settings.
- **Conversion law (ratified).** `ticks = ceil(ms × physics_tps ÷ 1000)` — 140 ms → **9 ticks (150 ms @ 60 Hz)**: the window never rounds stingy. Override knobs are expressed in milliseconds; conversion is centralized in the buffer service. *Other touch feel timers (fades, hold-guards, hysteresis) run on wall-clock* — they are UI-feel, not gameplay intents (TR-INP-061).

#### 5 · Priority and conflict resolution — the post-office doctrine

- **TR-INP-026 — Input delivers, timestamps, dedupes; it does not judge.** Verbs are compatible by default; consumers arbitrate with honest, complete delivery.

|---|---|---|
| State + event (crouch held + Attack) | Both delivered; consumer selects the derived move | SYS-08 |
| Two events, same frame | Both delivered, arrival order preserved | consumers |
| Same verb repeated in window | Latest Order Honored — one pending intent | SYS-01 |
| Same verb after consumption | New window; one press = one execution | SYS-01 |

- **TR-INP-028 — Derived-move boundary (ratified).** *Crouch-state + Attack = crouch attack* is **Combat's rule** over input's honest delivery. **Consumption-frame body state governs** derived-move selection; the stamp-tick snapshot rides along as intent context (input delivers both timestamps so Combat can choose without re-plumbing). **Combat's selection reads the raw published crouch (`latch ∨ held_zone`, B2) — never a commitment-frozen value.** *Test: crouch → Attack → release crouch mid-window before the pull → stand attack executes; snapshot still logged `crouching=true`.*
- **TR-INP-029 — Body availability.** Interact (and Quick-use at VS) resolve only when the body is **available**: Movement publishes locomotion-locks, Combat publishes commitments; input ORs them into `body_busy`. A pending interact waits inside its window until the commitment ends — the body finishes what it signed before signing anew. **Derivation law (review fix, Annex EX-4; snapshot-scoped per review ruling D-3, 2026-11-11 — amends the prior review's max-over-all mandate, recorded in the review log):** `INTERACT_WINDOW_MS` is **derived, not tuned** — each body-busy publisher declares `worst_case_arc_ms` at registration (**owner: SYS-08 + SYS-06 jointly**); the window is evaluated **per press, at its stamp tick** (the body snapshot is taken at the stamp tick): `INTERACT_WINDOW_MS(press) = max(declared arcs of publishers busy in the stamp-tick snapshot) + INTERACT_MARGIN_MS` when the body is busy at press — sized to cover *the arc the player is actually inside*; `INTENT_WINDOW_MS` when the body is free at press (the order stands on its own breath). Cap: the busy-case value never exceeds max-over-all-declared-arcs + margin (a subset bound, by construction). **Degenerate cases (review fix):** empty registered set (the guaranteed early-boot state) or no declared arcs → fall back to `INTENT_WINDOW_MS` (fail-open keeps the verb alive); declared arcs are validated inputs — dev assert + clamp to [17, 5000] ms (one clamp GDD-wide, B14 — ≥ 1 tick @ 60 tps) + the production telemetry pattern on invalid declarations; **pending intents are grandfathered at their stamp-time window** — re-derivation never shortens a live order (mirrors F-01's rate-change care). **Residual (documented, Annex EX-4):** a commitment *starting after* the press is not covered — an idle-press order stands on its own breath and may expire; re-press is the honest remedy. The TR-INP-029 promise ("mid-swing tap → terminal opens at recovery end") is now *structural*. **Production watchdog:** dev — **a "silent publisher" = a registered body-busy publisher whose most recent publication is older than one `logic_tick` while its lock/commitment state claims busy (R4: instance-scoped — the watchdog keys on the specific publisher instance, not the global OR)** = loud assertion; production — behavior unchanged (fail-open **is** the shipping behavior) + a rate-limited (≤ 1/min) telemetry event naming the publisher instance. **Stuck-true watchdog (review fix; instance-scoped per R4):** a specific lock instance asserted longer than its declared `worst_case_arc_ms` + `INTERACT_MARGIN_MS` is by definition a publisher bug — the declared-arc infrastructure is the yardstick: dev assertion + production rate-limited telemetry (the only failure mode that permanently kills verbs; "no input timeouts" makes detection mandatory). *Test: tap Interact mid-swing beside a cache → opens at recovery end; tap outside the window → nothing.*
- **TR-INP-075 — Pull-and-hold custody (all verbs; Annex EX-5).** The ack transfers custody. The puller owns the honored intent through its own arc; input neither re-offers a consumed slot nor retains a copy, and a pulled order that dies mid-arc (gate entry) dies with the arc — it never re-enters an input buffer. Coverage: **Attack** — held across the Combat arc (SYS-08's ruling); **Jump, Dash, Interact, Quick-use** — execute-at-pull, no custody gap; a future pull-then-execute-later design inherits this rule automatically. *Test: a pulled attack intent survives a Combat-mock 300-tick arc and resolves at the mock's chosen execution frame; input-side records show the slot cleared at the ack tick.*
- **TR-INP-030 — Interact means the lit bubble.** Interact enters its window **only when a prompt is lit**; presses with no target expire immediately (ratified — no walk-by accidental saves). *Test: press Interact in an empty corridor → nothing; a later-lit bubble does not consume the stale press.*

#### 7 · The touch surface contract (the dp law)

- **TR-INP-059 — Two canvases, one truth (re-baselined per D1, re-review #5 — reverses ruling R-2).** The world renders in a **SubViewport at a safe-derived integer scale** (`k`, F-06); root stretch is **disabled**; the touch/HUD layer renders at **native resolution** in dp semantics — the dp→px conversion is direct (`× screen_density`), no stretch-model bridge exists. Hit-rect floors: `hit_px = max(visual_dp, dp_floor × touch_hit_scale) × screen_density` (F-06's form — one notation GDD-wide) — the dp floor is exact in native pixels; hit rects are ceil-rounded to integer px, like the safe-area insets (two claims, stated separately — R10, re-review #7). Target sizes in dp/mm, never art pixels. **Two named rects (the clamp targets):** `ART_VIEWPORT` (the 480k×270k world SubViewport's on-screen band, centered in the safe rect per F-06) and `SCREEN_SAFE_RECT` (screen minus system insets). Interactive HUD regions (stick zone, cluster, chip, pause, a11y sockets) clamp into **SCREEN_SAFE_RECT** on the native HUD layer — they may occupy the non-band area where the layout needs room; world-layer taps bind only within ART_VIEWPORT (bars/crop edges = dead world taps, TR-INP-063); the Interact bubble is world-anchored (inside ART_VIEWPORT) with cross-rect clearances to the HUD layer (TR-INP-067). **The render mechanism (SubViewport wiring, root-stretch disable, screen→art transform) is the Input Architecture ADR's; engine-verification item 8 verifies the model end-to-end.** *Test: hit circles ≥ dp floors at every device scale (exact in native px); no HUD hit rect crosses a system inset; no world tap binds on a bar.*
- **TR-INP-060 — Touchdown binding.** Each touch binds to exactly one region at landing (movement zone / one button / one chip / one bubble / dead); no mid-touch rebind; release unbinds. Stick finger + chip/bubble finger work simultaneously. *Test: two-finger stick + attack tap.*
- **TR-INP-061 — Clock split.** The intent window ticks on the **game clock** (TR-INP-024); every other touch feel timer (fades, hold-guards, hysteresis) runs on **wall-clock** — UI-feel is not gameplay intent, and frame-rate independence is the glitch doctrine.
- **TR-INP-062 — Impulse vs state (touch-facing taxonomy).** Impulse verbs (Attack, Jump, Dash, Interact, Quick-use) are single signatures through the intent window; state verbs (Move, Crouch) are held orders with no buffer — honored while held, never queued.
- **TR-INP-063 — Letterbox bars are dead world-taps (rewritten — review fix, landscape-primary per EX-10/D-1; both-axis bars 2026-11-11).** With landscape-primary, the 16:9 art band is centered in the safe rect. HUD controls (stick zone, cluster, chip, pause, a11y sockets) live on the dp canvas (TR-INP-059's `SCREEN_SAFE_RECT`) and may sit over a bar; they never bind world taps there. The **world-anchored Interact bubble** is the exception: it stays inside `ART_VIEWPORT` and never straddles a bar's world-area edge. On screens whose safe rect is smaller than 480×270 (the crop case), the crop edge plays the bar's role (Edge Cases). Predictable over clever. *Test: on a 20:9 landscape phone, taps on both side bars bind dead; on a 16:10 tablet and a 4:3 iPad, taps on both top/bottom bars bind dead; on a 16:9 screen with no bars, taps at the safe edge bind normally; the bottom-anchored cluster binds normally.*

## Formulas

The Input system is a foundation layer: its formulas are **conversion laws** (human ms → engine ticks), **band/threshold math** (analog noise → discrete orders), and **timing predicates** (when a promise is alive, honored, or silently dropped). Three laws govern every formula:

- **Named-symbol law.** Every literal (140, 280, 150, 90, 0.25, 0.45, …) appears here only as a named symbol; values live in exactly one place — the Tuning Knobs table. This section defines *structure*; that section defines *numbers*.
- **Never-stingy boundary law (ratified GDD-wide).** Every boundary comparison is **inclusive on entry, strict on exit**: an order engages at `≥`/`≤` the threshold; leaving requires strictly beyond the exit counterpart.
- **Clock law.** Gameplay timing (intent windows, backdash tap window) runs on the **game clock**; touch-feel timing (hold-guards, fades, chip hold measurement) runs on **wall-clock** (TR-INP-061, TR-INP-024). Where they meet (F-09 → F-05), the handoff is explicit.

Data chain: raw touch → **F-02** normalization → **F-03** bands / **F-04** crouch cone; F-03's edge stream → **F-07** backdash; presses → **F-05** windows, gated by **F-08**/**F-10**; **F-09** produces a press into F-05; **F-06** is the independent layout-conversion law feeding all touch geometry.

**Formula ledger — law vs knob.** No entry is a bare tuning constant; each defines structure, and each constant it consumes is flagged to Tuning Knobs.

#### F-01 · ms_to_ticks — the window conversion law (TR-INP-024)

`window_ticks = ceil(window_ms × physics_tps ÷ 1000)`

**Variables:**

| Variable | Symbol | Type | Range | Description |
|----------|--------|------|-------|-------------|
| Design duration | `window_ms` | int | 1–5000 | Contract duration in ms — `INTENT_WINDOW_MS` or a per-verb override |
| Physics rate | `physics_tps` | int | 1–1000 (project pins 60) | Engine physics ticks per second |
| Window length | `window_ticks` | int | ≥ 1 (practical ≤ ~500) | Ticks the intent stays offerable |

**Output Range:** `window_ticks ≥ 1`; effective duration is **always ≥ window_ms** — the window never rounds stingy. Overshoot ≤ 1 tick (16.7 ms @ 60, 8.3 @ 120, 33.3 @ 30). Exact-integer products round with zero overshoot.

**Rate-scaling rule:** `window_ms` is the contract; ticks are derived. Higher physics rates shrink overshoot toward the exact value — fidelity improves, the promise never shrinks below it. **Integer form (R1):** `window_ticks = (window_ms × physics_tps + 999) div 1000` — the same ceiling in exact integer arithmetic (no float-ceil drift); tests assert equivalence with the float form. On `physics_tps` change mid-session: recompute all `window_ticks` from ms and re-baseline pending ages through ms so no intent expires early or overstays from the rate event alone. **Boundary honesty (re-review #4 note):** an intent pending exactly at the old boundary when the rate rises can still expire inside the overshoot band (150 ms contract > new 141.7 ms effective) — the re-baseline honors the **ms contract**, not the old derived overshoot; the contract ms is the promise, the overshoot is rate-derived texture.

| physics_tps | ticks from 140 ms | effective window |
|---|---|---|
| 30 | 5 | 166.7 ms |
| 60 | **9** | 150.0 ms |
| 120 | 17 | 141.7 ms |
| 240 | 34 | 141.7 ms |

**Example:** 140 ms @ 60 tps → `ceil(8.4)` = **9 ticks** = 150 ms effective (the ratified value). At 120 tps: 17 ticks = 141.7 ms — same promise, finer granularity.

#### F-02 · stick_vector_normalization (TR-INP-014, TR-INP-009)

The stick_vector_normalization formula is defined as:
`stick_vector = (raw_offset ÷ |raw_offset|) × stick_magnitude`, where
`stick_magnitude = 0` if `|raw_offset| ≤ D_src`; otherwise `min(|raw_offset| ÷ L_src, 1)`; and `(0, 0)` when `|raw_offset| = 0`.

**Per-source parameters (review fix — the unit correction):**
- **Touch:** `raw_offset` in dp; `L_src = STICK_LEASH` (dp); `D_src = STICK_DEADZONE` (dp).
- **Gamepad stick:** `raw_offset` = the axis vector, already unit-normalized; `L_src = 1.0` (identity); `D_src = PAD_DEADZONE_RATIO` (**ratio, not dp** — applying a dp deadzone to a unit vector was the unit bug this fixes).
- **Keyboard/d-pad:** boolean sources deliver ±1.0 directly, bypassing F-02.

**InputMap deadzones are forced to 0 on every registered action** (TR-INP-014 amendment): F-02 is the **sole deadzone authority** — one implementation, one test seam, and injected `action_press(strength)` behaves identically to hardware. **Axis-sourced button bindings (review fix):** on pad families where Dash's binding lands on a trigger axis (RB/R2 as JoypadMotion axes in Godot's standard mapping), the button decision is thresholded at the source-filter layer (0.5 with hysteresis) while the InputMap deadzone stays 0 — the F-02 noise story covers the stick path; axis-buttons get the comparator path (rides engine-verification item 6's pad-family normalization).

**Variables:**

| Variable | Symbol | Type | Range | Description |
|----------|--------|------|-------|-------------|
| Raw offset | `raw_offset` | Vector2 | unbounded | Touch: dp (px → dp via `screen_density` — the native-resolution HUD makes the conversion direct; no stretch-model bridge exists). Gamepad: axis units (unit-normalized) |
| Leash radius | `L_src` | float | > 0 | Touch: `STICK_LEASH` (dp). Gamepad: 1.0 (identity) |
| Noise floor | `D_src` | float | ≥ 0 | Touch: `STICK_DEADZONE` (dp). Gamepad: `PAD_DEADZONE_RATIO` (ratio) |
| Stick vector | `stick_vector` | Vector2 | [−1, 1]² | Normalized output; feeds F-03 (x) and F-04 (x, y) |

**Output Range:** magnitude exactly [0, 1]; pinned at exactly 1.0 at/over the leash (a flick saturates); exactly (0, 0) inside the deadzone. **Past the leash (R6):** the vector pins at 1.0 and the stick base holds position (no base-chase) until release — the anchor re-homes only at the next touchdown (SYS-24 renders the base). No smoothing, no curve — geometry, not feel; ramps are Movement's. The **pre-normalization raw trace** is the SYS-12 gesture channel; gestures read raw, orders read normalized.

**Example:** leash 56 dp; finger drags 72 dp right → pinned **1.0**, full walk. Finger at 19 dp → 0.34 → creep band (F-03). Finger at 3 dp (deadzone 4 dp) → **0** → neutral.

#### F-03 · movement_band_selection — one axis, three orders (TR-INP-065)

`move_order = sign(x) × band_multiplier(band)`, with **two independent hysteretic comparators** on `|x|`:

```
creep_on: OFF→ON when |x| ≥ BAND_CREEP_ENTER (0.25)   ON→OFF when |x| < BAND_CREEP_ENTER − BAND_HYST (0.20)
walk_on:  OFF→ON when |x| ≥ BAND_WALK_ENTER  (0.45)   ON→OFF when |x| < BAND_WALK_ENTER  − BAND_HYST (0.40)
band      = WALK if walk_on · CREEP if creep_on · NEUTRAL otherwise
multiplier= 0 · CREEP_RATIO (0.5) × walk · 1.0
```

**Variables:**

| Variable | Symbol | Type | Range | Description |
|----------|--------|------|-------|-------------|
| Axis sample | `x` | float | [−1, 1] | `stick_vector.x` (touch/gamepad); keyboard & d-pad report ±1.0 directly |
| Comparator states | `creep_on`, `walk_on` | bool | {true, false} | Persistent — the hysteresis memory |
| Band | `band` | enum | {NEUTRAL, CREEP, WALK} | Selected order |
| Published order | `move_order` | float | {−1.0, −0.5, 0, 0.5, 1.0} | Sign × multiplier; Movement maps to speed with its own ramps |

**Output Range:** five discrete values. Nesting invariant: `walk_on ⇒ creep_on` (ordered thresholds and ordered exits) — assert in tests. Sticky no-chatter zones: [0.20, 0.25) and [0.40, 0.45). Sign is read fresh every sample; magnitude band is sticky. Keyboard at MVP presses ±1.0 → always WALK or NEUTRAL (walk-only MVP, ratified).

**Delivery recommendation (→ ADR):** SYS-01 publishes **(raw_axis, move_order)** — raw for the audit/trace channel, order as the contract; band *selection* is SYS-01's (TR-INP-065), speed execution is SYS-06's.

**Example:** settle at x = 0.30 → creep engages → 0.5× walk. Drift to 0.22 → still CREEP (exit needs < 0.20). To 0.19 → NEUTRAL. Push 0.47 → WALK. Slide 0.42 → still WALK. 0.39 → CREEP. No sequence oscillates bands while the thumb holds position.

#### F-04 · crouch_zone_test — the down-zone cone (TR-INP-066, TR-INP-061, TR-INP-029)

`in_entry = (angle_from_down ≤ CROUCH_ENTRY_ANGLE) ∧ (magnitude ≥ CROUCH_ENTRY_MAG)`
`in_exit  = (angle_from_down ≤ CROUCH_EXIT_ANGLE)  ∧ (magnitude ≥ CROUCH_EXIT_MAG)`
`angle_from_down = atan2(|stick_vector.x|, stick_vector.y)` degrees, screen-space y-down (0° = straight down).

State machine (analog sources; keyboard/d-pad-down deliver a boolean directly, bypassing cone and guard):

```
published_crouch = latch ∨ held_zone          (raw, every tick — TR-INP-013)
held_zone:  STANDING → CROUCHING:  in_entry sustained ≥ CROUCH_HOLD_GUARD_MS (wall-clock accumulator)
            CROUCHING → STANDING:  ¬in_exit   (unguarded — ratified: the asymmetry IS the design)
accumulator: += Δwall while in_entry ∧ context = Gameplay; resets on any out-of-entry sample
             or at gate entry (TR-INP-047)
latch: toggle mode — set/cleared by the confirmed-tap gesture (touch) or a button press (KB/pad;
       each press toggles); both modes — set/cleared by the pause action (D3);
       Jump clears it in both modes (TR-INP-066)
```

**Publication law (B2, re-review #5 — input's deferred-during-commit rule deleted):** the published channel is `latch ∨ held_zone`, evaluated and delivered **every tick, raw and immediately** — states never wait (TR-INP-013). Whether the body *applies* the posture while committed is a **SYS-06 handoff** (the Movement GDD owns posture application during commitments); input takes no side and freezes nothing. **Lift judgment:** the lift is judged on the **last in-contact sample** — F-02 reports (0,0) once the finger lifts, so the post-lift sample is never the lift's truth.

**Variables:**

| Variable | Symbol | Type | Range | Description |
|----------|--------|------|-------|-------------|
| Stick vector | `stick_vector` | Vector2 | [−1, 1]² | From F-02, unsmoothed |
| Angle from down | `angle_from_down` | float | 0°–180° | Symmetric down-cone (both diagonals count) |
| Magnitude | `magnitude` | float | 0–1 | From F-02 |
| Guard accumulator | `guard_ms` | float | 0–∞ | Sustained-in-entry time; resets on any out-of-entry sample |
| Latch | `latch` | bool | {true, false} | Toggle-mode latch (confirmed-tap / button press, each toggles) + both-modes pause-action latch (D3); Jump clears in both modes (TR-INP-066) |
| Crouch state | `crouch_engaged` | bool | {true, false} | **Published state channel — `latch ∨ held_zone`, raw every tick** — never buffered, never commitment-frozen (B2) |

**Output Range:** boolean state (`latch ∨ held_zone`) + internal accumulator. Stick released → the held-zone half stands; a latch stays latched until cleared (toggle mode: the toggle gesture or a button press; both modes: the pause action — D3; Jump clears in both modes — R1 scoping, re-review #7: the unscoped list read as any-mode setters). Brush < `CROUCH_HOLD_GUARD_MS` never engages the zone half (inclusive at exactly the guard); the 25°→45° / 0.65→0.40 dual thresholds **are** the hysteresis — crouch-walking on diagonals never pops her up.

**Example:** vector (0.30, 0.95) → 17.5°, 0.996 → in entry; held 90 ms → crouch. Drift to (0.52, 0.85): 31.4° → outside entry (31.4 > 25) but inside exit (31.4 ≤ 45) → **stays crouched** — diagonal crouch-walk. Drift to (0.80, 0.60): 53.1° > 45 → the zone half stands. **Worked trace (B2 — committed lift):** confirmed-tap lift lands at tick T while an attack commitment runs → the published stream flips to `crouching=true` **at tick T** (raw, no deferral); a Combat cancel-window pull at any tick ≥ T reads crouching; the SYS-06 mock applies the posture on the first body-free tick (the Movement handoff).

#### F-05 · intent_window_evaluation — the breath (TR-INP-018…024, TR-INP-028)

`intent_pending = (current_game_tick − press_tick ≤ window_ticks) ∧ ¬consumed ∧ (context = GAMEPLAY)`
with `window_ticks = ms_to_ticks(override_map.get(verb, INTENT_WINDOW_MS))` (F-01) and the supersede rule:
`on new press of same verb (pending or consumed): press_tick ← current_game_tick; snapshot ← new; consumed ← false` (a new press always opens a new window; one slot per verb forever; age restarts — Latest Order Honored).

**Variables:**

| Variable | Symbol | Type | Range | Description |
|----------|--------|------|-------|-------------|
| Game clock now | `current_game_tick` | int | 0–∞ | **`logic_tick`** counter; freezes under hit-stop, stops in non-Gameplay contexts (TR-INP-076) |
| Press stamp | `press_tick` | int | 0–∞ | **`logic_tick`** at the stamp boundary; the stamp-tick body snapshot attaches (TR-INP-019) |
| Window | `window_ticks` | int | ≥ 1 | From F-01; per-verb override map |
| Consumed flag | `consumed` | bool | {true, false} | Set by puller ack (TR-INP-020); cleared by the next press (new window); permanent for the current intent instance |
| Pending | `intent_pending` | bool | {true, false} | Offered to the single puller every tick while true |

**Output Range:** boolean per verb slot (exactly one slot per verb). Boundary: `age = window_ticks` is **still pending** (honored on its last eligible tick — never-stingy); `age = window_ticks + 1` → silent expiry (TR-INP-023). Offer begins on the stamp tick itself — same-tick resolution possible (CONTINGENT on the ADR pipeline order — OQ#1). **Lazy expiry, one reading picked (review fix):** expiry IS the predicate evaluation at offer time — an over-age intent is **never offered**; the choreography's "expiry" phase is slot-reset bookkeeping only. One-consumption per TR-INP-021.

**Supersede rule (corrected):** on new press of same verb (pending or consumed): `press_tick ← current_game_tick`; snapshot ← new; `consumed ← false` (a new press always opens a new window; age restarts — Latest Order Honored). Corollaries: **G1 count / G2 freshness / G3 keep-alive** — see TR-INP-022; a gap > window between taps leaves the slot momentarily empty (**pending-gap property** — the retracted stall-parity claim's root cause).

**Interact override (review fix — derived, not tuned; snapshot-scoped per D-3 ruling 2026-11-11):** the override map's `INTERACT_WINDOW_MS` entry is **evaluated per press at its stamp tick** — max(declared arcs of publishers busy in the stamp-tick snapshot) + `INTERACT_MARGIN_MS` when the body is busy at press, else `INTENT_WINDOW_MS` (TR-INP-029); SYS-08 and SYS-06 jointly own the declarations; pending intents are grandfathered at their stamp-time window. The ratified promise is now structural (U-4 closes).

**Example (the Early Press):** Attack pressed at tick 2000 during a windup → pending ticks 2000–2009. A second tap at 2004 supersedes → pending to 2013, still one slot. Combat pulls at tick 2010 (strike-contact): age 6 ≤ 9 → **honored**; ack → consumed. No pull by 2013 → tick 2014 drops it silently.

#### F-06 · hit_rect_floor — the dp law's arithmetic (TR-INP-059, TR-INP-070, TR-INP-063) — re-baselined per D1

`hit_px = max(visual_dp, dp_floor × touch_hit_scale) × screen_density` — the native-resolution HUD makes the dp floor a direct px computation — where `integer_scale k = max(1, min(floor(safe_w ÷ 480), floor(safe_h ÷ 270)))` **sizes the world SubViewport: 480k × 270k px, centered in the safe rect** (D1 render model, re-review #5 — root stretch disabled; the ADR wires the SubViewport and owns the screen→art transform; the art band is cropped when the safe rect is smaller than 480×270, so the safe-area clamp never displaces a registered region).

**Variables:**

| Variable | Symbol | Type | Range | Description |
|----------|--------|------|-------|-------------|
| Art scale | `k` | int | ≥ 1 | Integer scale of the **world SubViewport** (D1 model): art viewport = 480k × 270k physical px, centered **in the safe rect**; letterbox bars are dead world-taps (TR-INP-063); orientation-dependent — audits run in the locked (landscape) orientation |
| Screen density | `screen_density` | float | ≥ 0.1 | OS density (physical px per dp); the single dp↔px bridge for the native-resolution HUD |
| Visual size | `visual_dp` | float | > 0 | Drawn extent in dp |
| Accessibility floor | `dp_floor` | float | 44–72 | 48 gameplay / 44 absolute / 56 bubble |
| Hit-only scale | `touch_hit_scale` | float | {1.0, 1.25, 1.5} | Player a11y setting |
| Hit rect | `hit_px` | float | ≥ floor-equivalent | Region rect for touchdown binding (native px) |

**Output Range:** `hit_px ≥ (dp_floor × touch_hit_scale × screen_density)` always. Identity: `hit_px ÷ screen_density = max(visual_dp, dp_floor × touch_hit_scale)` — **the floor is guaranteed in dp at every device scale, exact in native px**. A hit rect larger than visual is legal and intended; smaller is a bug.

**Example (landscape frame — the shipping orientation, EX-10; landscape floors sides 59 / bottom 48 / top 0, ceil-rounded to px):** phone 2400×1080 px, density 2.75 → floor insets ceil-rounded: 59 dp × 2.75 = 162.25 → **163 px** per side, 48 dp × 2.75 = 132 px exact → safe width 2400 − 2×163 = 2074 px, safe height 1080 − 132 = 948 px → `k = min(floor(2074 ÷ 480), floor(948 ÷ 270)) = min(4, 3) = 3` → the world SubViewport is 1440 × 810 px, centered in the safe rect. HUD native-resolution: Attack drawn 60 dp = 60 × 2.75 = **165 px**; floor 48 × 2.75 = 132 px → `hit = max(165, 132)` = 165 px = **60 dp** ✓. World taps map screen→art by the ADR's transform (bounds-test then floor division — ADR raw material), verified end-to-end by engine-verification item 8. *(The full per-profile matrix arithmetic lives in the QA plan's layout ledger — altitude law, re-review #5.)*

#### F-07 · backdash_pattern_recognition (TR-INP-056) — clock: game clock (ratified)

`backdash_fires = (press2_tick − press1_tick ≤ tap_window_ticks) ∧ release1_observed_strictly_between ∧ body_available(completion_tick)`
evaluated at the press2 edge; `tap_window_ticks = ms_to_ticks(BACKDASH_TAP_WINDOW_MS)` (F-01).

**Edge definitions (rig-blind — every rig compiles to the same edge stream; review fix; direction model per D4, re-review #5 — amends R-13):**
- **Back edge (arming)** = entering the **arming zone** — by default the WALK band (F-03; one definition of "back is pressed"; keyboard/d-pad binding press = edge directly). The **chain's first entry (press1) must be a true back entry: `entry_sign = −facing` at press1** — the check reads the facing **published as of press1's tick** (publication cadence is the ADR's facing-publication rider); a forward entry (`entry_sign = facing`) never arms, so a forward double-tap never fires. Later entries are classified against the **chain's latched `entry_sign`**: same-sign continues (the sustained retreat arms normally after the press1-induced turn); opposite-sign resets. **If the evidence-gated `BACKDASH_ARM_MAG` fallback is promoted (AC-64), the arming zone decouples from the walk band** (pairwise-validated, V12/V16).
- **Release edge** = magnitude strictly **below the arming release threshold** (with the default arming zone this is exactly the walk-band exit, < 0.40; promoted fallback: `BACKDASH_ARM_RELEASE` = ARM − 0.05, V17).
- **Reset condition (re-keyed per D4):** an entry into the active arming zone with the sign **opposite the chain's `entry_sign`** between press1 and press2 (a direction change mid-pattern), **an in-band sign flip** (crossing zero without dropping below the release threshold — ruled release + opposite entry, and the opposite entry resets), or a `facing` flip **after press1 other than the press1-induced flip**, **resets the machine to IDLE**; a late press2 **re-arms as a new press1 inheriting the chain's `entry_sign`** (classification never re-keys mid-chain — re-arm and reset no longer claim the same event); **an opposing-key edge** (the other direction's binding between presses) is an opposite-sign entry → reset; **a rig-transfer edge** is an ordinary entry edge of the new source in the rig-blind stream (same-sign continues, opposite-sign resets); creep-magnitude wobble between edges is ignored (below the arming threshold = no committed direction).

Machine: `IDLE → BACK1 (back edge) → RELEASED (release edge) → fire-or-drop at back edge → IDLE`. Release must be **strictly** between the presses (same-tick release+press fails the ordering and restarts). Held back produces one edge, then nothing — *held back = walk, never backdash*.

**Body-free completion:** fire only if `body_available` at the completion tick — else **dropped at MVP, not deferred** (the Combat/Movement defensive-family ruling D-5 may amend to short-defer by recorded ruling — EX-1). The published intent is a **single-tick offer carrying `(entry_sign)`**: **hop direction = `entry_sign`** (away from the threat she faced at press1); **SYS-06 sets facing = −`entry_sign` during the hop**; SYS-06 pulls the same tick (hop starts on the completion frame) or it vanishes — no window slot, reflex-class. The a11y direct source publishes the same payload with `entry_sign = −facing` at the press tick.

**Variables:**

| Variable | Symbol | Type | Range | Description |
|----------|--------|------|-------|-------------|
| First back press | `press1_tick` | int | `logic_tick` | Arms the detector |
| Release | `release1` | bool | {observed, not} | Must occur strictly between presses |
| Second back press | `press2_tick` | int | `logic_tick` | Completion edge — the pattern dies or fires here |
| Tap window | `tap_window_ticks` | int | ≥ 1 | From F-01 (280 ms default) |
| Facing | `facing` | enum | {left, right} | **Published by SYS-06** (new contract item, ratified — registry candidate); read at press1 for the **true-back check (`entry_sign = −facing`) and flip-attribution only** — hop direction no longer derives from it (D4) |
| Entry sign | `entry_sign` | enum | {−1, +1} | Press1's entry sign — defines "back" for the whole pattern **and the hop direction**; late re-arms inherit the chain's value; carried in the intent payload |
| Fire | `backdash_fires` | bool | pulse | Single-tick publish carrying `(entry_sign)`; consumed or vanished |

**Output Range:** boolean pulse (at most one per completion) + internal 3-state machine. `press2 − press1 = tap_window_ticks` exactly → honored (inclusive). A late press2 **re-arms as a new press1 inheriting the chain's `entry_sign`** — attempts chain naturally. Post-fire → IDLE; re-edges during the hop hit the body-free rule → dropped; no input-side cooldown. Detector state clears at gate entry.

**Worked traces (D4):** (a) *standstill back* — facing right, flick left → press1 entry_sign = −1 = −facing ✓ arms; completes → hop left, facing set to +1 during the hop. (b) *sustained retreat with a late press2* — press1 arms (entry_sign −1), she turns and keeps flicking left (now forward-relative-to-body, same sign); a late press2 beyond the window re-arms inheriting entry_sign −1; the next in-window completion hops left — away from the enemy, matching the stick. (c) *forward tap-walk* — facing right, D-D taps: press1 entry_sign = +1 = facing → never arms; nothing ever fires.

**Example:** @ 60 tps (window 17 ticks = 283.3 ms). Facing right; flick left → press1 @ 5000: entry_sign = −1 = −facing ✓ true back entry, chain latches −1; release @ 5010; press2 @ 5050 → 50 ticks apart → invalid; **re-arms (BACK1 @ 5050, inheriting entry_sign −1)**. Release @ 5062, press2 @ 5064 → 14 ticks = 233 ms ✓, release strictly between ✓, body free → **fires at 5064 carrying entry_sign −1 → the hop goes LEFT (with the stick, away from the threat), and SYS-06 sets facing = +1 during the hop**. Had the body been mid-attack: nothing, silently. Forward trace: D-D taps while facing right → press1 entry_sign = +1 = facing → the machine never arms.

#### F-08 · body_busy — the availability composition (TR-INP-029)

`body_busy(t) = (∨ movement_locks)(t) ∨ (∨ combat_commitments)(t)`; `body_available(t) = ¬body_busy(t)`

**Variables:**

| Variable | Symbol | Type | Range | Description |
|----------|--------|------|-------|-------------|
| Movement locks | `movement_locks` | set[bool] | any | SYS-06's published locomotion locks (committed movement, hop, knockdown, …) |
| Combat commitments | `combat_commitments` | set[bool] | any | SYS-08's published commitments (startup/active/recovery, clash lock, …) |
| Availability | `body_available` | bool | {true, false} | Input-owned OR; consumed by F-07 (completion), F-10, item resolution — F-04 takes no body gate (B2: publication is raw; posture application is SYS-06's handoff) |

**Output Range:** boolean. Evaluated **fresh every `logic_tick`** — a state, never buffered; the stamp-tick snapshot records it at the stamp boundary while consumption-frame state governs derived moves (input delivers both, never arbitrates). **Fails open (ratified):** empty/missing publication = available (OR of nothing = false), with a loud dev-mode assertion when a registered publisher goes silent — fail-closed would silently kill verbs during development.

**Example:** tick 4000: locks = {}, commitments = {attack} → busy → a pending interact keeps waiting. #### F-09 · chip_commit_rule — spend weight (TR-INP-068, TR-INP-061)

`chip_committed = release_observed ∧ ¬slid_off ∧ (release_wall_ms − touchdown_wall_ms ≥ commit_threshold_ms)` — where `commit_threshold_ms = CHIP_TAP_COMMIT_MS` when the `CHIP_TAP_COMMIT` a11y toggle is on, else `CHIP_HOLD_COMMIT_MS` — and `release_observed` = finger lift **while still bound** (binding-based, position-independent across region re-registration — review fix: after rotation/density re-registration a valid hold stays committable; the world moved, not the order)

**Variables:**

| Variable | Symbol | Type | Range | Description |
|----------|--------|------|-------|-------------|
| Touchdown time | `touchdown_wall_ms` | int | monotonic wall ms | Chip region touchdown |
| Release time | `release_wall_ms` | int | ≥ touchdown | Finger lift **while still bound** (binding-based — survives region re-registration) |
| Commit threshold | `commit_threshold_ms` | int | ≥ 1 | `CHIP_HOLD_COMMIT_MS` (150 default) or `CHIP_TAP_COMMIT_MS` (50) when the a11y toggle is on |
| Slide-off flag | `slid_off` | bool | {true, false} | Finger exited chip bounds while held → set **at the exit frame** (cancel fires then, free — ratified: slide-off dominates hold duration) |
| Commit | `chip_committed` | bool | pulse | On true: a `quick_item` press is stamped into the F-05 window at the commit tick (`logic_tick`) |

**Output Range:** boolean pulse. Boundary: exactly `commit_threshold_ms` → commit (inclusive); one tenth below → no order. Keyboard `Q` / gamepad `LB` skip this formula entirely — their press **is** the `quick_item` press at press-tick (the hold is the touch rig's confirmation weight, TR-INP-008 fixed semantics).

**Handoff semantics (where the clocks meet):** the hold is *measured* on wall-clock (touch-feel); the resulting press enters the F-05 window **stamped at the commit tick on the game clock**. The window does not retroactively start at touchdown. An app blur mid-hold cancels silently — no commit on return.

**Example:** touchdown wall 5023 ms, release wall 5180 → 157 ≥ 150 → commit at `logic_tick` 9000: `quick_item` pending ticks 9000–9009. Touch-and-lift in 80 ms → no order, preview fades, item unconsumed — always.

#### F-10 · interact_target_binding (TR-INP-030, TR-INP-067)

The interact_target_binding formula is defined as:
`interact_resolvable = intent_pending(interact) ∧ prompt_lit ∧ (lit_prompt_id = bound_prompt_id) ∧ ¬signed(bound_prompt_id)`

**The signed latch (review fix):** a prompt that has consumed a press latches **signed** while it stays lit; further presses on the same lit prompt are unresolvable at entry — one lit bubble, one signature per lighting. The latch clears on unlight or context change (gate entry).

**Variables:**

| Variable | Symbol | Type | Range | Description |
|----------|--------|------|-------|-------------|
| Pending intent | `intent_pending` | bool | — | From F-05 (interact slot) |
| Prompt lit | `prompt_lit` | bool | {true, false} | Exactly one prompt at a time (nearest wins) |
| Bound prompt | `bound_prompt_id` | id | registry ids | The prompt lit **at the press's stamp tick** — the press binds to it |
| Lit prompt | `lit_prompt_id` | id | registry ids | The prompt lit now |

**Output Range:** boolean. The intent is valid **only for the bubble it was pressed on**. Any term failing → the intent clears **immediately** (not at window end): prompt unlights mid-window (walked away) → expired at once; a later-lit bubble never consumes a stale press. Rig-agnostic: keyboard `E` in an empty corridor expires on the stamp tick.

**Example:** `E` at tick 300 with the CACHE bubble lit (bound = cache_A), window 9. Player steps back; prompt unlights at tick 305 → cleared immediately. At tick 320 a nearer door lights: nothing pending → no fire, no accidental save.

## Edge Cases

**Amendments ratified during edge-case derivation** (they amend Detailed Design): **TR-INP-071 Cross-rig state arbitration (rewritten — review fix: edge-driven; assertion semantics re-fixed 2026-11-11).** Events deliver honestly from any rig into the same slots. Each state channel (`move_x`, crouch) has exactly one owning rig. **Transfer happens on positive edges only** — a rig takes a channel on its activating edge (touchdown in the zone / key-down / stick crossing into a non-neutral band); continuous position alone never transfers ownership. **Re-assertion cadence (review fix — active republish, not event receipt):** **assertion = the dispatcher actively re-publishing the tracked channel value every tick** from its tracked source state (touch: the live touch vector; KB/pad: the per-tick poll of held inputs) — a stationary held thumb re-asserts every tick, because holding is a state, not an event stream (`InputEventScreenDrag` fires only on movement). **Publish neutral on the first missed assertion** — raw truth, no stale-value tail (TR-INP-014's no-grace law); **two consecutive missed assertions clear ownership only** (device removal, focus loss, phantom-touch end per TR-INP-073's rider). A released channel is taken only by a later edge — never by silence. **TR-INP-072 Movement-zone single-owner** — the first touchdown owns the stick until release; a second finger in the zone is inert. **TR-INP-073 Touch-cancel semantics** — an OS-cancelled touch (system gesture, app switch) is never a signed order: a chip hold mid-cancel cannot commit, the stick releases, the crouch guard resets. **Fallback (rider):** if a device profile under-delivers OS cancels, that profile disposes all touch-originated held states on every visibility change + a telemetry counter for suspected phantoms; per-platform cancel delivery is verified in device QA (OQ#15). **TR-INP-074 Knob validation (rewritten — review fix: pairwise law).** Every knob value AND every knob pair is validated at config load, before any region or service registers. **Dev builds: violation = boot failure naming both knobs and the invariant. Production: the listed resolution applies + rate-limited telemetry naming the clamp** (a shipping build must never crash from config; a broken pair must never drift silently). The exhaustive pair table (V0–V21) lives in the Tuning Knobs preamble. `[TR-INP-*]` = owning rule(s); `F-xx` = the formula doing the work.

### Value & config extremes

- **If `physics_tps` changes mid-session**: all windows recompute from their ms contracts and pending ages re-baseline through ms — no intent expires early or overstays from the rate event alone. Full rate-scaling rule is F-01's. [TR-INP-024 · F-01]
- **If any timing/geometry knob is set out of range** (window override ≤ 0, `STICK_LEASH` ≤ 0, `STICK_DEADZONE ≥ STICK_LEASH`): clamped at registration — overrides floor at 1 ms (⇒ 1 tick, never-stingy), leash clamps to its 40 dp safe-range floor (the formula separately asserts `L_src > 0`), deadzone clamps to ≤ 10% of leash. Publisher-declared arcs clamp to [17, 5000] ms (≥ 1 tick — a 1 ms declaration is nonsense and cannot keep TR-INP-029's promise). [TR-INP-018, TR-INP-014, TR-INP-009 · F-01, F-02 · TR-INP-074]
- **If the safe area is smaller than 480×270 physical**: `k` clamps to 1 and the art viewport is center-cropped to the safe rect — the crop edge behaves as the viewport boundary; taps beyond it are dead; hit rects clip to the visible region. [TR-INP-059, TR-INP-063 · F-06]
- **If the safe height cannot fit the audited cluster at the active hit-scale** (V21 fails at runtime — the corrected cluster-side requirement is ≈ 178 dp at scale 1.5; the display-size-scaled density-2.75 720p probe (1440×720 px @2.75 — a high-density panel under Android display-size scaling, not a low-DPI device — R9 relabel, re-review #7) reaches H_safe ≈ 214 dp, which fits): the layout audit flags the profile and telemetry logs it — **compact mode is deleted (D2, re-review #5: the relocated pause removed the old 238 dp vertical stack)**; the hit floors are never violated, and the a11y pause-menu actions (Interact, Toggle-Crouch — R-10) plus the AC-64 a11y Backdash button cover reachability on any flagged profile. [TR-INP-069, TR-INP-070 · V21, F-06]
- **If SYS-06 has not published `facing`**: the backdash detector disables itself (it cannot classify "back" — the true-back check `entry_sign = −facing` at press1 needs the published facing, and flip-attribution still needs it; hop direction is `entry_sign`'s, D4) + dev-mode assertion. Deliberate asymmetry with body_busy's fail-open: a misfired dodge is worse than a missing one — the gesture fails closed while availability fails open. [TR-INP-056 · F-07, F-08]

### Simultaneous rules

- **If two prompts light on the same frame**: the publisher's nearest-wins resolves before any press binds — input never observes two lit prompts, because the Prompt Slot publishes at most ONE. A tie is the publisher's deterministic order. [TR-INP-067, TR-INP-026]
- **If an Interact press lands the same tick its bubble-confirm opens a gate**: the press resolves the prompt and the context transitions on that tick; every OTHER pending intent clears at gate entry; atomic transitions guarantee no third verb resolves on the transition frame. **Gate-request terminality (generalized per B4, re-review #5 — replaces the ack-only rule):** ANY gate request raised in tick T — from an ack (bubble-confirm) or from a non-ack source (death, cutscene trigger) — is terminal for every uncommitted ack in T: no further offers or acks resolve that tick, uncommitted acks are voided, and remaining pending intents clear at the gate entry on that same tick; the ADR's pipeline order polls gate-capable pullers first so a gate-opening pull commits before other pullers act, and places the transition's effect immediately on the ack commit. [TR-INP-047, TR-INP-030]
- **If a press is captured on the exact frame a gate opens**: dropped with the buffers — age is irrelevant at entry. [TR-INP-047]
- **If hit-stop freezes while a backdash pattern is mid-recognition**: the game clock stops, the tap window cannot close; a completion during the freeze hits the body-free check — busy during a clash-freeze → dropped. No wall-clock leak can fire a dodge into frozen frames. [TR-INP-024, TR-INP-056 · F-07]
- **If a wall-clock feel timer crosses its threshold during hit-stop** (crouch guard's 90 ms, chip hold's 150 ms): the timer keeps counting (UI-feel doctrine) while the published channel never waits — the crouch guard's crossing flips `latch ∨ held_zone` raw at the crossing tick regardless of body state (B2: input publishes raw every tick; posture application is SYS-06's handoff, not input's); a chip crossing 150 ms during a Gameplay-context hit-stop commits, and its window age stays frozen until the clock resumes. [TR-INP-061, TR-INP-066, TR-INP-068 · F-04, F-09, F-05]
- **If supersede, puller-ack, and expiry all land on one tick**: intra-tick order is captures/supersedes → **gate-entry** → offers → acks → expiry — the puller deciding at tick T honors the order signed at tick T (Latest Order Honored extends *within* the tick); state **re-assertion publishes with the captures phase, ownership-conditional** (an edge beats same-tick assertion — TR-INP-071); gate-entry sits after captures and before offers so no same-tick offered intent can be pulled past a gate that opens that tick (TR-INP-047), and **gate-request terminality applies (B4): a gate request raised in tick T voids every uncommitted ack in T; gate-capable pullers are polled first** (see the simultaneous-rules entry). Rides the ADR's same-tick capture/consume verification item. **CONTINGENT on the ADR pipeline order (review fix):** this ordering is achievable only if the ADR's per-tick node pipeline processes captures before gate-entry before offers (gate-capable pullers first) before acks before expiry; if the ADR lands a different achievable order, this entry and AC-18/AC-20/AC-44 re-baseline to it — the ordering is a contract item *pending* the ADR (OQ#1), not an independent truth. [TR-INP-022, TR-INP-021, TR-INP-047 · F-05]
- **If an interact press and its prompt's unlight land on the same tick**: the press binds to the lit-at-stamp snapshot; F-10 re-evaluates against the next state publication → unlit → cleared immediately. A press on the exact tick the bubble dies does nothing. [TR-INP-030 · F-10]
- **If a gate entry lands mid-chip-hold or mid-crouch-guard** (pause, blur, Reading, Cache-terminal): the in-flight hold and accumulator are cancelled silently — no commit on return, no "not signed" haptic: a context change is not a player error. Held *states* persist; in-flight *measurements* do not. [TR-INP-047, TR-INP-068 · F-04, F-09]
- **If a gate opens mid-attack-commitment**: input's part is done — the intent was pulled and Combat owns the arc; input clears buffers and persists states; no half-signed order survives the transition. [TR-INP-047, TR-INP-029]

### Device & rig anomalies

- **If a gamepad disconnects mid-hold or mid-window**: held states sourced from that device release as if the player lifted (honest body truth); already-captured pending intents survive untouched (rig-agnostic once stamped); no error state. [TR-INP-006, TR-INP-013]
- **If touch and keyboard are used simultaneously**: events deliver honestly from either rig into the same slots; each *state* channel is owned by the most recently active rig — a stick touch after a key press owns `move_x` until the keyboard presses again. [TR-INP-006, TR-INP-016 · F-03 · TR-INP-071]
- **If the device rotates or the density rescales mid-chip-hold**: no mid-touch rebind — the hold keeps its binding, slide-off is set only by *finger* movement crossing bounds, never by the region moving under a stationary finger; release duration alone decides commit (release_observed is binding-based, F-09). The world moved, not the order. (Orientation is landscape-locked at MVP — EX-10; the in-scope case is density/scale change; rotation rides the post-MVP re-audit.) [TR-INP-060, TR-INP-068 · F-09, F-06]
- **If a palm or edge touch lands outside every zone or on the letterbox/crop**: binds "dead", release unbinds — no capture, no error, no haptic. [TR-INP-063, TR-INP-060]
- **If a second finger enters the movement zone while the stick is owned**: inert — the zone is single-owner. [TR-INP-060, TR-INP-016 · TR-INP-072]
- **If the stick finger lifts and a different finger lands a moment later**: a full release-then-touchdown handoff — at least one honest neutral frame between (the anchor re-homes at touchdown); never a blended continuation masquerading as one hold. [TR-INP-060, TR-INP-013 · F-02]
- **If the OS cancels a touch mid-hold** (system gesture, app switch, notification shade): cancel ≠ release — a chip hold cancels silently (never commits), the stick state releases, the crouch guard resets. A cancelled touch is never a signed order. [TR-INP-060, TR-INP-068 · TR-INP-073]

### Pattern & buffer edges

- **If backdash press2 keeps landing beyond the window** (mash-back slower than 280 ms): every late press2 re-arms as a fresh press1 **inheriting the chain's `entry_sign`** — attempts chain indefinitely by design, but a fire still needs release-strictly-between + in-window + body-free; hops only when the body frees, at whatever rate Movement's hop lock allows. The input layer adds no cooldown — grace is not the post office's. [TR-INP-056 · F-07, F-08]
- **If a crouch flip lands while the body is committed**: input publishes the raw `latch ∨ held_zone` state at the flip tick — states never wait (B2: input's deferred-during-commit rule is deleted); whether she crouches during or after the commitment is SYS-06's posture handoff (Movement GDD) — input takes no side. The lift is judged on the last in-contact sample; a leave-and-return mid-commitment re-serves the guard on the zone half (TR-INP-066's re-entry rule). [TR-INP-066, TR-INP-013 · F-04]
- **If toggle-crouch is latched and Jump is pressed**: the latch clears — Jump stands her up in both modes, one rule with no mode exceptions; re-crouch is one deliberate press. [TR-INP-066, TR-INP-070]

### Degenerate strategies — named and shut down

- **If the player mashes Attack** (12 taps/s vs 4 taps/s): structurally shut on **resolution count** — one slot per verb, supersede restarts the age, never adds resolutions; the keep-alive corollary (G3) is owned in TR-INP-022: a sustained mash keeps one order pending through an arc; **stall parity across tap rates is explicitly not guaranteed (retracted — Annex EX-6)**; and the mash stream must be **weakly dominated** in Combat's gating — some timing exists where the single early press clashes and the mash fails or pays (D6; probe at Combat's GDD review). [TR-INP-022, TR-INP-021 · F-05]
- **If the player presses Interact while walking toward a not-yet-lit cache** (pre-range buffering attempt): shut — a press with no lit bubble dies on the stamp tick (TR-INP-030), a stale press dies the moment its bubble unlights (F-10), a later-lit bubble never consumes it. Walking by a cache cannot open it. [TR-INP-030 · F-10]
- **If the player chains backdash into backdash hunting an i-frame loop**: input's exposure is minimal by construction — single-tick publish, drop-when-busy, no input-side cooldown — at most one dodge per body-free completion; invincibility overlap math (i-frame duration vs hop recovery) is Combat/Movement tuning, not input's to enable or prevent. [TR-INP-056, TR-INP-014 · F-07, F-08]
- **If a quarter-spin lands the same frame as a plain attack press (VS)**: the composite consumes the press — ratified precedence. Guarding knob: `STINGER_TOUCH_TOLERANCE_MS` — generous enough to stumble into, narrow enough that ordinary attacks aren't eaten. [TR-INP-033, TR-INP-031]

### Accessibility space

- **If TOUCH_HIT_SCALE = 1.5 makes hit rects overlap neighboring regions**: touchdown still binds to exactly one region — overlap is resolved by registration z-order, which SYS-24 owns (input consumes the winner). At 1.5 the overlap must be resolved, not prevented — the floors are promises. **SYS-24 contract note: registration z-order is load-bearing.** [TR-INP-070, TR-INP-060 · F-06]
- **If attack hold-repeat is toggled ON**: repeat presses are ordinary edge-triggered presses — supersede applies, one slot caps the stall, clash timing unaffected (the slot always holds the newest order). OS key repeat stays filtered separately (TR-INP-015) — the toggle is input-generated repeat, a different mechanism. New designer-only knob: repeat rate, when the toggle ships. [TR-INP-070, TR-INP-015, TR-INP-022]
- **If toggle-crouch is latched during stair or slope traversal**: the crouch channel stays latched and raw — whether crouch-walking traverses stairs is Movement's call; input delivers state truth unchanged and takes no side. [TR-INP-066, TR-INP-013, TR-INP-014]
- **If haptics are set to global strength 0**: every haptic meaning goes silent legally — acknowledgment falls entirely to the held-state visual, which the text+shape-never-color law keeps sufficient; no input timeout exists to compensate, because none exist anywhere. [TR-INP-064, TR-INP-070]

## Tuning Knobs

**Knob validation law (TR-INP-074, review fix) — pairwise invariants, validated at config load. Dev: violation = boot failure naming the knob(s). Production: the listed resolution applies + rate-limited telemetry. Adding a knob without its pair rows is a review blocker.** **Fixpoint law (review fix — resolutions can cascade):** the resolution pass iterates (max 12 passes) until no invariant is violated; a non-fixpoint config = dev boot failure / production last-good config + telemetry (e.g., V1's raise can trip V2). **Range enforcement (review fix, V0):** every knob's value must lie in its safe-range column — clamp to the nearest bound; enum knobs clamp to the default — documented ranges are now enforced, not advisory. `DEADZONE_EQUIV = STICK_DEADZONE ÷ STICK_LEASH` (smallest non-zero magnitude a touch can produce).
