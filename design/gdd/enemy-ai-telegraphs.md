## Overview

Enemy AI & Telegraphs is the other half of the trust contract: the player's read deserves an enemy worth reading. SYS-13 owns the enemy archetype table (three MVP types on SYS-07's tier economy), the **strike contract** Combat consumes (`power` 1–3, `telegraph_ms`, `active_ms`, `recovery_ms`, the hitbox with its height band, the locked `facing`, the `unclashable` flag), the **telegraph doctrine** (color-flash + shape lean, never opacity; the power is legible in the flash, the band in the shape, the commitment's pace in the duration — "a breath", never milliseconds), the deterministic **decision scheduler** that turns those archetypes into threats, the **follow-up punish** that makes losing a clash cost (the Warden's second breath lands inside the player's 500 ms clash-lose stagger, shown for ≥ 250 ms), and the enemy **stagger state** that makes winning one pay. It owns the enemies' bodies — their motion, their commitment, their bands — and publishes geometry and schedule; it never resolves an exchange (Combat does), never owns HP (SYS-07 does), never raises a freeze (SYS-29's clock, consumed not fed), never reads input. The enemy that lands a hit here earned it in front of the player: telegraphed, legible, and answerable — every MVP cadence strike answers backdash, clash, or crouch, verified as a review artifact, with the stagger-context punish recorded as the matrix's one deliberate exception (the shown cost, legible but not answerable).

## Player Fantasy

### The named fantasy — The Threat Keeps Its Word

Every enemy here is an honest conversation partner. It shows you what it will do — a flash of the color of its weight, a lean in the direction of its arc, held for a breath — and then it does exactly that: same direction, same height, same moment, every time. Nothing lands that was not shown; nothing shown fails to land unless *you* made it miss — or the world itself broke under you (a gate, a death), and those breaks are recorded rules, never silent exceptions. The little ones swarm and can be bullied out of their swings; the tall one does not flinch — it finishes what it signs, and if you answer its heavy with a light press, it shows you the second strike while your body is still paying for the first. When you win the exchange, it staggers — a long, earned stillness you can punish. The enemy is not a puzzle box or a reaction test; it is a sparring partner that always tells the truth.

### The contract — four terms, each testable

1. **The telegraph is the promise.** Every strike displays color-flash + shape lean for at least a breath before its active window; the flash carries the power band, the lean carries the height band, the duration sets the commitment's pace. No opacity pulses. Nothing lands unshown.
2. **The strike is whole.** Once the enemy's telegraph signs, the strike carries through telegraph → active → recovery, interrupted only honestly (a clash, a hit that cancels an unpoised windup, a gate, a death). The `facing` is locked at the telegraph's first tick — the enemy does not track you through its swing. Dodge out and it whiffs; that whiff is your tempo.
3. **The power is legible before you commit.** The player can read the power band from the telegraph alone: light flash = light strike (meetable, doublable), heavy flash = heavy strike (move, or answer from the timing the strike's window allows). Every MVP **cadence** strike is answered by backdash, clash, or crouch — at least one legible answer each — and the matrix shipping them is a review artifact of this GDD, not a hope; the stagger-context punish is the matrix's recorded exception: legible, not answerable.
4. **Losing has a shape, winning has a window.** Losing a clash costs the stagger plus a shown follow-up (the punishing elite's lesson); winning one buys the 800 ms counter-stagger — enough for the chain's answer. The punish is readable in advance; the reward window is learnable on first sight.

### Anchor moment — The Wide One

The tall one leans wide and flat — the denied lean. There is no meeting this: you flick back, the sweep passes through the space she left, and its whole recovery is spent on empty air. The space is yours now, and the Warden's next breath is a full telegraph away — the tempo is the reward for the read that stepped out.

### Anchor moment — The Second Breath

You misread the heavy chop and your light press met it: your body folds into the stagger — and in the same breath the Warden is already winding the short stab, its flash heavy, its lean low. You *see* the punish before it lands; the lesson lands with it. Next time, you answer the heavy flash with distance — and the tall overhead, met a breath early with the chain press, eats your counter and buys you a long, earned stillness.

### Language law

**Use:** read, telegraph, shown, signed, carried, finished, weight, band, breath (for timing windows), honest, answerable.
**Ban:** crisp, snappy, instant, frame-perfect, twitch, reflex, reaction speed, "just dodge", artificial, cheap.
The enemy's timing windows are "a breath" in player-facing copy; no millisecond talk reaches the player. The telegraph doctrine is color-flash + shape lean; opacity pulses are banned language and banned craft (Combat's ban, ratified here). Falsifiability rule: every feel-sentence must be verifiable by playtest or build check; if verifying it would need a stopwatch on reflexes, cut it.

### Success signature

Playtesters say: "I saw the wide one and stepped back"; "the tall guy doesn't care when I hit him — I have to move"; "when I lost the exchange it hit me again — I knew it would"; "the little ones I can just beat out of their swing"; "the big overhead — I pressed early and my counter landed"; nobody says an enemy hit them without showing it, and nobody calls the enemies unfair. **Anti-signatures** (the failure modes this GDD exists to prevent): "it telegraphed but I couldn't tell what it would do" (the shape/band encoding blurred), "the heavy one looks the same as the light one" (the flash bands merged), "it hit me during my stagger with no warning" (the punish telegraph skipped), "I hit it out of every attack — it never does anything" (the poise missing on the elite).

*Pillar ties: "Read, Then Strike" (the telegraph is the system's first promise, kept here) · "SotN, Not Hollow Knight" (the enemy commits with weight; no i-frame-pinata enemies) · "Remade Every Death" (an honest enemy makes death attributable — the read you missed is nameable).*

## Detailed Design

### Core Rules

**Rule IDs (`TR-ENM-*`) are stable requirement proposals — each carries a falsifiable test and is ready for `tr-registry.yaml` ingestion.** PROVISIONAL marks indicate contracts whose owning GDD is not yet authored; ownership boundaries are intended final. **No direct `Input` reads; no new verbs; the enemy is not an input citizen.**

#### 1 · The enemy contract — consume, publish, never resolve

- **TR-ENM-001 — Consume, publish, never resolve.** SYS-13 consumes SYS-07's strike damage table (the `power` maps 1:1 to F-HD-01 — SYS-13 authors no damage value on a strike), Combat's resolution orders (the counter-stagger order; the lose-branch outcome is Combat's to apply), SYS-06's raw publications (the player's position/hurtbox/facing — the AI's read), and SYS-07's `DefeatEvent`. It **publishes** the strike contract (the schedule, the hitbox, the bands) and the enemy state. It **never resolves an exchange**: the contact detection, the clash matrix, the deflect branch and the `DamageEvent` publication are Combat's (TR-CMB-025/028) — SYS-13 publishes geometry and schedule, Combat's probe decides what the geometry means. It never reads `Input.*`, never inspects the player's hurtbox directly for resolution purposes, never owns HP, never publishes a damage event. *Test: a mocked order/publication stream behaves identically to live capture; `src/enemies/` contains no `Input.` symbol, no hitbox-resolution symbol, no HP field, and no damage-publish call (CI grep); the exchange traces route through the Combat mock.*

- **TR-ENM-002 — The enemy is not an input citizen.** The eight body verbs, the intent windows and the registry are the player's. Enemy behaviors are archetype data, not verbs; zero registry entries; no enemy-side intent buffer; no enemy input gates. *Test: the registry diff is empty; input-layer files unchanged; the enemy build contains no buffer/offer symbols.*

- **TR-ENM-003 — Data-driven behavior.** Every archetype row, strike value, band, reach, cadence and rule parameter is a Tuning Knob or table row; no gameplay literal in code. *Test: changing a knob changes behavior with no code edit.*

#### 3 · The strike contract (the published schema)

- **TR-ENM-007 — The strike record.** Every enemy strike publishes exactly: `EnemyStrike {strike_id, archetype_id, power ∈ 1…3, telegraph_ms, active_ms, recovery_ms, hitbox {shape: RECT (the only MVP shape), reach_px, band_floor_px, band_ceiling_px}, facing ∈ {−1, +1}, unclashable: bool, source_entity, display_name}`. **No damage field** (SYS-07's table stamps `raw_damage` at Combat's contact); **no knockback field** (the single `power` drives F-CMB-09 and `KNOCKDOWN_POWER` 2); **no i-frame field** (the enemy grants none); **`soul_class` is not on the strike** (it is the archetype's — the HealthProfile's). The schema matches TR-CMB-028's required fields (`power`, `active_ms`, `telegraph_ms`, `hitbox`, `facing`) plus the enumerated additions: **`strike_id` and `archetype_id`** (identity), **`recovery_ms`** (the enemy's cost of signing), **`unclashable`** (the flag TR-CMB-028 names in prose), **`source_entity`** (the DamageEvent's source), **`display_name`** (the cause row's), and the hitbox expanded from TR-CMB-028's opaque `hitbox` to `{shape, reach_px, band_floor_px, band_ceiling_px}`. The strike's schedule: the telegraph occupies `[t0, t0 + telegraph_ticks − 1]`, the active `[t0 + telegraph_ticks, … + active_ticks − 1]`, the recovery follows; `facing` is locked at `t0` (TR-ENM-019). *Test: the schema fixture validates every field; the required set matches TR-CMB-028 and the addition list is exactly the six enumerated items; no damage/knockback/i-frame field exists (static audit).*

- **TR-ENM-008 — The clashability floor and the remaining-active law — the reconciliation.** The schema floor: a **clashable** strike carries `active_ms ≥ 84` (the smallest ms contract that ceil-converts to 6 ticks at 60 tps — V-CMB-11's law). The per-exchange law is always TR-CMB-008(c): the enemy's **remaining active** must satisfy `remaining ≥ (pull_tick − contact_tick) + startup + 1`. **The printed reconciliation:** the 84 ms floor is *necessary, not sufficient*. Free-body (`pull = contact`): the condition reduces to `remaining ≥ startup + 1` — ATK1 (6-tick startup) needs **7 ticks**, crouch/air (5-tick startup) need **6**; a strike contacted on its first active tick has `remaining = active_ticks`, so the floor's 6 ticks admit exactly the crouch/air arcs and never ATK1; contacted one tick later, remaining drops below the floor and the clash dies by geometry (AC-CMB-19's later-contact case). The chain clash (`pull = ATK1's chain point = start + 11`): the condition is `remaining-from-contact ≥ 18` in the worst case (ATK1 started on the contact tick). The floor therefore guarantees *potential* clashability; the authored **active margins** (TR-ENM-009/010) deliver the *actual* answers, and every authored strike's real per-arc clashability is recorded in the answerability matrix (AC-ENM-19) — the floor alone is never cited as an answer. An authored strike with `active_ms < 84` **must** carry `unclashable = true` (the sweep — TR-ENM-010); a sub-floor strike without the flag is a dev boot failure. *Test: the reconciliation fixture table (7/6/6/18 per arc); an 84 ms strike is clashable-by-crouch at first-tick contact only; a 7-tick strike admits ATK1; the sweep (80 ms) is flagged; an 80 ms unflagged fixture fails the boot.*

- **TR-ENM-009 — The long-active power-1 heavy (the counter's key).** SYS-13 authors exactly one power-1 strike with a **long active**: the **Warden's tall overhead** — `telegraph_ms 450` (27 ticks), `active_ms 350` (**21 ticks**), `recovery_ms 250` (15), band [30, 48] (HIGH), reach 34, display_name *tall overhead*. The chain-clash arithmetic (the worst case, ATK1 started on the contact tick): the enemy's remaining active from contact must be ≥ 18 ticks; authored 21 gives **3 ticks of margin**; when ATK1 pressed early (before the contact), the margin grows by the early-press lead. The overhead is also the duet teacher: ATK1 (power 1) vs it → the **double clash** (contact on active ticks 1–15 passes the free-body ATK1 condition, remaining ≥ 7); ATK2 via the chain (power 2) → the **counter** (30 damage + the enemy's 800 ms stagger — TR-CMB-010). ATK1's free-body counter is unreachable by construction (1 vs 1 is the double, never the counter — AC-CMB-12's recorded fact). **Vocabulary precedence:** in SYS-13's shape-family language "tall/vertical = meetable, wide/flat = the denied lean"; Combat's Early-Press anchor predates this law and says "wide overhead" — harmonized at the next Combat doc touch (recorded, not edited here — Combat is APPROVED). *Test: the chain fixture (S = C: 21 ≥ 18 ✓, the counter fires 30 + the stagger order); the early-press fixture (S < C widens the margin); the double-clash fixture (contact ticks 1–15); the free-body-ATK1-counter impossibility fixture.*

- **TR-ENM-010 — The `unclashable` flag (the sweep).** The flag is **schema data on every strike**, and exactly **one MVP strike is authored unclashable**: the **Warden's low sweep** — `telegraph_ms 350` (21), `active_ms 80` (**5 ticks** < the 84 floor — V-CMB-11's flag path), `recovery_ms 133` (8), band [4, 44] (MID — the crouch does not save), reach 40, `unclashable: true`, display_name *low sweep*. **Why the sweep:** the roster needs one threat that teaches *read the lean, not every threat is meetable* — and the flag must be honest: the sweep is authored below the clashability floor so a meeting press is denied by geometry and by flag alike; its resolution under Combat's law is TR-CMB-009's **no-clash branch** (the normal hit — the sweep's own damage and knockback, never the clash matrix). The sweep is **not** the lose cause (an unclashable strike cannot resolve a clash, so it cannot produce the lose stagger); **the lose cause is the heavy chop** (TR-ENM-017's pairing — the one clashable power-2 strike). **The legibility rider:** an unclashable strike's telegraph must carry the **denied-lean** — the wide flat extension family, visually distinct from every clashable strike's vertical-lift family. The read language: *vertical lift = meet it; wide flat extension = step out*. A future unclashable strike (SYS-14's bosses) inherits the rider; the shape-family audit (AC-ENM-06) enforces it. The flag's other half is the fixture half: the flag-vs-floor invariant (clashable ⇒ ≥ 84) is dev-boot-validated, and a synthetic sub-floor unflagged strike exercises the failure (never shipped as authored data). *Test: the sweep fixture (80 ms, flagged, denied-lean in the shape family); the no-clash fixture (a meeting press resolves TR-CMB-009's no-clash branch — the sweep's damage and knockback, never the lose branch); the invariant (a synthetic 80 ms unflagged strike fails the boot); the shape-family audit finds the sweep's lean outside the vertical-lift family.*

- **TR-ENM-011 — The projectile record (the deflect family).** `EnemyProjectile {projectile_id, owner_entity, damage: 8, power_band: 1, telegraph_ms: 350 (21 ticks — the rear-coil windup), launch_tick (= the telegraph's end + 1), speed_px_s: 170, hitbox (12×12 rect, radius 6), band [14, 26] (MID — the crouch does not save), recovery_ms: 250 (15 — the recoil), lifetime_ms: 3000 (180 ticks), unclashable: true (type-constant), deflectable: true, display_name: *charge orb*}`. The orb is **deflect-family, never clash-family**: TR-CMB-015's deflect resolves it (the projected attack's overlap + the age gate; the reversal at `DEFLECT_SPEED` 360 ≥ 170 — V-CMB-08 validated at registration); the melee clash never engages a projectile (V-ENM-10's disjoint: a strike is melee-or-projectile, never both). **The orb telegraphs like every threat** — the rear-coil windup is its telegraph window (≥ 250 ms, V-ENM-01 applies), it completes before the launch tick, and it carries the band-1 (light) flash; its `power_band` is the projectile's own authored member (the orb has no `power` field — its 8 damage sits between the P1 6 and the P2 12 rows, and band 1 says light). The orb's damage is its own registered value (8 — the prototype default TR-CMB-015 records), **not** a strike-table row. Pre-deflect, the orb collides with the **player's hurtbox only** (no team damage at MVP); post-deflect (the owner flipped to the player), friendly fire is real (TR-HD-019) — the orb can kill its own Spitter, and the `DefeatEvent`'s `killer_id` is the orb's **owner at the killing contact** (the player). *Test: the travel fixture (170 px/s = 2.833 px/tick); the telegraph fixture (350 ms, ends strictly before the launch tick); the reversal fixture (360 ≥ 170 validated); the disjoint fixture (no strike is both families); the friendly-fire kill publishes `killer_id = player`; the lifetime despawn is silent.*

#### 4 · The telegraph doctrine

- **TR-ENM-012 — The telegraph language.** Every strike telegraphs with **color-flash + shape lean**; **opacity pulses are banned** — the definition is SYS-29's (TR-JCE-016): an alpha (or alpha-equivalent) oscillation with ≥ 2 direction reversals within one effect instance; a single monotonic fade is legal. The telegraph occupies its full authored window and **completes before the active window's first tick** (readable-before-active — Combat's requirement). At MVP each telegraph fires **one flash** (≤ 3 flashes/s — SYS-29's photosensitivity law, satisfied by construction); the flash and the lean render for the telegraph's whole duration (a hold, not a blink). The **shipped-asset audit is SYS-13's gate** (AC-CMB-35's owner/trigger record): the fixture set of telegraph poses + flashes passes the opacity/palette/shape audits before any enemy asset ships. *Test: the static audit finds zero opacity-pulse telegraphs and zero multi-flash telegraphs; every telegraph's end tick < its strike's first active tick; the shipped-asset gate record names SYS-13 as owner.*

- **TR-ENM-013 — Power legibility (the two-channel law).** The player must be able to **read the power before committing**. Two authoritative channels: **the flash carries the power band** — three designer-fixed band members (band 1 light / band 2 heavy / band 3 dire; bands 1 and 2 are the MVP set), adjacent bands separated by **≥ 2 value-ramp steps** of the world palette (the TR-JCE-016 metric; the PROV_PALETTE ordering until the art-bible pin), never sigil-red, never color-alone (the shape disambiguates for a colorblind read — SYS-01's meaning = text + shape law); **the shape carries the height band and the meet/deny verdict** — the lean's direction and extent mirror the hitbox, in three families: **the vertical-lift family** (a tall lift or a quick vertical chop — the meetable strikes: swat, tall overhead, heavy chop), **the denied lean** (a level wide extension — the unclashable sweep), and **the low stab** (the second breath's short low stab — stagger-context only: it never appears as a free read, because it only fires while the player is already staggered, so no free-read disambiguation is owed). **The duration sets the commitment's pace** — a feel cue, authored per strike (the overhead's grand 450 is its *commitment* — the poise that cannot be interrupted; the swat's brisk 250 is its expendability); it makes **no weight claim** (the overhead is band 1 and the longest telegraph on the roster — duration and power are deliberately decoupled, so "long breath = heavy hit" is never a heuristic the roster can falsify). The telegraph floor: every authored telegraph is **≥ 250 ms (15 ticks)** — the read's minimum breath (V-ENM-01). Player-facing copy never states milliseconds ("a breath" — the language law). *Test: the flash-band fixture (each strike's band ∈ {1, 2}, and each strike's band index equals its `power` — power 1 → band 1, power 2 → band 2; the orb's `power_band` = 1); adjacent separation ≥ 2 value-ramp steps; the shape-family audit covers all six attacks (five strikes + the orb) with the family assignments above; a colorblind-simulation pass distinguishes the bands by shape alone (the device AC); the copy audit finds no ms text in any telegraph string.*

- **TR-ENM-014 — The band model (the crouch contract, consumed).** Every strike's hitbox carries a height **band** `[band_floor_px, band_ceiling_px]` above the floor — **both endpoints are active pixels** (the closed-interval convention, stated once here and used by every table in this GDD). Bands are authored against Movement's F-MOV-11 contract: the standing hurtbox occupies [0, 44], the crouched [0, 26.4]. The MVP vocabulary: **HIGH** — band_floor > 26.4 (whiffs the crouched body, connects standing); **MID** — the band spans into [0, 26.4] (hits both postures); **LOW** — band_ceiling ≤ 26.4 with band_floor > 0 (hits both; *no MVP strike is authored LOW* — recorded so the absence is a decision, not an omission). A strike hits a posture iff `band ∩ posture-band ≠ ∅`; the crouch answers **HIGH only** — the sweep and the orb exist to teach that the crouch is a read, not a panacea. ### States and Transitions

The enemy machine is one small FSM per enemy; the decision table is the edge-set, the strike schedule is the committed arc.

| State | Enter | Exit | body_busy | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **IDLE** | boot/spawn; recovery end; approach done | the decision table | no | the cadence runs |
| **APPROACH** | the aggro decision | in reach; aggro lost; gate | no | `walk_speed_px_s`; edges refused |
| **TELEGRAPH** | the strike decision | telegraph end; hit (unpoised); gate; death | yes | `facing` locked at the first tick (TR-ENM-019); the flash + lean render |
| **ACTIVE** | telegraph end | active end; gate; death | yes | the hitbox schedule is live; Combat's probe resolves |

**Transition laws.**

1. **The strike is whole once signed.** TELEGRAPH → ACTIVE → RECOVERY completes unless a hit cancels an unpoised TELEGRAPH (Husk/Spitter only), a gate kills it, or death ends it. ACTIVE and RECOVERY are never cancelled by player damage.
2. **The order-entered states override everything.** STAGGERED (the counter) and CLASH_LOCKED (the double) are entered from any state on Combat's orders; nothing else enters them; nothing but expiry/gate/death exits them.
3. **DEAD is terminal.** No state exits DEAD; pending strikes never land; the shell's presentation is SYS-02/SYS-05's.
4. **The gate is honest.** At gate entry the machine dies (any state); on resume IDLE with nothing queued; no damage on the transition frame (the telegraph that was shown may not land — the context broke, not the read).
5. **Freeze holds, never clears.** A freeze holds every timer mid-arc; the commitment stays published; no new decision starts frozen.

#### F-ENM-01 · phase_to_ticks — the conversion (TR-ENM-007)

`phase_ticks = max(1, ceil(phase_ms × physics_tps ÷ 1000))` · the same family as F-CMB-01/F-JCE-01; the strike's schedule is `telegraph_ticks → active_ticks → recovery_ticks`.

**Output at defaults (60 tps):** swat 15/8/12 = 35 ticks (583.3 ms) · tall overhead 27/21/15 = 63 (1050.0) · heavy chop 21/7/8 = 36 (600.0) · low sweep 21/5/8 = 34 (566.7) · second breath 15/8/15 = 38 (633.3) · spit (the orb) 21 telegraph + 15 recovery, the flight up to 180 ticks. **Example:** 133 ms → ceil(7.98) = 8 ticks (133.3 effective); 80 ms → ceil(4.8) = **5** (83.3) — below the 84 floor, hence the sweep's flag; 116 ms → ceil(6.96) = **7** (116.7 effective — the chop's exact ATK1 window: 7 ticks is the smallest active that admits `remaining ≥ startup + 1 = 7` at first-tick contact).

#### F-ENM-02 · clashability_margin — the reconciliation (TR-ENM-008)

`remaining ≥ (pull − contact) + startup + 1` · free body (`pull = contact`): `remaining ≥ startup + 1` → **ATK1 7 · crouch 6 · air 6 (VS — the air clash is grounded-only at MVP, TR-CMB-009's recorded branch)** · chain (`pull = ATK1's chain point = S + 11`, ATK2 startup 6): `enemy_active_end ≥ S + 17` → worst case (`S = C`): **remaining-from-contact ≥ 18**; ATK1 pressed early (`S < C`) widens the margin by the lead.

**The floor's role (printed):** `active_ms ≥ 84` ⟹ ≥ 6 ticks ⟹ *potentially* clashable (the crouch/air condition exactly); the *actual* per-arc answers come from the authored margins — the swat (8 ticks) admits ATK1 at contact ticks 1–2 only; the overhead (21) admits ATK1 (ticks 1–15), crouch (1–16), and the chain (21 ≥ 18, worst-case margin 3); the sweep (5) is below the floor and flagged unclashable. **Example:** the overhead contacted at its first active tick with ATK1 started the same tick: remaining 21 ≥ 18 ✓ — the ATK2 counter resolves at `S + 17` with 3 ticks to spare.

#### F-ENM-03 · punish_window — the follow-up's landing (TR-ENM-017)

```

its only losing branch: the free-body ATK1 at the chop's first active tick
contact C = A (the chop's active start)          # remaining 7 = ATK1's 6 + 1, exactly

stagger = [L, L + 29]                             # 30 ticks from the resolution tick
elapsed = 24 ticks = 400.0 ms — inside, always    # margin 5 ticks

```

**Output:** the punish lands at stagger-elapsed **exactly 24 ticks (400.0 ms)**, inside the 500 ms stagger (busy through 29, the land leaves 5 ticks of margin); the telegraph is visible ≥ 250 ms (its own duration) and the stagger-start-to-land lead is 400.0 ms ≥ 250. **Total cost (power 2):** `400 + 1200` = **exactly 1600.0 ms** ∈ F-HD-12's [1450, 1700]. **Why the single value:** the chop is HIGH, so the crouched attacker is whiffed (no contact, no crouch-clash); the sweep is unclashable and the second breath is context-locked; the ATK2 chain needs remaining ≥ 18 (7 ✗) — the ATK1-at-first-tick branch is the MVP's only lose, and the fixture is exact. **Example:** the lose resolves at A+6; the stagger runs A+6…A+35; the Warden frees at A+15; the second breath's telegraph runs A+15…A+29; the land at A+30 cancels the stagger's 25th busy tick and enters KNOCKDOWN.

## Edge Cases

**Format: If [condition] → [exact outcome]. [rationale where non-obvious.]**

### Freeze and gates

- **If a freeze begins mid-telegraph** → the telegraph timer holds; the flash holds mid-flash; the strike's wall arrival shifts with the world; the facing lock was already captured. [TR-ENM-021; the held-breath coherence.]
- **If a freeze begins mid-active or mid-recovery** → the timer holds; the hitbox schedule holds; on unfreeze the arc completes with its remaining duration. [TR-ENM-021.]
- **If a freeze begins mid-stagger or mid-punish** → both the enemy's 800 and the player's 500 hold; the punish window's tick math is freeze-invariant (the lead is measured in ticks). [TR-ENM-021; F-ENM-03.]
- **If a gate opens mid-telegraph or mid-active** → the machine dies; no damage on the transition frame; the strike never lands; on resume IDLE with nothing queued. The player saw a threat that never arrived — the context broke, not the read. [TR-ENM-016; SYS-05's context; ADR §6.]
- **If the context is non-GAMEPLAY (Reading, pause)** → `logic_running` false; every enemy timer holds; the raw pose publications continue. [ADR §3; TR-ENM-021.]

### Death and defeat

- **If the enemy dies mid-telegraph, mid-active, mid-recovery or mid-stagger** → the `DefeatEvent` ends the machine; the pending strike never lands; no sting, no SYS-13 presentation; SYS-04's defeat tick is the audio. [TR-ENM-024.]
- **If a deflected orb kills the Spitter** → the `DefeatEvent` carries `soul_class DEMONIC`, `killer_id = player` (the orb's owner at the killing contact). [TR-ENM-011/024.]
- **If the killing blow is the counter** → `killer_id = player`, `soul_class` per the archetype; the stagger ends at death (DEAD is terminal). [TR-ENM-018/024.]

### Exchange geometry

- **If two enemies' strikes contact the same tick** → both schedules were passed id-ordered; Combat resolves the first, the second resolves normally; the enemies' machines are independent (no pack coordination at MVP). [TR-ENM-020; TR-CMB-029.]
- **If a strike reaches a knocked-down or getting-up player** → damage applies, no new lock (the no-re-knockdown law); the decision table **refused to initiate** it anyway — a strike already in flight resolves honestly. [TR-ENM-015; TR-MOV-021.]
- **If the second breath's contact falls inside the revive's 1000 ms i-frames** → SYS-07's term-8 lock rejects the damage; the stab's arc completes; no retry; the Warden's next cadence decision runs. The second life is real. [TR-ENM-017; TR-HD-018.]
- **If the second breath's contact falls inside the getup's 300 ms i-frames** → impossible by construction (the land is exactly 400 ms into the stagger — the knockdown that follows occupies the getup); recorded so nobody "fixes" the window. [F-ENM-03.]
- **If a held a11y answer (an old hold-repeat origin) meets the sweep** → the flag denies the clash regardless of age; the sweep resolves TR-CMB-009's no-clash branch (its damage and knockback); the cost is named (the denied-lean taught "step out", not "hold"). [TR-ENM-010; OQ#7's exemption does not override the flag.]
- **If a player tries to deflect a melee strike or clash an orb** → impossible by type: melee is clash-family (optionally unclashable, never deflectable); a projectile is deflect-family (never clashable, unclashable by type-constant). V-ENM-10 enforces the disjoint. [TR-ENM-011.]
- **If the player crosses behind during the telegraph** → the facing lock holds; the strike lands in the locked direction; the whiff hands the tempo to the player. [TR-ENM-019.]
- **If the player leaves the reach during the telegraph** → the strike completes and whiffs; the enemy pays its full recovery; the whiff is the tempo window, not a damage promise. [TR-ENM-019; the strike-is-whole law.]
- **If a player hit lands during an unpoised telegraph** (Husk, Spitter) → the strike cancels: no damage, no contact; the enemy re-decides on its cadence; the interrupt is the swarm's DPS answer. [TR-ENM-016.]
- **If a player hit lands during the Warden's telegraph or active** → the strike completes (poise: telegraph_active); the damage applies to the Warden; the lesson stands. [TR-ENM-016.]
- **If a player hit lands during any recovery** → the recovery completes (never interrupted); the strike already resolved. [TR-ENM-016.]
- **If the player tries a crouch-clash at MVP** → impossible across the whole roster: every clashable strike is HIGH (the crouched attacker is whiffed — no contact, no clash), and the MID attacks are the unclashable sweep, the context-locked second breath, and the orb (deflect-family). The crouch's answer is the whiff, never the duel. [TR-ENM-014; TR-ENM-010/017.]
- **If the crouched player meets the swat, the overhead or the chop** → the HIGH band whiffs the crouched body — no contact, no clash, the whiff *is* the answer. [TR-ENM-014.]
- **If a third strike reaches the Warden during its CLASH_LOCKED** → impossible at MVP (one player, whose attack was consumed by the double; the locks run concurrent and expire together); recorded so a VS multi-party case knows the state is not a stagger and hangs no punish window. [TR-ENM-016.]

### Scheduler and world

- **If the 5th enemy is spawned** → refused + telemetry; `MAX_ACTIVE_ENEMIES` = 4 is the scheduler's bound. [TR-ENM-020.]
- **If the Warden is STAGGERED or CLASH_LOCKED when the punish trigger fires** → the latch expires (TR-ENM-017's semantics): no action; the punish waits for a future stagger. [TR-ENM-015/017.]
- **If the player is in CLASH_LOCK (the double clash) when the punish trigger would fire** → the trigger is the STAGGER level only; a locked player is not staggered; no punish. The third-strike case is Combat's. [TR-ENM-017.]
- **If the enemy's approach reaches a ledge** → the motion stops at the edge (the MVP locomotion refuses edges); the aggro re-aims; no ledge-walk AI. [TR-ENM-015's motion rules.]
- **If the Spitter's orb expires** (180 ticks) → silent despawn; no damage, no edge. [TR-ENM-011.]
- **If the orb meets another enemy pre-deflect** → no collision (the orb's MVP target is the player's hurtbox only); post-deflect friendly fire is real. [TR-ENM-011.]
- **If `physics_tps` changes mid-session** → every enemy constant recomputes from its ms contract; no arc expires early or overstays from the rate event alone. [F-ENM-01.]
- **If a knob is invalid** → the V-ENM law: clamp + rate-limited telemetry; dev asserts; a non-fixpoint config is a dev boot failure. [V-ENM.]

### Degenerate strategies — named and shut down

- **If the player crouches as a panacea** → the sweep and the orb both hit the crouched body; the crouch answers HIGH only — the roster teaches the read, not a stance-spam. [TR-ENM-014.]
- **If the player DPS's the elite out of its lessons** → the Warden's poise completes its strikes; the punish stays reachable; the swarm is the DPS-able pressure, the elite is the dodge/clash pressure. [TR-ENM-016.]
- **If the player stalls in the punish window** → the punish is deterministic (no AI hesitation); the telegraph is shown; there is no stall to exploit — the cost arrives. [TR-ENM-017.]
- **If the player farms orb-deflects for damage** → the orb is 8 damage and the Spitter's cadence (90 ticks) bounds the supply; the deflect is a tool, not a faucet. [TR-ENM-011.]
- **If the swarm stun-locks the player** → the downed-body refusal + the no-re-knockdown law make the 1200 ms arc untouchable; the Husks wait. [TR-ENM-015.]
