## Overview

Dual-Reality State & Rendering is the presentation half of the truth contract: the stage where SYS-02's world truth becomes visible — one geometry, two palettes — and the moment the truth changes becomes drama. It consumes SYS-02's dual-truth data model as data (`truth_epoch` + named flags + `epoch_pin`; the material lookup `PALETTE_MAP[epoch_pin ?? epoch]` — F-RWS-04, already contractual) and owns the render of it: every zone of every room renders at its resolved truth every tick (the Denial arena's `epoch_pin: 0` pocket renders the pristine lie — truth A inside the flipped zone; the Cold Corridor wing renders the liminal material that is neither truth), and it owns the two-truth palette tables at MVP — the reality-A steel/monitor-glow set and the reality-B candlelit set as named members, hexes pinned at the art-bible pass. The flip is this system's signature moment, co-owned with SYS-10 under an explicit seam: **SYS-10 owns the mutation state and applies the epoch at the operation's completion boundary (SYS-02's law, consumed verbatim); SYS-09 owns everything the player sees** — the held breath (a freeze raised on SYS-29's clock as a `request_freeze` source, ADR-0001 §3), the glitch that carries the re-materialization (SYS-29's TR-JCE-017 component, triggered and scaled here), and the ambient handoff edge SYS-04 designs against (contract-first, published with the flip-trigger contract). The values reconcile in this GDD (OQ-J1's consumption): `FLIP_FREEZE_MS` and `FLIP_GLITCH_MAG` are authored here with printed fixtures, under SYS-29's photosensitivity law (≤ 3 flashes/s; `REDUCED_FLASH` binds) and inside SYS-02's `REMATERIALIZE_BUDGET_MS` (100). During the flip the camera is a witness, never a participant (no camera mode — SYS-32's TR-CAM-013 reveal hold is consumed as-is; the freeze holds the eye). The **tells** — the epistemic layer (user decision D-6, the session's headline obligation) — are named MVP requirements here: the Epoch-0 presentation telegraphs the post-flip openings (symmetry, dead conduits, error codes) with **zero geometry change** (the tell surface is this system's render of SYS-02's existing data), authored elicitable — gate 2's tell-noticing probe measures exactly that. The shift/transition shader is the week-1-of-MVP validation risk (the systems index's high-risk row): this GDD carries the standalone **probe register** (the PR-CAM pattern) whose results gate the first SYS-09 story and feed S1-007, which gates this GDD's APPROVED verdict. It never owns the epoch (SYS-02's), never mutates it (SYS-10's), never reads input, never owns the camera, never writes HP. Without it the two truths are a database entry: the player would never watch the world stop pretending, everywhere at once.

## Player Fantasy

### The named fantasy — The One Who Remembers

*"The world forgot. She forgot. I remember both."* The player is the only entity in the zone holding both truths at once — not an ability, not a meter, an inevitability of having been there. The world's amnesia is the fiction; the player's memory is the fact. What this system renders is the material that remembering lives in: two truths wearing one shape, the moment the truth changes while you watch, and the half-noticed details that turn out to have been true all along. **Remembering is a condition, not a command** — the game never offers a verb for it; there is nothing to *do* with what you remember except walk back in and be right.

### The contract — four terms, each testable

1. **Nothing is told that could be shown.** The flip carries no UI, no text, no camera cut, no scripted announcement — all post-threshold information arrives as rendered world; the transition frames are world-only.
2. **Your half-notices become true, and the game never takes credit.** Every post-flip novelty traces to at least one Epoch-0 tell in the same room's presentation (at MVP: the zone gate's opening traces to RM-03's tell set), and the game never points back — no highlight, no flashback, no codex entry, no reward ping keyed to noticing. The recognition is self-served; that is what makes it earned.
3. **The shape is the memory; the material is the truth.** Room silhouettes persist across truths — you navigate reality B by recognition, never re-learning; what a shape *is* is decided solely by the resolved epoch. No hybrid materials, no per-room exceptions — except the two authored pockets: the Cold Corridor (neither truth — the liminal grey) and the Denial arena (pinned truth A — the pristine lie).
4. **Where the truth did not change, it is immaculate.** The arena is the most confident room in the zone: perfect symmetry, untouched sheen, zero glitch residue inside its bounds — over-fidelity, never under-render. It reads as eerie fidelity to a witness ("she still believes this"), never as a rendering failure ("buggy", "didn't load").

### Anchor moments

- **The Held Breath.** You finish reading the Sanctum log. Everything stops — you included. One breath, no sound but the monitor-hum thinning out; then the fault runs through everything at once, every room you have ever walked re-materializing in the same breath, and candle-hush arrives where monitor-glow was. You did nothing. You were standing in the room when the world stopped pretending.
- **The Double-Take.** You go back for the memory the counter says is missing — and the Cistern is not the Cistern you remember. The arch is exactly where your feet remember it; it is a gothic arch now. And the dead conduit you once glanced at — stamped with the code you couldn't read — is a candle bracket now, and where the wall was there is a passage into somewhere that was always there. Not "the game changed this room" — *I had seen this, and didn't know what I was looking at.*
- **The Too-Perfect Room.** The arena seals behind you. Her world is pristine — the same steel, the same perfect symmetry, the same untouched sheen — while everything you crossed to get here is wrong now. She looks at you and does not recognize you. Her world never changed, because she never stopped believing it. You remember both. She can't.

### Language law

**Use:** remember, witness, half-noticed, wrong now, truth, material, shape, recognize, still, breath, fault. **Ban:** detective words (clue, solve, evidence — nothing to hunt); command words (shift, toggle, control, flip — the world moves on its own); trick words (trick, fool, gotcha — the lie is the fiction's, never the level design's); tech-failure words (bug, corruption, crashed — except where the in-fiction diagnostics themselves speak: the error-code tells are the fiction's voice, not the engine's); design jargon never reaches the player. Remembering is a condition, not a command. Falsifiability rule: every feel-sentence is verifiable by playtest rubric or build check; if verifying it would need a stopwatch on spectacle, cut it.

### Success signature

Playtesters say: *"that arch had bugged me — now I know why"*; *"I went back and the room was wrong — and I'd seen the seam before, I just didn't know what it was"*; *"when it changed, everything changed at once — the whole world, one breath"*; *"her room was too clean — like she couldn't let it go."* **Anti-signatures** (the failure modes this GDD exists to prevent): *"it did a cool glitch effect"* (the effect upstaged the moment — check the transition's restraint first, then the tells' subtlety); *"the game hid it from me"* (elicitation failed — resentment, not recognition); *"the arena looked broken"* (the pristine lie read as a rendering failure); *"I started checking every wall for secrets"* (tell-hunting — the world became a puzzle board); *"so the world changes when I press X?"* (the pivot violated in presentation). **The design test:** after the first playtest with the flip in, ask unprompted — *"what happened when you read the log?"* Answers leading with what they saw and remembered ("that arch had bugged me") mean the fantasy landed; answers leading with the effect mean spectacle drift.

*Pillar ties: "One World, Two Truths" (the pillar's spine rendered — the two truths, the flip, the tells) · "Five Goodbyes" (this system constructs the epistemic position every goodbye lands from: you remember her two truths; the Denial boss doesn't even remember you — the asymmetry built by presentation, not script) · "SotN, Not Hollow Knight" (the inverted castle's actual experience — traversing the doubled map from memory) · "Read, Then Strike" (the tells are the read: the world telegraphs its own future inside its first truth's material) · "Remade Every Death" (the reconstruct returns you to a world whose truth persists — your memory survives your death; SYS-15 owns the counter, this system owns the world you wake back into).*

## Detailed Design

### Core Rules

**Rule IDs (`TR-DR-*`) are stable requirement proposals — each carries a falsifiable test.** PROVISIONAL marks indicate contracts whose owning GDD is not yet authored. **No `Input` reads; no epoch writes; no geometry writes; no camera logic; no audio content; no save writes.**

- **TR-DR-001 — Consume the truth, own the render, publish the contract.** SYS-09 consumes SYS-02's dual-truth data model **as read-only data** — `truth_epoch`, the named truth flags, the per-zone `material_rule` and `epoch_pin` columns (F-RWS-04's lookup is resolved by this system's core: `epoch_in = epoch_pin(zone) ?? truth_epoch`), and `REMATERIALIZE_BUDGET_MS` (SYS-02's constant, consumed never authored). It owns: the two-truth palette tables, the per-tick render law, the re-material application, the tell registry and its rendering, the pristine-lie and liminal presentation rules, the flip's presentation drama (`FLIP_FREEZE_MS`/`FLIP_GLITCH_MAG` — authored here, passed at request time), and the **Flip-Trigger Contract** (TR-DR-004). It never touches: `truth_epoch` or any flag/mutation write (SYS-10's), TileMap/tile data (the no-geometry law, TR-DR-009), the player body and input, collision, audio content (SYS-04's), camera logic or leases (SYS-32's), save files (SYS-03's), palette hex literals (data, not code). **`RealityCore`** is a pure `RefCounted` over injected ports: `TruthPort` (read: epoch, flags, pin, zone data), `ClockPort` (read: ticks), `RenderPort` (write: material keying, re-material, tell render, present orders), `FreezeFxPort` (write: the SYS-29 freeze request + the glitch trigger — the trigger surface resolves against OQ-DR-7 (ii), the admission semantics against (iii)), `WorldOpsPort` (read: `operation_in_flight` + the completion edge — SYS-02's publication; the PENDING_DEFERRED decision's input), `TelemetryPort` (write), `TuningPort` (read). No `Input`, no `SceneTree`, no `Time`, no `RenderingServer` inside the core (the ADR §15 seam pattern, applied after `InputCore`/`WorldCore`/`CameraCore`). Flip edges do not arrive through a port: the node adapter subscribes to SYS-10's flip-request signal and calls core intake methods; the core returns a declarative flip plan the adapter executes. *Test: the purity audit greps zero write calls to truth/mutation/body/camera/audio/save APIs in SYS-09 modules; every state read enters via `TruthPort`; the core instantiates with fakes and replays bit-identical.*

- **TR-DR-002 — The two-truth palette tables (isomorphic, named, provisional-hexed).** One table, three groups, names by role never by hue: the **reality-A slots** (`PROV_A_WALL/FLOOR/TRIM/FRAME/GLOW/HAZARD/VOID/SEAL` — steel, monitor-glow, Warped-City dialect), the **reality-B slots** (`PROV_B_WALL/FLOOR/TRIM/FRAME/GLOW/HAZARD/VOID/SEAL` — candlelight, wine red, Gothicvania dialect; `B_SEAL` dormant at MVP), and the **liminal ramp** (`PROV_LIM_DEEP/MID/PALE/LIGHT` — the third table; greys own seam space; pack-less by intent: A's pack with the machine detail stripped). Plus: `PROV_GLITCH_ACCENT` (the cyan tear accent — SYS-29 consumes the name), `PROV_GRIEF_TINT` (overlay anchor, not a member — the memories' color, the corridor's one admitted hue), and `PROV_SIGIL_RED` (recorded as **reserved, not a member** — lord presence only). Every entry carries a provenance record `{hex, status: PLACEHOLDER|PINNED, source}`; PLACEHOLDER is legal through the greybox MVP, PINNED is required before content ship (the art-bible pass upgrades hexes in place — the placeholder rule takes the owning pack's nearest existing tone, value band first). **The isomorphism law:** both truths fill the same slot list at the same placements — a re-skin is a table swap over unchanged coordinates, never a redraw; the shape-carrier exemplar is `FRAME` (the same opening coordinates, both packs render their frame). **Discriminator tests the table must pass:** the 2-second identifiability test (A cool-dominant / B warm-dominant; each truth's `GLOW` is its largest chroma area); hue-swap survival (recolor each set's glow to the other's temperature — A must still read machine, B must still read crafted; if truth dies in the swap the table fails); value zoning (entities ≥ 2 value-ramp steps clear of every backdrop member of their frame's truth — SYS-29's metric). *Test: replacing the hex data file re-skins the zone with zero code change; the data-membership scan finds zero A-members in any epoch-1 state record (outside the pin) and zero B-members in any epoch-0 record (the decoded-render sweep is PR-DR-01's device instrument); the discriminator suite passes on the built table.*

- **TR-DR-003 — The per-tick render law (one geometry, two palettes, made visible).** Every rendered zone resolves `epoch_in = epoch_pin(zone) ?? truth_epoch` and renders its single byte-identical TileMap under `PALETTE_MAP[epoch_in][zone.material_rule]`. **Steady state is free:** material keys resolve only on state change (room entry, the flip) and cache thereafter — a tick with unchanged state performs zero material lookups (the render-side equivalence: SYS-02's per-tick data-level read persists unchanged — the cached key is a pure function of (epoch, `material_rule`), bit-identical to the per-tick read; AC-DR-01's counter counts SYS-09's render-cache lookups only); the law governs what renders, never per-tick work. **The wing (Rect2(1312, 176, 160, 96), RM-03)** renders as ordinary A-wall at epoch 0 (there is no corridor yet — never a hole, never grey, zero tells inside the range) and `PROV_LIM_*` at epoch ≥ 1 — **epoch-driven, not flag-driven** (the mutation rides the flip's one transaction, so `epoch ≥ 1 ⇒ revealed` always holds; the flag gates only mechanics, SYS-02/10's side). **The tells** render in epoch 0 with the truth-A table's own members (no special tell color — see TR-DR-008) and **resolve at epoch 1**: the same footprints re-skin to their B reading (mounts light, plates become plaques, conduit routes become correct) — the anomaly resolving is a consequence of the truth change, never a scheduled event. **The re-material is player-visible-atomic:** no frame in the world layer ever shows a mixed skin — the flip's visible moment renders the resolved new truth whole (the ratified presentation, TR-DR-005's machine). **Mechanism requirements (the GDD states what, never how):** whole art px at every frame including mid-glitch (integer displacement, no fractional zoom, no subpixel sampling, no second resample — the device-res capture downscaled by k with nearest reproduces the art-res render exactly); no texture regeneration or resize during re-material; per-zone/rect keying able to include and exclude the arena without global passes; deterministic render (truth materials are a pure function of (epoch, material_rule, texel, position) — no wall-clock uniforms; the glitch layer is the sole stochastic surface and is disabled for captures); the flip-moment application cost ≤ `REMATERIALIZE_BUDGET_MS` (100) asserted in the flip run and measured by PR-DR-02. *The T2 reconciliation (recorded for SYS-02's sync): SYS-02's "every zone's material re-resolves the tick after" governs the data-level keyed lookup (O(1), the tick after the epoch boundary — the law holds verbatim); the render application is this system's, bounded by the budget.* *Test: a fixed-state run renders bit-identical frames across 600 ticks; the steady-state profiler shows zero material lookups per tick; the epoch-diff frame scan contains only designed palette deltas and zero geometry-pattern changes.*

#### F-DR-01 · epoch_resolution — the consumed lookup (TR-DR-001/003)

```
epoch_in(zone, tick)       = epoch_pin(zone) ?? truth_epoch(tick)
rendered_material(zone, t) = PALETTE_MAP[epoch_in(zone, tick)][zone.material_rule]
```

**Variables:** `zone` (a rendered zone — SYS-02's zone data; the `material_rule`/`epoch_pin` columns), `epoch_pin(zone)` (int ≥ 0 or null), `truth_epoch(tick)` (int ≥ 0, monotonic), `tick` (wall tick).

**Output:** the arena pins epoch 0 at both epochs; the wing's rule resolves the liminal ramp at epoch ≥ 1; the data-level key re-resolves the tick after the epoch boundary (SYS-02's law verbatim); the steady-state cache makes a state-unchanged tick perform zero lookups. **Implementation note:** the `??` is SYS-02's mathematical notation — GDScript has no null-coalescing operator; the core implements an explicit null test (`var pin := truth.epoch_pin(zone)`; `return truth_epoch if pin == null else pin`). **Examples:** RM-06 at tick 5000 with `truth_epoch` = 1 → `epoch_in` = 0 → the A map (the pristine lie); the wing zone at epoch 1 → `PROV_LIM_*`; every unpinned zone at epoch 0 → the A map.

#### F-DR-02 · flip_freeze_ticks — the conversion (TR-DR-006)

```
N         = max(1, ceil(FLIP_FREEZE_MS × physics_tps ÷ 1000))
wall_span = N ÷ physics_tps × 1000
```

**Variables:** `FLIP_FREEZE_MS` (the contract, ms); `physics_tps` (60 at MVP). **Output:** ticks ≥ 1; wall span ≥ the contract (never-stingy). **Examples:** 300 × 0.06 = 18.0 → **18 ticks (300.0 exact)**; 240 → 15; 600 → 36; 100 → 6 with runway 83.3 < 100 → **illegal** (the G4 invariant). **Rate law:** a `physics_tps` change re-baselines from ms (never restarted; the registry's F-01 rule). The full fixture table is TR-DR-006's.

#### F-DR-03 · flash_gate — the photosensitivity law's tick encoding (TR-DR-004 G6)

```
FLASH_MIN_INTERVAL_TICKS = ceil(FLASH_MIN_INTERVAL_MS × physics_tps ÷ 1000)   # 350 → 21 exact at 60
law: any two flashes from any source are ≥ FLASH_MIN_INTERVAL_TICKS apart
     ⟹ ≤ 3 flashes per any sliding 1000 ms window
       (4 beats need span ≥ 3 × 350 = 1050 > 1000 — impossible)
beats_inside_hold = 1 + floor((N − 1) ÷ FLASH_MIN_INTERVAL_TICKS)
```

**Variables:** `FLASH_MIN_INTERVAL_MS` (≥ 350, fixed), `physics_tps` (ticks/s), `N` (the freeze span in ticks — F-DR-02's; pre-extension — extension-driven beats ride the spacing assertion), `FLASH_MIN_INTERVAL_TICKS` (derived, ≥ 1).

**Output:** the trace-checkable strobe constraint (the replay asserts every consecutive-beat interval ≥ `FLASH_MIN_INTERVAL_TICKS` wall ticks (21 at 60 tps), all sources). **`beats_inside_hold` is a compliance capacity bound** (how many beats the law admits wherever they land), never a mandate — an extension-driven in-hold beat remains governed by the ≥ `FLASH_MIN_INTERVAL_TICKS`-spacing assertion (any **future** in-hold beat — none is authored at MVP). **Examples:** N = 18 → 1 + ⌊17/21⌋ = **1 beat** (τ0 only); N = 36 (600 ms) → 1 + ⌊35/21⌋ = **2** (τ0, τ0+21); `REDUCED_FLASH` → **0 beats**. Why 21, not 20: 20 ticks = 333⅓ ms; beats at 0 / 333⅓ / 666⅔ / 1000.0 put 4 onsets inside the closed window — 21 is the minimal tick-safe floor.

#### F-DR-04 · glitch_channels — amplitude-only magnitude (TR-DR-006)

```
tear_px    = min(8, round(FLIP_GLITCH_MAG × 8))      # cap 8 — never tile-aligned (8 < 16)
desat      = FLIP_GLITCH_MAG
jitter_px  = round(FLIP_GLITCH_MAG × 3)              # cap 3
scan_px    = max(2, round(6 − 4 × FLIP_GLITCH_MAG))  # static per frame
scan_alpha = 0.15 + 0.35 × FLIP_GLITCH_MAG           # cap 0.5
aftershock_tick = max(τ0 + FLASH_MIN_INTERVAL_TICKS, freeze_close_boundary + 1)  iff FLIP_GLITCH_MAG ≥ 0.75
                 # N = 18 → R+22 (50.0 ms post-release); N = 36 → R+37 (the release boundary)
```

**Variables:** `FLIP_GLITCH_MAG` ([0.4, 0.8]), `τ0` (the swap boundary tick), `freeze_close_boundary` (the R+N boundary — the release tick boundary; `+1` = R+N+1, the E6 convention), `FLASH_MIN_INTERVAL_TICKS` (F-DR-03's derived quantity).

**Examples:** at 0.6 → 5 px / 0.6 / 2 px / 4 px / 0.36 / off; at 0.4 → 3 / 0.4 / 1 / 4 / 0.29 / off; at 0.8 → 6 / 0.8 / 2 / 3 / 0.43 / **on**. **No flash frequency ever scales with magnitude** — temporal frequency is a function of the transition duration and the flash gate, never of MAG (compliance is structural, not tuned).

#### F-DR-05 · extension_ticks — the seamless extension (TR-DR-005)

```
ext_ticks = max(1, ceil(remaining_ms × physics_tps ÷ 1000))
```

**Variables:** `remaining_ms` (the measured application remainder at the last held tick, ≥ 0 ms — the injected fixture uses 40), `physics_tps` (ticks/s).

Raised **during** the last held tick (zero unfrozen gap — a raise after the boundary would leak one half-applied frame); cap 2 extensions, then force-release + error report. **Example:** remaining 40 ms at 60 tps → ⌈2.4⌉ = **3 ticks (50.0 ms wall)**.

#### F-DR-06 · runway_invariant — G4 by construction (TR-DR-004/006)

```
runway_ticks = N − 1
capacity_ms  = runway_ticks × SWEEP_TICK_BUDGET_MS          # SWEEP_TICK_BUDGET_MS 8, safe 4–10
invariant:  FLIP_FREEZE_MS > REMATERIALIZE_BUDGET_MS        # the mechanical floor (strict)
staged path (the heavy-mechanism fallback): capacity_ms ≥ REMATERIALIZE_BUDGET_MS
  # the visible swap is atomic at τ0 for EVERY mechanism class (transition law 4; E4; AC-DR-19):
  # the staged capacity bounds only the invisible preparation sweep (material pre-bake/upload),
  # which completes within the freeze and may never alter a rendered frame mid-hold
  # (the visible set is fixed during the hold — TR-DR-013's witness law);
  # a mechanism that cannot deliver the atomic τ0 swap is disqualified at the ADR, whatever its capacity
```

**Variables:** `N` (F-DR-02's conversion), `runway_ticks = N − 1`, `SWEEP_TICK_BUDGET_MS` ([4, 10]), `REMATERIALIZE_BUDGET_MS` (SYS-02's, 100), `FLIP_FREEZE_MS` (F-DR-02's).

**Both floors, printed:** mechanical — runway ≥ 6 ticks (6 × 16.6̄ = 100.0 exactly) → N ≥ 7 → any ms > 100, zero margin; practical — hosting 100 without extension needs runway ≥ ⌈100/8⌉ = 13 → N ≥ 14 → the N = 14 exemplar band is (216.67, 233.33] ms → **nominal floor 240**; if PR-DR-02 proves the sweep ≤ 64 ms, the floor relaxes to 150 (runway 8 × 8 = 64 ≥ the measured sweep; **the staged operand switches from the 100 ms budget to the measured sweep**) — a same-edit change to V-DR-07's band, the knob row, TR-DR-006's stated safe range and fixture-table floor-row label, **V-DR-03's staged operand and AC-DR-07(a)'s and (c)'s fixtures**, conditional on the probe's record, never a silent drift. **The direction check (both directions, every config edit — the RM-01 lesson):** lowering `FLIP_FREEZE_MS` below the floor and raising `REMATERIALIZE_BUDGET_MS` without re-checking are equally boot-failing; the check runs on every edit of either operand. At the keyed-lookup class the invariant is vacuous (setup ≤ 2 ms ≪ one tick's 16.6̄). **Examples:** 300/18 → capacity 17 × 8 = 136 ≥ 100 ✓; 240/15 → 112 ✓; 150/9 → 64 ms capacity vs a 64 ms measured sweep = zero margin; extension-dependent unless the probe's record lands.

#### F-DR-07 · remat_bound — the measured assertion (TR-DR-003)

```
remat_ms ≤ REMATERIALIZE_BUDGET_MS (100.0)   # asserted in the flip run — a measurement record, never replay-compared
```

**Variables:** `remat_ms` (the measured application total, ≥ 0 ms), `REMATERIALIZE_BUDGET_MS` (SYS-02's, 100).

The data-level key resolution is an **ordering law** (the tick after the epoch boundary), not a wall-clock bound. **Example:** the keyed-lookup class measures the setup batch ≤ 2 ms ≪ 100 ✓.

## Edge Cases

**Format: If [condition] → [exact outcome]. [rationale where non-obvious.]**

### The flip and the truth

- **If the Reading closes while a border arc is in flight** → the whole choreography defers to the arc's completion boundary (the freeze included); truth A continues silently through the gap (≤ 700 ms for a full arc; activations ≤ 16 ms ≈ 1 tick); every fixture shifts (T0 := R). [TR-DR-005 G3; a half-traveled border hanging behind the tear reads as a bug, and an arc's remainder can outlast the freeze.]
- **If chained operations stack** (an arc completing as another activates) → re-entrant parking; the flip fires at the first stamped tick observing the request with no operation in flight. [TR-DR-005.]
- **If a combat freeze is live at the Reading's close** → nominally impossible (SYS-05's Reading suspends combat — the verification rides OQ-DR-1); the leak path (a hit resolving in the close tick itself) joins the set: 2 concurrent sources ≤ 4, the union law holds, the combat source's ≤ 6 held ticks expire inside the flip's 18, the epoch still applies at τ0 (the machine is wall-native). [TR-DR-005's invariants.]
- **If the freeze request is denied** (5 concurrent sources) → the sequencer retries each subsequent tick until admitted; the epoch waits for the *admitted* raise — the choreography never runs uncovered. [SYS-29's dev assert; the defensive path.]
- **If the player stands in the arena when the flip applies** → the application skips pinned zones (a data predicate, recorded in the trace); the player sees the tear over the unchanged arena; release: the arena identical, the world beyond its border truth B — the seam is the point. Reachable only through a long deferral chain. [TR-DR-011.]
- **If the app suspends mid-freeze** (mobile OS) → physics stops; on resume the countdown continues from its record (SYS-29's leak-proof law); the flip completes on resume; no edge is lost. [SYS-29's clock semantics — recorded for its verification pass.]
- **If a save is requested inside the flip window** → queued to the first unfrozen tick; it captures the post-flip state; a mid-choreography snapshot is never written. **If a save happens during a deferral gap** → it persists `{epoch A, flag set, pending-flip}`; the flip re-fires deterministically at the first post-load tick (no operations are in flight at load-complete by construction). [TR-DR-015's save-scumming law.]
