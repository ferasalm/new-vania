## Overview

Camera & Framing is the eye of the trust contract: it owns the one camera the game renders through under ADR-0001's D1 model — the world renders in a root-stretch-disabled SubViewport at a safe-derived **integer scale** (the camera lives in the world's 480×270 art-unit space at `zoom (k, k)`), the HUD renders native above it, and **every framing output is whole art pixels**: the camera never zooms, never subpixel-drifts, never re-scales. It consumes SYS-02's per-room bounds as its **default limits** ("room limits first" — the room's honest rect is the frame's word; when a room is smaller than the view the centered rule holds and the room's backdrop covers the slack), consumes SYS-06's publications (`facing`, the body-center position (the recorded rider), `grounded`, the wall state's two bits, `body_busy`, the hop/knockback events, **the posture-selected hurtbox extent**) to drive the follow, and executes SYS-29's shake requests — **clamping them inside the room limits** — while publishing the on-screen player position SYS-24's thumb-coverage caps and the hold targets SYS-10/11's truth flip and reveal consume. The MVP camera is deliberately small: follow + a small facing-keyed look-ahead with a commit delay, vertical motion only on landing, a long-fall look-down, and the **two authored framing zones** (the Denial arena's hold level; the backtrack reveal's held frame). It **reads no input** — no ninth verb, no manual look, no zoom (rejected: it breaks the integer scale and the mobile fill-rate) — an eye that never gives you reason to mistrust it, because it never lies about where you are. Without it the world has no frame: honest bounds without a window, a boss without a stage, a reveal without the held breath.

→ *Implementation patterns (the `Camera2D` limit wiring under the SubViewport render path, `reset_smoothing()` on the atomic handoff, the look-ahead's game-clock integration, integer-snap enforcement) are ADR raw material for the planned **Camera & Framing ADR**; this GDD stays at the behavior level. The camera reads no input (TR-INP-004's eight-verb closure — the down-zone is Crouch); the follow runs on the game clock (SYS-32's plan, consumed by SYS-29).*

## Player Fantasy

### The named fantasy — The Room Waits to Be Read

You never think about the eye. It is already ahead — a few steps in the direction you chose — and once it commits, it holds: your strike, your retreat, your stumble all happen inside a frame that has already decided. It moves on the ground's schedule — the wall riding like the ground too — and rises and falls only when you land; when the drop is long it looks down with you and shows you the whole fall. In a duel it gives you the stage; in a revelation it gives you time. And its weight — the tremble when the exchange answers — stays inside what the room can honestly show. In a world of errands, contracts, and receipts, nothing is given freely — but **the read is hers**: the assassin's trained patience is the one uncontracted thing she owns, and the frame is its instrument — not the employer watching her, never the surveillance register; an eye that waits because she reads.

### The contract — four terms, each testable

1. **It goes ahead of you, and then it holds.** The frame leads by your facing and does not chase: the commit delay is the hold's promise, and a strike or a stumble happens inside a frame that already decided. *(Facing-keyed look-ahead with a commit delay; frozen through attack, backdash, knockback, hit-stop.)*
2. **It moves on the ground's schedule — and the wall counts as ground.** The eye re-frames on landing, on the fall's tip, or on a cling — the wall clings like the ground, her slide riding it the same way. No free vertical chase while airborne; a long fall is shown whole — the eye tips down with you and the floor arrives inside the frame. *(Vertical reframe on landing and on the phase-keyed tip; the cling shares the ground bias; long-fall look-down presents the full drop.)*
3. **In a duel it gives you the stage; in a revelation it gives you time.** The Denial arena is held whole for the length of the fight — the duel reads as staged choreography. The backtrack truth resolves inside a held frame while the world waits — **shown, not sprung**, SYS-02's words inherited without paraphrase. *(The arena framing lock; the reveal hold at the framing zone.)*
4. **Its weight stays inside the room's honesty.** The tremble is weight, not spectacle: the clash family's few pixels, clamped within what the room can truthfully show. The eye never reaches past the room — no closer view, no straddled thresholds — because a frame that exceeds the room is a frame that lies about it. *(Shake clamped to the room limits; never zooms; never straddles two rooms.)*

### Anchor moment — The Wrong Corridor, Held

You take the long corridor back — the one your feet know by now. The eye rides a few steps ahead of your facing, the way it always does, already holding the ground you're about to cross. Then the wall is simply framed — it already was: the eye never travels to meet the reveal, because it was already holding the ground the reveal lives on. The partition opens, and the corridor's other truth resolves inside a frame that does not move to meet it — at most a breath of settle, and still. Shown, not sprung. By the time you arrive, the room is simply larger than it was, and the eye that showed you never once grabbed you into knowing.

### Anchor moment — The Whole Fall

You miss the perch. The drop is longer than the screen. The eye does not chase your feet — it tips down with you, the deeper the fall the deeper the tip, and the tip holds while you drop — and you see the whole fall and the floor it ends on. The height was told before the pit answered.

### Language law

**Use:** frame, eye, room, stage, duel, shown, waits/waited, read/reading, hold/held/still/steady, whole, honest, weight, settle, ahead, land, fall, courteous.
**Ban (player-facing copy):** *camera* (dev sections only — player-facing says *the frame* or *the eye*); all mechanism jargon — viewport, deadzone, lerp, smoothing, easing, snap, screen-space, integer scale, look-ahead (say *already ahead*), zoom, pan, sweep, cinematic, dynamic. Ban the sinister register — *watch, spy, stalk, surveil* — the eye is patience, never surveillance. *Sprung* appears only inside the inherited phrase **"shown, not sprung."** **The `courteous` guard:** the word describes the frame's *patience* — never the world's kindness (warmth in her world is false, tragic, or corrupting) — and never appears in diegetic or log text (SYS-28's surface).
Falsifiability rule: every feel-sentence is verifiable by playtest rubric or build check; if verifying it would need a stopwatch on reflexes, cut it.

### Success signature

Playtesters say: *"saw it coming"* as a fact, not a boast — threats named while still steps out of range, without noticing the eye that made it possible; nobody dies to the unseen in framed zones — the Denial duel described as watched choreography, stages and tells and exchanges, not survived chaos; players stop on their own at the reveal — the held frame reads as an invitation to look. **Anti-signatures** (the failure modes this GDD exists to prevent): "the camera got me killed" in any dialect — an off-screen hit, a threat entering the frame mid-swing, a reveal that lurches (one instance in an authored zone is a breach, not a tuning note); hands reaching for a verb that doesn't exist — dragging the view, asking for manual look, fighting the frame's edges; the eye noticed as an effect — "that camera move was cool" is failure wearing applause, and shake that escapes the room's honest few pixels is the same failure with tremble. **The scoping, said honestly:** "nobody dies to the unseen" is guaranteed inside the **authored zones** (the arena's staging band, the reveal's threat-free arc window, the clash families' frames) — outside them, in ordinary rooms, the blind-side risk is real and owned by its own estate: SYS-13's telegraphy is the primary answer, encounter authoring the second, and the look-ahead's read window the frame's contribution; the off-screen-hit tripwire token in the P1 rubric is the playtest's tripwire for that whole estate. **Anti-signature tokens are canonical:** "that camera move was cool" and "I saw where I'd land" appear verbatim in the coding table below.

## Detailed Design

### Core Rules

Three laws govern everything below: **the D1 law** (every framing output is whole art pixels — never zoom, never subpixel), **the game-clock law** (the camera's world state advances only on elapsed `logic_tick`s — a held world holds its eye), and **the command law** (everything with a schedule — arcs, arena, shake — arrives as data from its owning system; the camera interprets no facing causes, no zone semantics, no boss state; it consumes publications and levels/commands only). Design-source note: the follow/look-ahead/vertical model adapts the Inbound Shovel metroidvania-camera devlog ("How Metroidvania Cameras are Shockingly Different than Platformer Cameras" — the half-screen boundary principle, the push-ahead with a settle, the phase-keyed vertical look-ahead), plus the Order of Ecclesia single-screen boss-stage pattern for the arena hold.

**Rule IDs (`TR-CAM-*`) are stable requirement proposals — each carries a falsifiable test and is ready for `tr-registry.yaml` ingestion.** PROVISIONAL marks indicate contracts whose owning GDD touch is recorded, not yet landed; ownership boundaries are intended final. **No input reads; no zoom; no subpixel placement; no room-straddle; no arc constants of its own.**

- **TR-CAM-001 — The purity seam.** **`CameraCore`** is a pure `RefCounted` reducer over injected ports: `BoundsPort` (SYS-02's generation-stamped publication), `BodyPort` (SYS-06's publication — position, grounded, facing, the wall state's two bits, body_busy, the events, **the posture-selected hurtbox extent**), `ShakeQueuePort` (SYS-29's request drain), `ZonePort` (SYS-14's `arena_hold` level + SYS-02's reveal arc commands), `ClockPort` (the `GameClock` read view: `logic_tick`, `physics_tick`, `logic_running`, **`context`** — read, never recomputed; the gate-vs-freeze discrimination and the shake's third guard need the context, `logic_running` alone cannot distinguish them), `TuningPort`, `TelemetryPort`. **No `Input`, no `SceneTree`, no `Time`, no `RenderingServer` — and no `Camera2D` — inside the core** (ADR-0001 §15's seam pattern, applied a third time after `InputCore`/`WorldCore`); the node adapter (`CameraRig`, owning one persistent `Camera2D` child) owns all engine contact. Determinism: the same inputs at the same ticks produce bit-identical state traces. *Test: the core instantiates with fakes; the purity audit greps the five forbidden symbols; two replays are bit-identical.*

- **TR-CAM-002 — The game-clock law and the tick table.** `CameraCore.advance_world()` is invoked **exactly once per elapsed `logic_tick` and zero times otherwise** — an elapsed tick is `logic_running = (context == GAMEPLAY) ∧ freeze_sources.is_empty()` (ADR §3, read from `GameClock`, never recomputed). The per-tick work is **not one pipeline** — it is a phase table with a gate per row:

  | Phase | Gate (which ticks) | Content |
  |---|---|---|
  | **drain** | every physics tick | command intake: the shake requests (ingest → replace-resolve), the `arena_hold` level, the reveal arc commands; the publication snapshots (body, bounds) |
  | **advance** | **iff `logic_running`** | the world pipeline: follow/look-ahead FSM, the commit countdown, the vertical phase state, hold evaluation — the game-clock law's content, once per elapsed tick, zero otherwise |
  | **compose** | every physics tick | the frame: the (frozen) `pos` + the live shake offset; the clamps apply |
  | **publish** | every physics tick | the publication — `authority` derived at publish time from the live clock state |

  During a hit-stop the **advance** row gates off — position, look-ahead FSM, commit countdown, vertical state, hold evaluation all hold bit-stable — the camera is a *witness of a held world, not an actor in one* (SYS-29's damage numbers hold mid-flight because their world does; a converging follower would be a moving window over a still painting). The shake composes and publishes through the freeze (freeze-blind, SYS-29's physics-keyed contract). Pause/gates step nothing; resume continues from identical state — no teleport, no catch-up. **The commit countdown is logic-clock: it does not expire during a freeze — resuming, never dropping.** **The pipeline's position is a law, not a probe outcome:** the camera's advance runs **after Movement's body phase and after SYS-02's swap action, in the same physics tick, before the publication** (PR-CAM-07 verifies the engine-side mechanism; AC-CAM-10's same-tick atomicity is conditional on that verdict). *Test: a 6-tick hit-stop mid-follow holds the camera bit-stable; the countdown survives; resume closes the gap by normal convergence; the phase-gate fixture (a frozen tick runs drain/compose/publish and zero advance).*

- **TR-CAM-003 — The D1 integer-snap law.** The camera's rendered placement is **always whole art pixels**. All follow/hold state integrates in **integer hundredths** (the house's no-float law); the emitted position is the integer division `base_px = pos_h ÷ 100`, with the truncation remainder **retained in the gap** (`target − pos`) — the residual *is* the accumulator, so rounding error can neither accumulate nor jitter; a stop-snap at ≤ 0.5 px ends sub-pixel crawl. Dev builds assert the integer-ness of every written position. **The camera never zooms** (D1; zoom rejected through VS); the renderer's pixel-snap settings are admissible only as a failsafe, never as the law's enforcement. *Test: replay asserts integer output at every tick; the fractional-position probe (PR-CAM-05) demonstrates the artifact the law prevents.*

- **TR-CAM-004 — The room-limits law ("room limits first").** On the first camera tick in a room, the limits equal the published `RoomBounds.rect_px` **verbatim through F-RWS-02** — both systems compute identical integers by quoting the same formula (rect-exact when the room ≥ the view; the centered branch otherwise). Per-axis travel is `max(0, dim − view)`; no margins are added. **Missing bounds at the room's first tick is a fail-loud event** (dev assert + telemetry; production holds the last valid state) — never a silent default. *Test: the bounds fixture per room (RM-01 480×272 → 2 px vertical travel; RM-03 640×272 → 160 px horizontal, 2 px vertical; RM-04/06 640×352 → 82 px); the missing-bounds fixture.*

- **TR-CAM-005 — The centered rule.** In any axis where the room is smaller than the view, that axis **locks**: the view's offset into the room is `floor(slack ÷ 2)` from the room's top/left edge, the odd remainder is backdrop-covered at the bottom/right, and follow, look-ahead and shake are **zero in a locked axis**. Dormant at MVP (every room ≥ 480×272) but exercised by F-RWS-02's fixture alcove — the branch ships because the fixture tests it. *Test: the alcove fixture (rect 320×192 → slack_x 160 → 80 px extension per side, span == view on that axis).*

- **TR-CAM-006 — The follow law.** In FOLLOW, the camera target = the **body-center anchor** (SYS-06's published position — see the recorded rider below) + the horizontal lead, and the position chases the target with **one capped exponential on integer hundredths**: `move_h = clamp((target − pos) × ALPHA_H_H ÷ 100, −MAX_STEP, +MAX_STEP)` (the move's division truncates toward zero — the sign reapplied to the magnitude's floor; F-CAM-02's pinned semantic). Both target-side and emitted-side clamping to the center-clamp range; the stop-snap ends convergence. Only this tick's publications are consumed — no extrapolation between ticks, ever. *Test: the convergence fixture (the 64-px standing-flip swing settles ≈ 0.42 s — F-CAM-02's example); the capped-step fixture; bit-identical replay.*

- **TR-CAM-007 — The vertical law (phase-keyed — the Inbound Shovel model, adapted).** The vertical look-ahead is keyed to the jump phase, not to a threshold. **The phase key consumes the wall state** (SYS-06's publication) and the published anchor's own per-tick delta: `grounded → grounded; airborne ∧ wall ∧ toward-wall → cling; airborne ∧ ¬cling ∧ Δy < 0 → rising; airborne ∧ ¬cling ∧ Δy > 0 → falling` — **and `Δy = 0` (a quantization plateau or the apex tick) retains the previous airborne phase** (the predicates are strict inequalities; no phase change and no bias/`D_h` update on a plateau) — a wall slide (descending at `WALL_SLIDE_SPEED` against the wall, toward-wall held) classifies as **cling**, never falling, so the eye is never driven down a shaft she is climbing; `Δy` is the published body-center delta, measured on the tick-open publication snapshot. **The biases:** grounded or wall-clinging, the target sits `V_LOOK_UP_PX` (8) **above the body centre** — the jump-planning space (the grounded bias frames the takeoff; the apex read is carried by the rising tween, whose lag dominates); rising, the bias tweens to body-centre (bias 0) by the apex; falling, it tweens below the feet — the eye tips down with her, deeper fall = deeper tip. **One gain governs both axes** — the tween rides `ALPHA_H_H` (18/tick); there is no separate vertical gain. **The cap's one recorded exemption:** `MAX_STEP_H` bounds every vertical move **except while the falling phase is active** — a terminal fall (SYS-06's `TERMINAL_VELOCITY` ceiling: 900 px/s = 15 px/tick at 60 tps) outruns the 12-px/tick cap, and capping the fall's tracking would lose the body off the frame's bottom; the exemption is what makes the whole-fall promise true at the ceiling (the bound is derived under F-CAM-04; every other phase — the landing retarget, the rising tween, cling — stays capped). **Landing re-anchors** to the floor anchor in one retarget — never a stack: the grounded target is set and the position converges to it by F-CAM-02; the retarget's magnitude is the bias flip (72 px at the saturated bias, D ≥ 96) less whatever falling-lag the tween still carried (a tween fully caught up to the falling target gives the full 72-px class — the AC-CAM-21 fixture seeds exactly that ideal), capped by `MAX_STEP_H` on the grounded side. **No separate keep-in-frame backstop rule exists** (the hard-inset alternative was considered and rejected in favor of this model — the video is the design source). *Test: the phase fixtures (grounded bias; apex centre; full-fall offset); the landing-merge fixture; the cling-bias fixture (a wall slide classifies cling — the eye does not dive); the falling-lag fixture at the terminal ceiling.*

- **TR-CAM-008 — The long-fall look-down (the falling phase's completion).** The falling tween completes at **`LONG_FALL_PX` (96)** cumulative downward displacement since the last vertical anchor event — full below-feet offset `LOOKDOWN_PX` (64). 96 px engages before her body would sink past the frame's bottom band (~135 px to the frame edge; the mobile thumb band makes a "technically visible" fall effectively invisible well before that). Displacement counters reset on any grounded tick and on the knockback-family lock's start. Falls shorter than 96 px get a proportional tip; the law is nearly inert in the shallow rooms (RM-01/02/03 clamp it to a no-op) and visible in RM-04/06. *Test: the 96-px threshold fixture; the proportional-tip fixture; the counter-reset fixtures.*

- **TR-CAM-009 — The look-ahead commit law.** The lead is a **discrete committed offset** (`LOOKAHEAD_PX` 32, sign = the facing value): a facing change *proposes*; the proposal commits after `COMMIT_DELAY_MS` (150) of unchanged value on running clock; **the countdown restarts when the proposal changes** (no commit during turn-dancing) and never restarts while it continues. The commit moves the *target*, not the camera — the glide is the follow law's. Stopping never un-commits (facing is binary; the eye stays ahead of the last read). **Cause-blind**: causes are SYS-06's business — the camera reads values and locks only, so a `WALL_TURN` re-commits naturally after the delay with zero wall-specific code (SYS-06 publishes standing-turn facing as an ordinary `INPUT_TURN` value change — TR-MOV-006 — the camera needs no turn taxonomy). *Test: the commit fixture (flip at T → commit at T+9 — 9 elapsed ticks = 150.0 ms, 10 tick-instants counting the flip tick); the restart fixture (R→L→R never commits); the stop fixture.*

- **TR-CAM-010 — The look-ahead freeze law.** While **any** `body_busy` lock level is active (Movement's {backdash, dash, knockback, knockdown, getup, scripted, `freeze_hold`} + Combat's paired commitments), the FSM **holds its current state verbatim** — offset, proposal, countdown — and resumes (never drops) at lock release (the tick-open snapshot at `end_tick` is already free, TR-MOV-033). **One exception: the knockback/knockdown family drops the offset target to 0 at the lock's `start_tick`** (eased under `ALPHA_H`) — a hit re-centers the eye on the body; the bias re-commits afterward under the ordinary window. Death is the freeze family's terminal member: the camera holds on the body. *Test: the hold-through-dash fixture (invisible by construction — no steering mid-dash); the knockback-drop fixture; the resume fixture; the death-hold fixture.*

- **TR-CAM-011 — The room-swap law.** At SYS-02's commanded swap tick the camera's sequence is: **assert bounds-first** (the new room's `RoomBounds` generation is already published — TR-RWS-006's precondition as an enforceable invariant) → derive the new limits via F-RWS-02 → re-anchor the target at the re-anchored body (lead reset to neutral, vertical re-anchored, zone state cleared and re-evaluated next tick from the live `arena_hold` level — a spawn inside the arena zone re-locks next tick, the level being already active) → **kill the glide**: `pos = target` exactly, both axes, residual zeroed — one-tick teleport, atomic with the limits swap, so **no tick exists with new limits and an old position**: no straddle, no glide, by construction (the camera-side meaning of `reset_smoothing()`; we own the smoothing). A **freeze guard** asserts no swap under hit-stop. The camera node is **persistent across rooms** (rooms instantiate around it). **The every-tick no-straddle assert's predicate:** `view_rect ⊆ [limit_left, limit_right) × [limit_top, limit_bottom)` — the view stays inside the **limit** rect (the room rect when the room ≥ the view; on a sub-view axis the centered branch's deliberate overhang is backdrop-covered — the assert binds to the limit rect, not the room rect). *Test: the swap fixture (zero straddle frames; the glide-kill assertion; the freeze-guard assert); the every-tick no-straddle assert.*

- **TR-CAM-012 — The arena hold law (HOLD_ZONE — the OoE stage).** SYS-14 publishes **`arena_hold {zone_id, active}`** every physics tick — a **level**, idempotent, the TR-MOV-022 pattern (set membership, never an event): the FSM **derives** HOLD_ZONE from the boolean (active ∧ the zone belongs to the current room), and release is the boolean's fall. On the level's rise the camera approaches the **fixed single-screen stage point** — the zone's center clamped to the room limits, here RM-06's center **(3520, 176)** — by the **timed linear interpolation** (F-CAM-06's approach law; 24 ticks at `HOLD_EASE_MS` 400, exact arrival) and **fixes there for the fight**: follow, look-ahead and vertical laws all suspended (zone-as-subject); shake still applies, clamped. This is the **Order of Ecclesia pattern**: the boss room is a stage the camera never scrolls during the fight — OoE authors its boss rooms to fit the screen; RM-06 is already authored 640×352 in the APPROVED SYS-02 GDD, so the recorded analog is the **staging-band constraint**: SYS-14/level-design keep the fight inside the held frame's interior (≈ 448×222 px — x ∈ [3296, 3744], y ∈ [65, 287]; **the interior margin is 24 px vertical — the thumb-relevant axis, widened from 16 by the C2 repair — and 16 px horizontal**), and the duty covers **every fight participant and threat effect** — both fighters, projectiles, hazards — not only the boss anchor; the player's reachable space is sealed to the band (the arena's playable interior authored within it), so a reachable side strip is an authoring defect against this row, never a designed state (the honest `on_screen = false` publication and the fallback knob remain the robustness path). **The thumb-band authored constraint (priced here, not at SYS-14 authoring):** the arena's playable floor is authored inset above SYS-24's thumb-band ceiling (the widened 24-px margin leaves her feet 24 px from the frame bottom at the band's low extreme); **the reveal's cannot be** — its floor is the held frame's bottom edge (her feet walk the lowest ~2–16 px of the screen through the narrated hold; the hold point is clamp-pinned and cannot rise), so the reveal's exposure is structural and recorded: the risk is carried by the SYS-24 hold-mode contract row (below) + the touch-rig tester duty (the playtest protocol) — the greybox verifies the arena's floor inset and the reveal's recorded exposure. Release (the level's fall) returns to **fresh-FOLLOW**: the lead resets to neutral, any half-counted commit proposal is discarded (the countdown is dead), and the position converges to the body anchor under F-CAM-02 from rest — the post-fight re-center glide (typically ≤ 64 px → ≈ 25 ticks) is visible; SYS-14's victory presentation owns any masking beat (the OQ-RWS-8 rider). **No lock queue exists** — the level model has nothing to enqueue, dedupe, drop or acknowledge; a second active `zone_id` while a hold is live is a dev assert (MVP authors one arena). If the band is violated at runtime, the publication stays honest (`on_screen = false`); the **`ARENA_FOLLOW_FALLBACK` knob** (default off, boolean) re-seats the hold target on the body clamped inside the zone's center range — the robustness path, shipped off. *Test: the level fixtures (rise → hold → release; the idempotent re-publish is a no-op; the nothing-to-lose property — there is no event to drop); the approach fixture (exact arrival at tick 24); the fallback fixture (off: the stage holds against a band violation; on: the re-seat); the staging-band validator fixture.*

- **TR-CAM-013 — The reveal hold law (REVEAL_HOLD).** The trigger is SYS-02's arc-start command for the **`reveal_cistern` first crossing** — the first crossing **at/after the truth-epoch arm** (SYS-02's precondition, TR-RWS-009: the zone gate is SEALED at epoch 0, so no crossing can occur pre-arm — the epoch gate lives in SYS-02; the dev assert flags any pre-arm arc command as SYS-02's regression). ## Formulas

Three law families govern every formula: **L1 — the D1 integer law** (every camera quantity is a signed integer in hundredths of an art pixel — 1 hundredth = 0.01 px; constant names carry the `_H` suffix (`ALPHA_H_H`, `MAX_STEP_H`, `SNAP_EPS_H`; TR-CAM-006's prose `ALPHA_H`/`MAX_STEP` names the same values); all arithmetic integer; **the move's division truncates toward zero** — the magnitude `|gap| × ALPHA ÷ 100` floors and the sign reapplies (GDScript's sign-preserving int division; the pinned trace semantic — every printed fixture below reproduces under it); **the emit's division floors** (`base_px = pos_h ÷ 100` — the VS negative-origin rooms' floor-div emit semantics are flagged there); **no float equality on the camera path** — arrival tests use the explicit threshold `SNAP_EPS_H` 50; the truncation remainder stays in the gap (`target − pos`) — the residual *is* the accumulator; `pos ∈ [center_min, center_max] × 100` per axis always (the clamp domain — origin-agnostic: the MVP room set is positive-origin, VS rooms with negative origins drive `pos`/`view_rect` legally negative, with floor-div emit semantics flagged for VS); the render value is `base_px = pos_h ÷ 100`), **L2 — the game-clock law** (the registry's `ms_to_ticks` ceil family, cited not redefined — ms is the contract, ticks derived by the never-stingy ceil on the active rate; every tick figure re-derives on a rate change), and **L3 — the command law** (every behavior change arrives as data stamped with its tick, consumed at a physics-tick boundary, mutating state atomically within that tick — only this tick's publications are consumed, no extrapolation between ticks, ever; all stochastic behavior seeds from command data through a core-owned xorshift, never wall-clock — bit-identical replay).

#### F-CAM-02 · follow_step — the one convergent smoothing law (TR-CAM-003/006)

## Edge Cases

*Format: If [condition] → [exact outcome]. [rationale where non-obvious.]*

### Bounds that lie

- **If the bounds-first assert fails at the commanded swap tick** (the new room's `RoomBounds` generation absent, stale, wrong-room or malformed) → the swap cannot complete: dev assert + telemetry, fail-loud; production defers the swap tick-by-tick, executing on the first tick the assert passes, the view staying inside the old room's limits (the every-tick no-straddle assert still holds; `on_screen` may read false; no re-anchor, no glide). The commanded tick is the *earliest* tick, not the exact one. [TR-CAM-004/011]
- **If a second `RoomBounds` publish lands mid-room** → same generation, identical rect: idempotent no-op (the port dedupes on generation). New generation mid-room: dev assert naming the regression; production **adopts** — limits re-derive via F-RWS-02, both clamps re-apply within the same tick, no swap, **no glide — the re-clamp may move `pos` by up to the rect delta as a single whole-px correction, asserted as a clamp, not a smooth move**; SYS-24's caps re-derive from the live publication (never cached); **the consumer detects the adopt by `bounds_generation`'s change** (the 9th field). Same generation, different rect: a corrupt publish — rejected into the row above's path. [TR-CAM-006/015]

### Swaps under fire

- **If the swap commands while HOLD_ZONE is active** → the hold **force-exits as part of the swap action itself**, same tick: TR-CAM-011 runs, zone state cleared (the level model has no queue to clear); SYS-14's level re-locks next tick for any new-room hold (unreachable in authored content — the arena seals). [TR-CAM-011/012]
- **If SYS-14's release (the level's fall) races the swap tick** → both orders converge to exactly one re-anchor: release-first = at most one tick of fresh-FOLLOW inside the arena before the swap; swap-first = the release is a no-op (the hold already force-exited; the level read finds nothing active). No old-room frame publishes after the swap; no double approach. [TR-CAM-011/012]
- **If the swap commands mid-arc (REVEAL_HOLD)** → the true gate entry (TR-MOV-029) kills the arc and the approach mid-flight; **the exit processes on the first elapsed tick after the gate** (the advance cannot run on a frozen tick) → FOLLOW **seeded at the current position, no snap**, `active_hold` cleared — the vanished arc-end cannot deadlock the camera. Any other mid-arc swap is **unreachable by construction** (the body is on SYS-02's scripted-move lock); the no-swap-during-arc dev assert is the defence-in-depth. [TR-CAM-013; TR-MOV-029]
- **If the swap tick arrives at the freeze/lock edges** → (a) *hit-stop spanning the commanded tick*: `advance_world()` runs zero times — the swap structurally cannot run; the freeze guard marks the deferral; the swap executes on the first unfrozen tick. (b) *a `body_busy` lock ending exactly on the swap tick*: `end_tick` is already tick-open (TR-MOV-033), the swap proceeds same tick — and **the re-anchor wins over the FSM resume** (lead neutral, vertical re-anchored; a held countdown survives the seam and re-commits through the ordinary window if the facing held). (c) *a hold level already active stamped the same tick as the swap*: zone state re-evaluates next tick — one tick of seeded FOLLOW publishes between the swap and the approach, by design. [TR-CAM-002/010/011; TR-MOV-033]

### When the world stops

- **If hit-stop starts mid-commit-countdown** → the count holds its value and resumes — never drops, never expires; no restart race exists (the facing cannot flip during the freeze — ADR §5's invariant), so resume counts the remainder to 9 and commits. [TR-CAM-002]
- **If hit-stop meets the holds** → mid-reveal-approach: the approach freezes mid-flight (the advance row gates off), the arc freezes on the same clock gate so the **exact arrival at the midpoint is preserved in game-time** (the lerp's `t` counts elapsed advance-phase ticks only — no deadline breach, no catch-up burst); the frozen frame still publishes every tick and still trembles (the shake is the one freeze-blind channel); resume continues tick-by-tick. Mid-HOLD_ZONE: nothing visible halts — the held frame is already still, the shake keeps trembling, release restores the identical hold (no re-approach). [TR-CAM-002/014/015]

### Death and the outside world

- **If death lands mid-look-down** → at the death lock's `start_tick` the offset target drops to 0 (eased) and the D counter resets (the knockback-family reset) — the look-down bias collapses with the offset; the camera converges onto the body and holds; the countdown dies permanently (no resume, no re-commit). [TR-CAM-010; F-CAM-04]
- **If death lands during HOLD_ZONE** → the hold is **untouched by death** (only a room swap force-exits a hold): the death lock plays inside the held frame, the stage point never moves; exit comes from SYS-14's release (boss reset) or the respawn swap (revive *is* the swap mechanism → force-exit) — both end in FOLLOW. [TR-CAM-012]
- **If a non-GAMEPLAY context arrives mid-hold** → the asymmetry is the spec: `REVEAL_HOLD`'s exit processes **on the first elapsed tick after the non-GAMEPLAY interval** — FOLLOW seeded, no snap (the arc died on the context exit, TR-MOV-029; no re-approach on resume; SYS-02 must re-request). The frozen interval keeps publishing `mode = REVEAL_HOLD` + the frozen position + the live `tick` (the stall is readable); SYS-10/11 must not act on `active_hold` across the gate. `HOLD_ZONE` **persists** — the level is SYS-14's game-clocked state and the fight freezes with the world; resume continues the identical hold. Principle: REVEAL_HOLD follows its arc's death; HOLD_ZONE follows the level's lifecycle. [TR-CAM-002/013]
- **If the app blurs / lifecycle suspends** → zero ticks elapse — the state is bit-stable across the suspension: no catch-up, no wall-clock debt; the shake schedule freezes with the ticks and resumes mid-envelope. If the platform maps blur to a designed gate, the non-GAMEPLAY rules above apply — the camera has no blur-specific code and needs none. [TR-CAM-001/002]

### What the camera believes

- **If the `position` rider has not landed** (boot before the first publication; a tick whose BodyPort lacks a valid body-center **or a valid hurtbox extent**) → **the camera never fabricates a position or an extent** — no zero-default, no spawn-guess. Dev: assert + telemetry (the missing-bounds pattern). Production: the camera **activates on the first tick carrying valid bounds ∧ valid position ∧ a valid hurtbox extent** — the publication binds **from activation**; before it, the boot frame is presentation's, not the camera's. After activation, a lost rider freezes the body-driven pipeline for that tick (bit-stable, like a freeze tick) while command-driven channels continue; **a lost or invalid extent publishes `on_screen = false` for that tick** — the conservative default (SYS-24's caps treat her as off-frame and keep guarding; never a stale or fabricated true) — while the rest of the publication stays live, and the recovery is the extent's first valid tick (no activation restart, no snap); consumers detect the stall via the `tick` field. [TR-CAM-006/015; the recorded rider]
- **If `facing` is absent at boot** → FOLLOW runs (position-driven) but the commit window stays idle — no proposal can form without a facing value; the first valid facing opens the ordinary 9-tick window from its arrival tick; boot frames are centered, zero offset. [TR-CAM-009]
- **If the body's position delta is zero/garbled mid-fall, or a wall touch arrives without the toward-wall order** → the falling tween's D stagnates (no motion — the position failures ride the missing-rider path's freeze); the phase key classifies a wall touch without toward-wall as airborne (rising/falling by the Δy sign) — the camera consumes the wall state only as the phase key's two bits (wall ∧ toward-wall), never as a wall order, and consumes `velocity` by no law (the tween is Δy-driven from published positions). [TR-CAM-006/007]
- **If two `body_busy` locks overlap** (SYS-06-impossible; the FSM's defensive answer) → the freeze applies while *any* level is active and ends when the *last* ends; the knockback-family drop fires at the family level's `start_tick` (a second family start re-fires onto an already-0 target — idempotent); `authority` publishes the top present class; the dev assert flags the single-active-level violation to SYS-06's telemetry. Deterministic even from a buggy FSM. [TR-CAM-010/015]

### The one slot

- **If a request arrives outside the contract** (context ≠ GAMEPLAY; magnitude > `SHAKE_MAX_PX`; magnitude 0) → three guards, one shape: non-GAMEPLAY — SYS-29 drops at source, a leak is rejected by the executor's context guard; over-cap — SYS-29 clamps at source, a leak is clamped to 3 by the executor (the clamp is SYS-32's authoritative act); zero — outside F-CAM-05's declared domain, rejected, not executed. All three: dev assert + telemetry, **no slot entry, no xorshift advance**. [TR-CAM-014; TR-JCE-026]
- **If a cancel arrives with no active request, or keyed to a foreign source** → no-op — the slot is source-keyed; a stray cancel cannot kill an active shake. **Same-tick self-cancel** → the cancel clears on the next physics tick by contract — exactly one decay step publishes, then the slot is clear; no intra-tick mutation path exists, so there is no ordering race to lose. [TR-CAM-014]
- **If a stronger request and the compose land on the same tick** → no race: the tick order is fixed — drain (ingest → replace-resolve) → advance → compose → publish. Strictly stronger same-tick: replaces before compose (it shakes *this* tick, restarting t = 0 with the new seed); equal within the incumbent's first step (t = 0): first-come keeps (the incumbent composes); equal at t ≥ 1: the retrigger is honored (restarts t = 0); weaker: dropped + telemetry without touching the compose. [TR-CAM-014; F-CAM-05]

### Honest frames

- **If a consumer reads the publication on the swap tick** → it publishes the **post-swap frame atomically** (the action completes pre-emit): one room's truth — no tick exists with new limits and an old position; view-relative coordinates carry no room identity (identity lives in `view_rect_px` + `bounds_generation` — both published fields), so the entry "jump" is the re-anchor, correct by construction. [TR-CAM-011/015]
- **If a consumer reads during the reveal approach** → `on_screen` reports the truth per tick — she can sit at or past the frame edge (the hold point frames the wing, not her): a transient false is *designed behavior*, and the arc window is **threat-free** (TR-CAM-013's recorded duty — a transient-false frame contains no live strike, so the framed-zone guarantee holds); SYS-10/11 key on `active_hold`/the epoch, never on `on_screen`; SYS-24 derives from the live value, never cached. [TR-CAM-013/015; F-CAM-06]
- **If the hold announcement arrives before the approach completes** → `active_hold` publishes from the arc-start tick through approach and hold alike (`start_tick` = the command's tick): SYS-10/11 stage the truth-flip against the *announced* hold while the frame is still settling — nobody infers hold state from motion. [TR-CAM-015]

### The law's teeth

- **If a fractional px write or an inverted limit arrives** → fractional write: the ports quantize at the boundary; an *internal* fractional write trips the dev assert and is rejected (the hundredths accumulator is the only fractional carrier). Limit inversion: impossible from valid input — F-CAM-01's construction cannot invert; an inverted result means a garbled rect, failing validation into the missing-bounds path. Both asserts name the offending source. `pos`'s invariant is the **clamp domain** (`pos ∈ [center_min, center_max] × 100` per axis — origin-agnostic; the MVP room set is positive-origin, VS rooms with negative origins drive `pos`/`view_rect` legally negative, with floor-div emit semantics flagged for VS). ### Shape classes
