## Overview

Room & World Structure is the stage the trust contract is performed on: it owns the 16px tile grid the whole art doctrine normalizes to, the **room graph** (rooms as nodes, borders as edges, doors as locked borders), the **room-bounds contract** that makes the camera's limits the room's honest word, the **dual-truth data model** (one geometry, two palettes — a `truth_epoch` integer plus named truth flags, with mutations keyed to rooms), the **story-threshold flip** and the **backtrack reveal** it enables, the **hazard surface** that publishes SYS-07's `ENVIRONMENT` events (the pit: `raw_damage = hp_max`, bypassing i-frames and defense, through the death edge), the **spawner surface** that hands SYS-13's scheduler a priority-ordered spawn list and owns the 5th-spawn refusal, and the **interactable placements** (the cache terminal, the memory fragments, the logs, the doors) that publish prompts to SYS-01's Prompt Slot. It integrates the owned **Metroidvania System 1.6.1** addon (KoBeWi) — map data, borders, room instancing, discovery, object IDs, save-Dictionary — behind a port so the core stays pure, and answers at the data level what MetSys leaves open: which transitions are gated, what resets on a room load, what the camera clamps to, and which facts are world truth. It never reads input, never owns HP, never resolves combat, never renders the flip's drama. Without it the world is a hallway: no honest bounds, no persistent truth, no reveal worth backtracking for.

→ *Implementation patterns (`WorldCore` reducer, the `MetSysPort` boundary, the room-data tables, the transition choreography) are ADR raw material for the planned **World Structure / MetSys Integration ADR**; this GDD stays at the behavior level. The addon pin is **Metroidvania System 1.6.1** (KoBeWi) on **Godot 4.7.2** — the integration smoke-test gate is this GDD's device AC (TR-RWS-027), per the systems-index risk row.*

## Player Fantasy

### The named fantasy — The World Keeps Its Word, Even When It Lies

Every room here is an honest space. The walls are where the walls are drawn; the door opens where the door says it opens; when you walk off a ledge the world below you is the world you saw. The camera's frame is the room's word — what you see is where you stand, and nothing hides outside it. And the system's second promise is harder and stranger: **the world can change, and when it does, it changes everywhere at once, honestly**. The zone's truth flips and every room re-materializes — the same arches, the same stairs, the same distances, wrong materials now — and when you walk back the way you came, the room you crossed minutes ago is *wrong now*, and a wall you leaned on is a passage. The reveal is never hidden: the world shows you its new shape, holds the frame on it for a breath, and lets you walk through. The lie is the fiction's (a zone wearing a truth it does not have); the system never lies about mechanics.

### The contract — four terms, each testable

1. **The room is the world's word.** Every room's bounds are a tile-multiple rect published as the camera's default limits ("room limits first"); the camera never straddles two rooms; the transition re-anchors the body at the opposite anchor atomically. What you see is where you stand — no mid-air re-anchoring, no half-rooms, no silent teleports.
2. **A door is a promise, kept once.** A locked border shows its prompt (`OPEN`, or its state reason in Class B); the open is a committed arc (`DOOR_OPEN_MS` ≤ 1200 — the body finishes what it signed); the opened door is a passage forever after (world truth). Nobody re-picks the same lock.
3. **The truth is one, and it is whole.** The zone's truth is one integer epoch + named flags — stored as sources, derived everywhere else. The flip applies atomically: every room re-materializes at one tick boundary; there is never a room in the old truth beside a room in the new (except the authored seam, which is neither truth — the liminal corridor — and the one authored pocket, the Denial arena's pristine lie, which is room data, marked, and SYS-09/10's to render).
4. **The reveal is shown, not sprung.** The backtrack reveal opens a passage that did not exist; the first crossing is a held, framed arc — the world waits while you see what changed. The memory counter is the carrot that sends you back; the corridor is neither truth; what waits there is the reward for curiosity, and nothing about it is a trap.

### Anchor moment — The Threshold

The Sanctum's log sits beside the terminal. You read it — and the words undo the world. The zone re-materializes around you: candlelit where the cold steel was, the same arches, the same stairs, all of it *wrong now*. The room did not change shape. You did not move. The truth did. Nothing announced itself with a cutscene; the world simply stopped pretending, everywhere at once.

### Anchor moment — The Wrong Corridor

You go back for the memory you didn't find — the counter said 3 of 4 — and the Cistern you left is not the Cistern you remember. Candlelight where the monitor-glow was. And there, on the back wall you pressed against while dodging the Spitter: a passage. Beyond it a corridor the color of neither truth — the grey of a place between places, a purple bridge holding it together — and the last memory at its end. The backtracking wasn't busywork; it was the second half of the story.

### Language law

**Use:** room, border, passage, door, bound, truth, epoch, material, reveal, threshold, wrong now, the corridor.
**Ban:** teleport, warp, streaming, chunk, load screen, "loading", respawn point, spawn camp, cutscene.
Player-facing copy never names the machinery: a door transition is "passing through"; the flip is the world's truth changing; the reconstruct is SYS-15's fiction. Falsifiability rule: every feel-sentence must be verifiable by playtest or build check; if verifying it would need a stopwatch on streaming hitches, cut it.

### Success signature

Playtesters say: "I went back and the cistern was wrong — I found a new way through"; "doors are doors — I always knew where a passage was"; "when the world changed, everything changed at once"; "the frame never fought me — the room *was* the screen". **Anti-signatures** (the failure modes this GDD exists to prevent): "I fell through the world" (the bounds or the hazard edge lied), "the door ate me" (the swap misplaced the body), "the flip changed one room but not the corridor" (the epoch applied partially), "I got lost between rooms" (the transition dropped a frame of world), "I couldn't tell the new passage from a wall" (the reveal was hidden, not shown).

*Pillar ties: "One World, Two Truths" (the epoch + the mutation registry are the doctrine's data home; "same geometry, wrong world" is enforced as a validated invariant, not an art hope) · "Remade Every Death" (the reconstruct keeps the world's word — doors stay open, truths stay flipped; the designation counter is SYS-15's) · "SotN, Not Hollow Knight" (room-based exploration with deliberate doors; the bounds make distances learnable in strides) · "Five Goodbyes" (the four placed memories are the MVP's map-of-her-mind — the first word of the goodbye count).*

## Detailed Design

### Core Rules

**Rule IDs (`TR-RWS-*`) are stable requirement proposals — each carries a falsifiable test and is ready for `tr-registry.yaml` ingestion.** PROVISIONAL marks indicate contracts whose owning GDD is not yet authored; ownership boundaries are intended final. **No direct `Input` reads; no HP writes; no enemy behavior; no camera logic; no flip drama.**

#### 1 · Ownership — consume MetSys, publish the contracts

- **TR-RWS-001 — Consume MetSys, publish the contracts, never own what the contracts assign.** SYS-02 consumes the owned **Metroidvania System 1.6.1** addon (KoBeWi) for what it provides — the map editor's cell/border/room data, room↔scene association, the runtime scene-change API, room discovery, storable-object IDs, and its save-data Dictionary — and **publishes the game's contracts** on top of it: the bounds, the truth model, the hazards, the spawns, the prompts, the transitions. **One detector, one owner:** SYS-02 owns the crossing detection (its own border areas, physics-driven overlap); **MetSys's border-walk scene-change request is consumed as a signal only** — SYS-02's detector decides, SYS-02 calls the scene-change API; the addon never triggers a swap of its own accord. It **never** owns HP (SYS-07's pool; hazards publish events, never write it), never resolves an exchange (SYS-08's probe), never owns enemy behavior (SYS-13's; the spawn list is data), never reads `Input.*` (SYS-01's Prompt Slot is the only Interact consumer; SYS-02 publishes prompts and consumes acks routed back), never owns the camera (SYS-32's; the bounds are data), never renders the flip's drama (SYS-09/10's; the epoch is data). The addon is a provision, not an authority: **where MetSys's defaults and the game's contracts differ, the contract wins** (the save path is SYS-03's — MetSys's Dictionary is written by the adapter, TR-RWS-025; the discovery *policy* is SYS-25's, deferred — the discovery *facts* are SYS-02's source family at MVP). *Test: `src/world/` contains no `Input.` symbol, no HP-write symbol, no camera-write symbol, no hitbox-resolution symbol, and no enemy-behavior symbol (CI grep); a mocked MetSys port reproduces the scripted trace (AC-RWS-25) and the mock ≡ live-addon equivalence is the integration gate's (AC-RWS-28).*

- **TR-RWS-002 — The one tile grid.** `TILE_PX = 16` is fixed; every authored geometry rect — room bounds, hazard rects, border openings, anchor positions, interactable positions — is a whole-tile multiple. The visual identity's normalization audit ("single tile grid, shared outline color, harmonized saturation before promotion from `inbox/`") has its **data home here**: one grid means one conversion law (F-RWS-01) and no per-pack grid drift. Rooms are authored on the grid; the MetSys cell is a *map* unit, not a *tile* unit (see TR-RWS-004). *Test: the grid fixture converts tile↔world exactly; a non-multiple rect fails the dev boot naming the record (V-RWS-01).*

#### 2 · The room graph and the room record

- **TR-RWS-003 — The room graph.** The world is a graph: **nodes are rooms, edges are borders** (a shared, flush wall segment between adjacent rooms — the adjacency law is V-RWS-16); a **door is a locked border** (TR-RWS-007); the graph is **pure data** — authored tables, not scene wiring. Every edge is complete: both sides carry exit anchors, and no passage leads to nowhere (MetSys's own map validation is invoked at boot as a second net). Border openings respect the body: **≥ 2 tiles wide (32 px) and ≥ 3 tiles tall (48 px)** — the standing body (44 px tall, `STAND_HEIGHT`; the stand width pins ≤ 24 px at the art pass — recorded assumption) clears a 48 × 32 px opening with margin on both axes, so no scripted arc ever meets a no-headroom refusal inside its own doorway. Anchors are **safe**: ≥ 2 tiles from any hazard rect, ≥ 1 tile from any void border, interior to the room — no spawn ever re-kills. *Test: the graph fixture validates completeness, anchor safety, opening sizes and id uniqueness; a passage-to-nowhere fails the boot (V-RWS-03/12/16).*

- **TR-RWS-004 — The room record.** Every room registers exactly: `RoomData {room_id, cell_coord (exactly one MetSys cell — V-RWS-04), local_rect (tiles within the cell), bounds_px (derived, F-RWS-01), zones: [ZoneRect {zone_id, rect (tiles), material_rule}] (a room may carry sub-zone rects — the Cold Corridor is RM-03's; an unmaterialized zone renders its tiles in the current epoch's enclosing wall material — it never renders as a hole), borders: [BorderData], zone_gates: [ZoneGateData], spawns: [SpawnEntry], hazards: [HazardData], interactables: [InteractableData], truth_flags_touched: [the flags this room's content reads or writes], void_borders: [the sides whose bound is a void], framing_zones: [FramingZone {zone_id, rect, trigger}], epoch_pin: null (PROVISIONAL — SYS-09/10's field; the Denial arena pins epoch 0; V-RWS-17)}`. **Sub-records are typed in full:** `BorderData {border_id, kind: DOOR|PASSAGE (the epoch-keyed inter-room truth kind is VS-reserved — it is not in the MVP union), from_room, to_room, side (the shared cell edge), opening_rect (tiles, on the shared edge), from_anchor, to_anchor, label, state_source}`; `FramingZone` is above; `ZoneGateData {gate_id, partition_rect (tiles, interior), room_id, to_epoch, opening_rect}`. A missing or invalid field is a dev boot failure, never a silent default. *Test: the six-room fixture registers whole; a stripped fixture fails the boot naming the field.*

#### 3 · The room-bounds contract (SYS-32's, made contractual)

- **TR-RWS-006 — The bound is per-room, and it is the camera's default limits.** The bound's shape is a **per-room rect** (`bounds_px`, F-RWS-01) — not per-zone: the zone is the truth/epoch scope (TR-RWS-013), the room is the camera scope. **"Room limits first"** is contractual: on every swap tick SYS-02 publishes `RoomBounds {room_id, rect_px, spawn_anchors, framing_zones}` on **`BoundsPort`** **before the camera's first tick in the room**, and SYS-32's default limits are exactly the rect (F-RWS-02). When a room is smaller than the view (a framing-zone room), the rect-exact rule switches to the centered rule and the room's backdrop covers the slack — the rule, not a hack. The **handoff is atomic**: the swap tick publishes the new bounds in the same tick; the camera never straddles two rooms; the follow runs on the game clock (SYS-32's plan, consumed). **Minimum extent:** every room is ≥ `ROOM_MIN_TILES` (30×17 tiles = 480×272 px — the 480×270 view fits with 2 px of vertical scroll) **unless the room is registered with a framing zone** (no MVP room needs the exception — RM-06 satisfies both clauses; the branch is exercised by the F-RWS-02 fixture). *Test: the bounds fixture per room; the handoff fixture (no straddle; the bounds message recorded on the mock's `BoundsPort`); the extent law's exception path (V-RWS-11).*

#### 4 · Borders, zone gates, transitions

- **TR-RWS-007 — The border state machine (inter-room edges).** Every inter-room edge is a border in exactly one of two **derived** states: **LOCKED** (the crossing is solid; the physics collision holds) or **OPEN** (a passage — walk-through). Unlock sources, by border kind: **DOOR** — the border's id in `doors_opened` (world truth, set **at the open ack**); **PASSAGE** — always open; **TRUTH_BORDER** (VS-reserved — the epoch-keyed inter-room kind is recorded for the future; none ships at MVP, no MVP fixture: the MVP's truth edge is RM-03's intra-room zone gate). The border's state is **always derived** from the world truth at the current tick — never cached in the border (store sources, derive targets). *Test: the state fixture per kind; the derivation fixture (the state follows the truth the same tick — no cached flag).*

- **TR-RWS-008 — The prompted door.** A locked DOOR publishes a Prompt Slot prompt: **`OPEN`** (Class A, 4 chars) when actionable, or the **Class B state reason** when not — the hub door publishes **`LOCKED`** (6 chars, dimmed, the linear stub's boundary; pressing it binds per F-10 and the publisher **refuses** the open: telemetry, no arc, no unlock — the reason is the answer). The open: the Interact ack commits (ADR §6's ack-sourced row — the door's pull IS the ack commit, applying immediately, same tick) → **at the ack**: `doors_opened` gains the border id (world truth — the border derives OPEN the same tick; the collision releases; the prompt unlights — the door is now a passage being walked) → the **scripted move** begins (the door swings, she steps through): the arc's duration is **supplied by SYS-02 at registration** (`DOOR_OPEN_MS`), declared ≤ `SCRIPTED_MAX_MS` (1200) so Movement's derived `worst_case_arc_ms` (1200) stands unchanged — the door arc is a Movement scripted-move lock, consumed by the same `body_busy` OR (SYS-02 supplies the duration; Movement publishes the lock and the declaration per TR-INP-029). The **swap at the arc's midpoint** (F-RWS-03) behind the door frame; the placement at the opposite anchor. Subsequent crossings are plain walk-throughs (TR-RWS-010). *Test: the open fixture (the ack-tick truth write, the arc, the swap tick, the anchor placement); the Class B refusal fixture; the declaration fixture (V-RWS-13).*

- **TR-RWS-009 — The zone gate (the intra-room truth edge; no prompt — the pivot honored).** The MVP's one truth edge is **RM-03's interior partition** (the hall/back-wall between the main hall and the Cold Corridor wing): a **zone gate** — a solid segment *inside* a room whose collision derives from a mutation effect. States: **SEALED** (epoch < `to_epoch`: solid collision; the wing's tiles render in the enclosing hall's current-epoch wall material — **sealed, never absent**: the geometry exists in both epochs, byte-identical, so "a room's shape never changes" holds everywhere) or **OPEN** (epoch ≥ `to_epoch`: a passage). It publishes **no prompt at any epoch**: the design pivot's "no on-command reality shift" means the seam is never interactable at MVP — the world moves on its own (the threshold), and the gate simply *is* a passage once the epoch reaches its record. The **reveal's first crossing** runs the **reveal arc**: a scripted move (the same law as the door's — `REVEAL_ARC_MS` supplied at registration, ≤ 1200) with **no swap** (nothing to swap — it is intra-room): the collision changed, she steps through, the camera holds on the framing zone (SYS-32's), the world waits while you see what changed. After it, a plain walk-through. *Test: the no-prompt audit (zero prompt publications from zone gates at any epoch); the SEALED/OPEN fixture per epoch; the reveal-arc fixture (the ticks, the no-swap, the framing trigger).*

- **TR-RWS-010 — The transition mechanics (the crossing; the swap; the budgets).** All room-to-room movement at MVP is **border-walk**: on a physics tick where the body's hurtbox crosses an OPEN border's opening, **SYS-02's detector** (the physics-driven overlap — TR-RWS-001's one-owner law) fires and the **swap executes within that tick**: the destination room is **activated** (all six MVP rooms are **resident** — instantiated once at boot, deactivated when away; six rooms ≤ 640×352 px each is trivial memory), the old room's local state is discarded (TR-RWS-011), the body is **re-anchored** at the opposite side's exit anchor with **`v_x` zeroed** (the wall-clamp analog — Movement's own law clamps `v_x` to 0 at a wall and the lock continues) **and `v_y` zeroed** — a deliberate SYS-02 rule, *not* attributed to Movement (a crossing must not carry a fall into the next room) — **`facing` preserved, body state (locks, posture, HP) preserved** — the locks' remaining *time* continues, their *motion* is truncated by the zeroing. **The swap is not a gate:** the room transition raises no context change (SYS-05's contexts are untouched); **TR-MOV-029 applies only at true gates** (Reading, pause, death, the recorded TRANSITION fallback); the swap's only motion is the re-anchor — **a position assignment, not integration**. While a border is ARCING, crossing detection for that border is **suppressed** (the arc owns the swap — no double-swap). **The discovery request fires** (SYS-02's source family — TR-RWS-025). **The budgets, two mechanisms:** (a) the **activation swap** is bounded by the tick itself — asserted ≤ 16 ms (one tick at 60 tps) in the tour; (b) `TRANSITION_BUDGET_MS` (50) governs a **non-resident first-entry load** (never exercised at MVP — all rooms are resident; the guard for zone 2+, where a room is instantiated behind an arc or a pre-warm): an overflowing first-entry load is a dev assert + telemetry, with the recorded fallback (SYS-05's TRANSITION context — a non-GAMEPLAY context covering the load, riding the ADR's non-ack pattern) never silently entered at MVP. **ADR §6 ruling — no new row:** the door transition needs no gate-source row of its own; the door's pull rides the existing **ack-sourced bubble-confirm** row (applies immediately at the ack commit), and the border-walk crossing is not a gate at all (no context change). *Test: the crossing fixture (the tick boundaries, the anchor math, the `v_x`/`v_y` zeroing); the same-tick atomicity fixture; the ARCING-suppression fixture (no double-swap); the activation-tour (AC-RWS-26); the slow-port overflow fixture (AC-RWS-26's synthetic path); the body-state preservation fixture.*

#### 5 · Lifetime and truth

### States and Transitions

Two small machines and one transient: the border FSM (inter-room), the zone gate (intra-room), and the arc (a transition-controller transient — **never stored in the border or the gate**; the derived-state law holds because the arc is not state *of* the edge).

**The border FSM (inter-room edges).**

| State | Enter | Exit | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **LOCKED** | derived: authored default (DOOR) | the unlock source (the door's ack-set flag) | solid; the prompt (`OPEN` / Class B) |
| **ARCING** *(controller transient)* | **at the ack** (the door's open) | the arc's end | the scripted move; the swap at the midpoint; crossing detection suppressed; the body is locked |
| **OPEN** | derived: the flag set (at the ack); authored PASSAGE | never (world truth) | walk-through; no prompt |

**The zone gate (intra-room partitions).**

| State | Enter | Exit | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **SEALED** | derived: `epoch < to_epoch` | the epoch | solid; the wing renders the enclosing wall's material — sealed, never absent; **no prompt** |
| **ARCING** *(controller transient)* | **at the first crossing** post-unlock (the reveal arc) | the arc's end | the scripted move; **no swap** (intra-room); the framing trigger fires; crossing detection suppressed |
| **OPEN** | derived: `epoch ≥ to_epoch` | never | walk-through; no prompt |

**Transition laws.**

1. **The swap is atomic.** One physics tick: detect → activate → discard → re-anchor → publish bounds. No half-rooms, no straddling camera. The swap's only motion is the re-anchor — a position assignment, not integration.
2. **The truth is load-invariant.** No load changes truth except the explicit sources' writers (the door's flag at the ack; the log's flag at the Reading's close; the memory's write at the ack).
3. **The reveal is shown.** A zone gate's first crossing is an ARC; a silent opening is a defect, not a feature.
4. **True gates are honest (consumed).** At *true gate* entry (Reading, pause, death, the TRANSITION fallback), arcs die (TR-MOV-029); no motion on the transition frame; held states persist (SYS-05's contexts; ADR §6). The room transition itself is **not** a gate.
5. **Derive, never cache.** Border/gate states, mutation openings, materials: always derived from world truth at the current tick. The arc is a controller transient, not edge state.

**The room lifecycle.** `RESIDENT-INACTIVE → ACTIVE (the swap tick) → RESIDENT-INACTIVE` — local state exists only in ACTIVE; truth exists always.

## Formulas

Three laws govern every formula: **the grid law** (everything is a whole-tile multiple on `TILE_PX` 16), **the derivation law** (border/gate states, materials, openings are derived from world truth — never cached), and **the conversion law** (ms is the contract, ticks are derived by the never-stingy ceil on the active rate — a rate change re-baselines through ms).

**Formula ledger — law vs knob.**

#### F-RWS-01 · grid_to_world — the one conversion (TR-RWS-002)

`world_px = tile × TILE_PX` · `room_rect = Rect2(cell.x × CELL + local_x × 16, cell.y × CELL + local_y × 16, w × 16, h × 16)` with `CELL = CELL_IN_GAME_SIZE_PX = 640` (40×40 tiles per cell).

**Output at defaults:** RM-01 at cell (0,0), local (10,0), 30×17 tiles → `Rect2(160, 0, 480, 272)` — **flush east** (the rect's right edge 640 = cell 1's west edge). RM-02 at cell (1,0), local (0,0), 40×17 → `Rect2(640, 0, 640, 272)` — flush west (640) and east (1280 = RM-03's west). RM-04 → `Rect2(1920, 0, 640, 352)`; RM-06 → `Rect2(3200, 0, 640, 352)`. **The Cold Corridor wing:** RM-03 local (2, 11), 10×6 tiles → `Rect2(1312, 176, 160, 96)` — `reveal_cistern`'s framing rect is this rect. **Example:** tile (37, 9) inside RM-03 → world px `(1280 + 592, 144)` = **(1872, 144)**.

#### F-RWS-02 · bounds_to_limits — the camera's word (TR-RWS-006)

```
slack_x = max(0, VIEW_W − rect.w)        # VIEW_W = 480
slack_y = max(0, VIEW_H − rect.h)        # VIEW_H = 270
limit_left  = rect.x − floor(slack_x ÷ 2)   # integer px — floor (a half-pixel slack rounds down)
limit_top   = rect.y − floor(slack_y ÷ 2)
limit_right  = limit_left  + max(rect.w, VIEW_W)
limit_bottom = limit_top   + max(rect.h, VIEW_H)
```

**Output:** the standard rooms (≥ the view in both axes) yield the **rect-exact** limits — `limit = rect` edges. A framing-zone room smaller than the view (e.g., the 20×12-tile fixture alcove: rect 320×192) yields the **centered** limits — slack_x = 160 → 80 px beyond the rect, the camera holds center, the backdrop covers. **Example:** RM-02's `Rect2(640, 0, 640, 272)`: slack_x = 0, slack_y = 0 → limits (640, 0)…(1280, 272); the view's 270 px height against the 272 px room leaves 2 px of vertical scroll. RM-04's 352 px height: 82 px of vertical scroll.

#### F-RWS-03 · arc_timing — the arcs' shape (TR-RWS-008/009)

`arc_ticks = ceil(arc_ms × physics_tps ÷ 1000)` · **the door arc:** `DOOR_OPEN_MS` 700 → ceil(42) = **42 ticks (700.0 ms effective)**; the arc occupies ticks `T…T+41` (busy through `T+41` — the lock's level law, TR-MOV-022); the body is **free at `T+42`'s tick-open**; `swap_offset = floor(arc_ticks ÷ 2)` = 21 → the **swap tick = T + 21 (the arc's 22nd tick)** — behind the door frame. **The reveal arc:** `REVEAL_ARC_MS` 700 → the same 42 ticks; **no swap** (intra-room); the arc's completion frees the body at `T+42`. **The declaration bound:** every SYS-02-declared arc ≤ `ceil(SCRIPTED_MAX_MS × tps ÷ 1000)` = 72 ticks at 60 tps — **`DOOR_OPEN_MS` and `REVEAL_ARC_MS` ≤ 1200 (V-RWS-13) keep Movement's derived `worst_case_arc_ms` (1200) standing, unchanged**. The arcs run on **logic ticks** (freeze-paused — a hit-stop extends the arc's wall-clock; the resume completes the remaining duration, TR-MOV-024's analog). **Example:** the arena door (RM-05 → RM-06) opened at tick T: the arc runs T…T+41; the swap at T+21 behind the door frame; she stands at the arena's anchor from the swap; the lock's last busy tick is T+41; the body is free at T+42.

#### F-RWS-04 · truth_material — one geometry, two palettes (TR-RWS-012/013)

`material(zone, tick) = PALETTE_MAP[epoch_pin(zone) ?? epoch(tick)][zone.material_rule]` · `gate_open(m, epoch) = epoch ≥ m.to_epoch` · the epoch is **monotonic non-decreasing** (the runtime law); a flip request during an activation or an arc is applied **at that operation's completion boundary** (never mid-swap, never mid-arc). **Output:** epoch 0 → every unpinned zone at the reality-A map (steel cyan/teal, monitor-glow); epoch 1 → the reality-B map (amber candlelight, wine red) — **the tile data byte-identical**; the pinned arena (pin 0) renders the reality-A member at both epochs. **Example:** RM-03's zone gate: SEALED at epoch 0 (`0 ≥ 1` false — the wing renders the hall's current-epoch wall material, never a hole), OPEN at epoch ≥ 1 (`1 ≥ 1` true — the wing's liminal material rule resolves).

#### F-RWS-05 · hazard_enter — the pit's two triggers (TR-RWS-016)

**Branch (a) — the interior rect:** `triggered(t) = hazard_rect.intersects(player_hurtbox(t)) ∧ ¬triggered(t−1)` — the **enter-edge** (one event per overlap entry, never a per-tick poll). **Branch (b) — the void border:** `crossed(t) = body_beyond(declared_bound) ∧ ¬crossed(t−1)` — the **bound-crossing edge** (the bound IS the pit). Either branch publishes `DamageEvent {source: ENV, target: player, raw_damage: hp_max, kind: ENVIRONMENT, contact_tick: t}`. **Output:** the death edge fires through SYS-07's pipeline (the i-frame and defense bypass are SYS-07's TR-HD-018 law; the death latch rejects any second event). **Example:** she falls into the Courtyard's cistern shaft at tick 1200: one event, contact_tick 1200; the `DeathRecord` carries `cause_source = ENV`, `cause_kind = ENVIRONMENT`, `raw_damage = hp_max` (100 at the default profile), `overkill = 0` at full HP.

#### F-RWS-06 · spawn_admission — the cap handoff (TR-RWS-017)

`admitted = spawns[0 : min(len(spawns), MAX_ACTIVE_ENEMIES)]` · refused = `len(spawns) − len(admitted)` entries, **one telemetry record per refused entry** (the list order is the priority). **Output:** the shipped rooms admit all entries (**0/2/2/0/1/0** — five enemies total, max 2 per room, each ≤ 4); the fixture's 5-entry list admits 4 and refuses 1. **Example:** a synthetic room data fixture with 5 Husk entries → the first 4 spawn on load; the 5th is refused at load with exactly one telemetry record and no state mutation.

#### F-RWS-07 · load_budget — the transition's wall clocks (TR-RWS-010/027)

**Two mechanisms, two bounds.** (a) **The activation swap** (all MVP rooms resident): the swap is same-tick by law — asserted `swap_ms ≤ 16` (one tick at 60 tps) across the pinned tour. (b) **The first-entry load** (non-resident rooms — the zone-2+ guard, never exercised at MVP): `load_ms` measured per transition; the estimator is the **SYS-01 QA plan §5.3's nearest-rank with n ≥ 1,000 measured transitions**: `p99.9(load_ms) ≤ TRANSITION_BUDGET_MS` (50). An overflow fires the dev assert + one telemetry record (the recorded fallback: SYS-05's TRANSITION context — never silently entered at MVP); the deterministic **slow-port fixture** (a `MetSysPort` fake whose instantiation exceeds the budget) exercises the overflow path in GUT. **Example (illustrative, not a result):** greybox rooms instantiate in single-digit ms; the pinned 24-transition tour's activation swaps measure ≤ 16 ms each; the 50 ms budget carries the art pass's heavier rooms at zone 2+.

#### F-RWS-08 · room_extent — the view coverage (TR-RWS-006)

`room_tiles ≥ ROOM_MIN_TILES (30, 17)` **∨** `room.framing_zones ≠ ∅` — the 480×270 view fits a 480×272 room with 2 px of vertical scroll; a smaller room must be framing-zone-registered (the centered-limits branch of F-RWS-02 covers it). **Output:** **all six MVP rooms satisfy the first clause** (RM-06 satisfies both — 40×22 ≥ 30×17 *and* it carries the arena framing zone); the framing-zone branch is exercised by F-RWS-02's fixture alcove, not by an MVP room. **Example:** RM-04's 40×22 tiles (640×352) — 82 px of vertical scroll, the Sanctum's vertical read.

## Edge Cases

**Format: If [condition] → [exact outcome]. [rationale where non-obvious.]**

### The flip and the truth

- **If the threshold flag sets while a room activation is in flight** → the epoch applies at the activation's completion boundary (the swap tick's end): the new room materializes at epoch 1; the old room never renders at epoch 1. [TR-RWS-014; no mid-swap partial truth.]
- **If the threshold flag sets while a border arc is in flight** → the arc completes at its current geometry; the epoch applies at the arc's end; the destination room materializes at epoch 1. [TR-RWS-014; the body finishes what it signed.]
- **If the epoch flip's scripted sequence runs mid-hit-stop** → the flip's source joins SYS-29's freeze set; the glitch renders over the held world; the freeze outlasts per the set law. [TR-JCE-017's provisional; OQ-J1 — the shape is SYS-09/10's.]
- **If the player stands in the Cold Corridor when another flip would apply** (VS epochs) → the corridor's liminal material is epoch-independent (it is neither truth); the corridor persists. At MVP there is no second flip — the rule is recorded for SYS-10's authoring. [TR-RWS-022.]
- **If a save is taken before the reveal and loaded after the flip's threshold was crossed** → the save carries `truth_epoch` (the source); the gate re-derives open; the reveal state is never stored. Materials derive from the epoch regardless of the flag's value. [TR-RWS-025; store sources, derive targets.]

### Transitions and the body

- **If the swap detects the crossing while a Movement lock is active (a knockback through an open door)** → the swap re-anchors at the exit anchor, **zeroes `v_x` (the wall-clamp analog) and `v_y` (SYS-02's own rule — a crossing must not carry a fall), truncates the lock's motion, and the lock's remaining time continues**. The body is never flung across a room boundary. [TR-RWS-010; F-RWS-03's shape.]
- **If a knockback order lands on the player mid-border-arc** → **SYS-02 cancels its own scripted move on the order** (SYS-02 is a *consumer* of the `KnockbackOrder`, not its owner — the rule is SYS-02's, over Movement's lock composition; no Combat rule is invoked): the scripted lock ends, the knockback runs, she remains in the origin room, the door already open (the flag set at the ack). She walks through after the getup. The world does not cheat; the doorway is a choke point and the data says so honestly. [TR-RWS-008; the knockdown rule consumed.]
- **If the player presses Interact mid-border-arc** → **no prompt is lit during the arc** (the door derives OPEN at the ack — the prompt unlit from the ack tick), so the press **expires on its stamp tick** (TR-INP-030's unlit-binding law) — a press never binds into the next room, and never binds to a mid-arc door. [TR-RWS-018/008; SYS-01's F-10 law consumed.]
- **If a door is pressed during a SYS-05 context (Reading, pause)** → the offer never happens (F-05's context gate); the intent expires per its window. A door ack can only commit in GAMEPLAY. [TR-RWS-008; ADR §6.]
- **If the Class B locked door (the hub) is pressed** → the intent binds (the prompt is lit), the publisher **refuses**: one telemetry record, no arc, no unlock — the reason (`LOCKED`) is the answer, and the dimmed label never silently hides. [TR-RWS-008; SYS-01's disabled-vs-hidden law.]
- **If a first-entry load exceeds the transition budget** (never at MVP — all rooms resident; the zone-2+ guard) → dev assert + one telemetry record at authoring; production keeps the last good room set; the recorded fallback (SYS-05's TRANSITION context) is never silently entered at MVP. [TR-RWS-010; F-RWS-07.]
- **If the physics rate changes mid-session** → every ms contract re-derives by the ceil law (the arcs, the budgets measured in ms); no arc expires early or overstays from the rate event alone. [F-RWS-03/07.]
- **If the body is held by a freeze at a border** → no crossing starts (frozen bodies do not move — the detector runs on motion); an arc in flight when the freeze begins holds its remaining duration and resumes (TR-MOV-024's analog). The swap's crossing detection does not run while the body is held. [TR-RWS-010; TR-MOV-024 consumed.]

### Hazards

- **If the pit's rect is authored overlapping the room bound** → the dev boot fails (V-RWS-08's interior rule); the *bound* mechanism for edge pits is the room's `void_borders` declaration, a separate authored surface (F-RWS-05's branch b). [TR-RWS-016.]
- **If the player falls past a void border's bound** → the same ENVIRONMENT event publishes at the bound-crossing tick (the bound IS the pit). [TR-RWS-016; F-RWS-05(b).]
- **If the module revive fires on a pit death** ("returns to ALIVE on the spot", TR-HD-025) → **the placement question is gated, not silently resolved**: the death spot is inside the void, so an on-the-spot revive re-falls; SYS-02 publishes **`last_grounded_safe_pos`** (room-scoped — updated every grounded tick outside hazards; **re-initialized to the exit anchor on every swap**) as the *proposed* placement resolver — "the spot the body last stood" — and the amendment is SYS-07/SYS-15's to ratify (**OQ-RWS-4 is a release-gate condition: the pit and the module revive must not co-ship in any build until the placement is ratified** — on the QA plan's S1 list). SYS-02 ships the publication; no SYS-07 doc is edited here. [TR-RWS-016; OQ-RWS-4.]
- **If the pit event fires while the death latch holds** (a second overlap entry before the death resolves) → SYS-07's DEAD state rejects the event; the cause is never rewritten. [TR-RWS-016; SYS-07's same-tick law.]

### Spawns and populations

- **If a room's spawn list holds 5 entries** → the first 4 admit in list order; the 5th is refused with exactly one telemetry record, no state mutation (the shipped rooms never exceed 4 — the fixture exercises the surface). [TR-RWS-017; F-RWS-06.]
- **If the player leaves the arena mid-boss and re-enters** → the room deactivates; its local state (including any boss-side state SYS-14 keeps there) dies with it; re-entry re-applies the spawn list. SYS-14 may add its own arena gate at VS — recorded (OQ-RWS-8). [TR-RWS-011.]
- **If an unknown archetype id appears in a spawn list** → the dev boot fails naming the entry (SYS-13's registry is the membership authority). [TR-RWS-017; V-RWS-09.]

### Interactables and prompts

- **If two prompts would light on the same frame** (a memory beside a door) → the Prompt Slot's nearest-wins resolves before any press binds; the tie-break is the registry id, never a float comparison. [TR-RWS-018; SYS-01's law consumed.]
- **If the Suture pickup is taken at a full quick slot** → the ack routes to SYS-17's grant, which **refuses** (TR-HD-031's rule); the item **stays on the ground**, the prompt persists on the next tick — never wasted, never silently consumed. [TR-RWS-020; AC-RWS-33.]
- **If the log's Reading context opens while the cache terminal's would** (RM-04 holds both) → one context at a time (SYS-05's machine): the terminal's request queues (the non-ack row) and applies after the Reading closes and the context returns to GAMEPLAY. No nested contexts. [TR-RWS-019/021.]

### Geometry and grid

- **If a doorway is authored narrower than 2 tiles or shorter than 3** → the dev boot fails (V-RWS-03): the 44 px body must clear every opening without a no-headroom refusal inside its own arc. [TR-RWS-003.]
- **If an authored step exceeds 16 px** → the dev boot **fails** (V-RWS-02's single resolution); an author intent-annotation downgrades it to a recorded warn + telemetry, never silence. [TR-RWS-023.]
- **If a one-way floor's drop is armed and the floor's state changes mid-drop** (a future truth-keyed floor) → the drop window's disable stands (`DROP_THROUGH_MS` 200); the floor's state at the release tick governs; no overlap ejection either way. [TR-RWS-023; Movement's release law consumed.]
- **If an anchor is authored inside a hazard rect or within 1 tile of a void border** → the dev boot fails (the anchor-safety clause of V-RWS-03): no spawn ever re-kills. [TR-RWS-003.]

### Degenerate strategies — named and shut down

- **If the player camps in a doorway** → the doorway is honest geometry: enemies reach it (they refuse ledges, not doorways), the body's arcs pass through it, and the swap's velocity-zeroing prevents doorway-physics abuse. No special rule. [TR-RWS-010.]
- **If the player tries to re-lock an opened door to farm** → the open is world truth, set once at the ack; the walk-through is free thereafter; there is nothing to farm. [TR-RWS-008.]
- **If the player expects to reach the Cold Corridor pre-flip** → the zone gate is SEALED at epoch 0 (the physics collision holds; the wing renders the hall's wall material — sealed, never absent); there is no pre-flip route. The reveal is epoch-gated by construction, not by trust. [TR-RWS-009; F-RWS-04.]
- **If the player expects the pit to be survivable** → `raw_damage = hp_max`, the bypass is SYS-07's law; the pit is the void, not a hit. [TR-RWS-016.]
